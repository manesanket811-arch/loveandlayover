Is Bugis Street really Singapore’s cheapest market, or just hype? We explored the shopping, real prices, street food and hidden photo spots so you know exactly what to expect before you visit in 2026.

📊 WHAT WE COVER

📜 ABOUT BUGIS STREET

Bugis Street is one of Singapore’s most iconic spots, with 600+ shops selling budget fashion, souvenirs and street food.

• History: Once a red-light district and part of “Little Japan” in the early days, it later became famous from the 1950s to the 80s for its wild nightlife, street food and drag queen parades.

• Transformation: The original street was demolished in 1985 for redevelopment, and the modern Bugis Street was recreated nearby.

• Shopping: Clothes, accessories and souvenirs from around S$3–10, perfect for bargain hunters.

• Art Lane: Colourful murals and spiral staircases, a great photo spot.

• Food: Satay, kaya toast and fresh fruit juices.

📍 LOCATION & HOURS

• Address: Bugis Street Singapore

• Nearest MRT: Bugis (East West & Downtown lines)

• Shopping hours: around 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily (varies by stall)

💡 PRO TIPS

• Carry some cash, as not every stall takes cards.

• Go on a weekday to avoid the crowds.

• Check the upper level for more unique finds.
