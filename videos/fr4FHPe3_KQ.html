Genting SkyWay Cable Car: Worth the Hype?

Travel · September 29, 2026

▶ Subscribe on YouTube Watch on YouTube

Experience Malaysia’s famous Awana SkyWay Cable Car journey with us as we ride up into the misty mountains of Genting Highlands and explore the Chin Swee Caves Temple!

In Day 3 of our Malaysia trip, we take you through the complete cable car experience: from boarding at Awana Station, riding above the rainforest, exploring the 9-storey Pagoda at Chin Swee station, all the way to SkyAvenue at the peak.
