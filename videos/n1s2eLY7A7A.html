Can Nikita stay awake ALL NIGHT? 😴 We took on a 12-hour overnight challenge in Singapore — 7 PM to 7 AM, no going home, no hotel, no sleep!

निकिता अख्खी रात्र जागी राहू शकेल का? 🌃☀️

Light shows at Marina Bay, night cycling past the Supertrees, the last MRT out of the city, Changi Airport and Jewel in the dead of night, and sunrise at Changi Beach — this is what Singapore really looks like when everyone else is asleep. And we did it all for just S$45. 💰

Did Nikita make it to sunrise without a single nap, or did she give up? Watch till the end to find out 👀

📍 Places in this vlog:

• Marina Bay Promenade – Spectra Light & Water Show

• Gardens by the Bay – Supertree Grove & Garden Rhapsody

• Marina Bay night cycling route – MBS loop & Merlion Park view

• Raffles Place MRT – catching the last train

• Singapore Changi Airport – Terminals 1, 2 & 3 (incl. Diwali decorations at T2 🪔)

• Jewel Changi Airport at 5 AM

• Changi Beach Park – sunrise
