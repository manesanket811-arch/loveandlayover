Why Visit Peru?

Peru offers ancient Inca wonders, Amazon rainforest adventures, coastal charm, and world-class cuisine. From Machu Picchu's mystical mountain ruins to Cusco's colonial architecture, from Sacred Valley alpaca trekking to Lima's gastronomic revolution, Peru captivates with history, nature, and flavors. Perfect for adventure seekers, history enthusiasts, foodies, and nature lovers.

Trek to Machu Picchu, explore Inca sites, hike mountain passes, visit local communities, taste authentic Peruvian food, and experience Andean culture. Peru is unforgettable.

2 Cusco: Inca History & Sacred Valley

Morning
Flight Lima to Cusco (PEN 200-400, 1 hour). Arrive at high altitude (3400m) - take it slow. Visit Plaza de Armas historic square (free). Explore nearby Inca walls and temples. Grab coca tea to combat altitude (PEN 5-10).
Afternoon
Sacred Valley day trip - visit local villages (tour PEN 80-120). Ollantaytambo Inca fortress (PEN 70). Pisac ruins and market (PEN 70). Meet alpaca herds. Lunch in mountain village (PEN 25-40).
Evening
Return to Cusco. Peruvian dinner - guinea pig (cuy) specialty or chicken (PEN 40-90). Pisco sours at local bars (PEN 20-35 per drink). Rest for Machu Picchu trek ahead.
PEN 200-320/day

3 Machu Picchu: Inca Wonder

Early Morning
Train Cusco to Aguas Calientes (PEN 100-180, 3.5 hours). Arrive at Machu Picchu gate. Guided tour (PEN 150-250 including entry) explores temples, terraces, Intihuatana stone. Sunrise hike option for best light and views.
Afternoon
Continue exploring Machu Picchu - hiking around ruins (included). Photos at iconic angles. Understand Inca engineering and history. Rest at viewpoints. Snacks at site cafe (PEN 20-40).
Evening
Return train to Cusco. Dinner to celebrate achievement (PEN 50-100). Evening rest and reflection on one of world's greatest wonders.
PEN 250-450/day

Best Time to Visit Peru

Best overall: May-October (dry season, clear skies, perfect for Machu Picchu trekking)

Peak season: June-August (busiest, most expensive, clearest weather)

Shoulder season: April-May, September-October (fewer crowds, good weather)

Budget tip: November-March (rainy season, lower prices, more rain but fewer tourists)

Getting Around Peru

Trains

PeruRail operates luxury and local trains. Cusco-Machu Picchu: PEN 100-300+ depending on class and speed.

Buses

Extensive intercity buses. Lima-Cusco: PEN 40-100 (10-12 hours). First-class buses have reclining seats.

Domestic Flights

Quick connections between major cities. Lima-Cusco: PEN 200-400. Book in advance for discounts.

Local Transport

Buses, collectivos, taxis in cities. Bus rides: PEN 1-3. Taxi negotiable or use Uber (available in Lima).

Visa & Entry

Important Notes

  • Altitude sickness common in Cusco/Sacred Valley - take time to acclimatize, drink coca tea.
  • Tap water not potable - drink bottled or filtered water.
  • Machu Picchu book tickets in advance (daily limit 2,500 visitors).
  • Peru's rainy season November-March affects mountain trekking.
  • Spanish helpful - English limited outside tourist areas.
