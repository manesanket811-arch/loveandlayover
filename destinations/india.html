Why Visit India?

India is a vast tapestry of cultures, religions, and landscapes. Visit the iconic Taj Mahal, explore Rajasthan's magnificent forts and palaces, trek in the Himalayas, cruise through Kerala's backwaters, experience spiritual sites, and taste incredible regional cuisines. India offers profound experiences and incredible value for 3-10 day trips and beyond.

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: INR 500-1,500/night
  • Food: INR 300-800/day (street to decent)
  • Transport: INR 200-500/day (trains, buses)
  • Activities: INR 500-1,500/day

Best Time to Visit

October-March (cool, dry, 15-30°C) is ideal. Avoid May-September (monsoon, very hot). December-January is peak season.

Visa Requirements

No visa required for Indian citizens! Enter with passport. If international travel, check visa requirements for your country.

Highlights

Taj Mahal: Monument to love, Agra. Rajasthan: Jaipur's forts, Udaipur's lakes, Jodhpur's blue city. Kerala: Backwater cruises, spice plantations. Himalayas: Trekking, meditation, spiritual sites.
