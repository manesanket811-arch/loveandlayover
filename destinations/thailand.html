Why Visit Thailand?

Thailand is Southeast Asia's gem offering incredible diversity. Experience vibrant Bangkok with ornate temples, relax on pristine island beaches (Phuket, Krabi, Koh Samui), explore northern mountains and tribal culture in Chiang Mai, enjoy world-class food at every street corner, and discover warm, welcoming locals. Perfect destination for 3-10 day trips.

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: THB 300-800/night
  • Food: THB 100-400/day
  • Transport: THB 50-200/day
  • Activities: THB 200-800/day

Best Time to Visit

November-February (cool, dry, 20-30°C) is ideal. Avoid May-September (monsoon, humid). December-January is peak season (crowded, expensive). September-October offers best value with fewer tourists.

Visa Requirements

Indian nationals get 60-90 days visa-free entry. Tourist visa (60 days) available from Thai embassy. Visa on arrival sometimes available but slower than visa-free entry.

Highlights

Bangkok: Grand Palace, Wat Phra Kaew, floating markets, street food. Chiang Mai: Temples, mountains, elephant sanctuaries. Islands: Phuket, Krabi, Koh Samui, Koh Phi Phi - beach paradise.
