Why Visit Thailand?

Thailand is the perfect blend of adventure, culture, and affordability. From Bangkok's chaotic energy and ancient temples to tropical islands with pristine beaches, limestone cliffs, jungle adventures, and legendary street food, Thailand delivers incredible experiences on a shoestring budget. It's one of the most budget-friendly and welcoming destinations in Southeast Asia.

Whether you're exploring ornate Buddhist temples, island-hopping in the Andaman Sea, learning Thai cooking, or dancing until dawn in beach clubs, Thailand has something for every type of traveler.

1 Bangkok Temples & Markets

Morning
Arrive at Bangkok's Suvarnabhumi Airport. Take the Airport Rail Link to central Bangkok (฿45, 30 mins). Check into your hotel in Khao San Road or Silom area. Freshen up and grab breakfast at a street stall (฿30–50).
Afternoon
Visit Grand Palace (Wat Phra Kaew) — Thailand's most sacred temple (฿500 entry). Dress respectfully (long pants, covered shoulders). The architecture is breathtaking. Take photos and explore for 2–3 hours.
Evening
Explore Khao San Road (backpacker hub with street food, bars, shops). Dinner at a street vendor or restaurant (฿60–150). Experience Bangkok's nightlife. Optional: Visit a traditional Thai massage place (฿200–300 for 1 hour).
฿1,000–1,500/day

2 Floating Markets & Local Life

Early Morning
Day trip to Amphawa Floating Market (30 mins from Bangkok by taxi/tour ฿1,000–1,500). Arrive before 8 AM to see vendors selling produce from boats. Explore the market, sample local snacks (mango sticky rice, ฿30–50).
Afternoon
Visit a nearby temple or local village. Return to Bangkok. Stop at Lumphini Park (free) for evening walk and Thai massage stalls.
Evening
Dinner at a local restaurant in Silom area. Try pad thai, green curry, or tom yum soup (฿80–200). Explore Bangkok's night markets or visit a rooftop bar for sunset drinks.
฿1,200–1,800/day

3 Island Escape

Morning
Quick flight or overnight train to Phuket/Krabi (from Bangkok). Alternatively, 4-hour minibus. Settle into beachside accommodation. Relax at Kata Beach or Patong Beach.
Afternoon
Island hop tour to Phi Phi Islands or Phang Nga Bay (฿1,000–1,500). See limestone cliffs, crystal-clear water, and limestone caves. Snorkel and swim.
Evening
Sunset dinner on the beach. Try fresh seafood (grilled fish, prawns ฿200–400). Relax and enjoy beach vibes. Optional: Go diving or try parasailing.
฿1,500–2,500/day (including tours)

Thai Food You Must Try

  • Pad Thai — Stir-fried rice noodles with shrimp and peanuts. ฿50–100. Street food staple.
  • Green Curry (Gaeng Keow Wan) — Spicy green curry with coconut milk. ฿80–150. Authentic and delicious.
  • Tom Yum Soup — Hot and sour soup with shrimp or chicken. ฿60–120. Refreshing and flavorful.
  • Mango Sticky Rice — Sweet dessert with mango and sticky rice. ฿40–80. Perfect for hot days.
  • Satay — Grilled meat skewers with peanut sauce. ฿40–80 per stick. Tasty and affordable.
  • Som Tam — Green papaya salad (spicy). ฿40–70. Tangy and refreshing.
  • Khao Pad — Fried rice with egg and meat. ฿50–100. Quick, filling meal.
  • Larb — Spicy meat salad. ฿60–120. Flavorful and protein-rich.

Best Time to Visit

Cool Season (November–February): Perfect weather, cool and dry. Best time to visit. Peak tourist season but worth it.

Hot Season (March–May): Extremely hot and humid (40°C+). Fewer tourists, lower prices. Physically exhausting.

Rainy Season (June–October): Monsoon rains, afternoon showers. Lush green scenery. Cheapest accommodation. Fewer tourists but unpredictable weather.

Getting Around Thailand

Bangkok Metro: BTS Skytrain and MRT subway cover most of Bangkok. Fast and efficient. Fares ฿16–59 depending on distance. Get a Rabbit card (rechargeable) for convenience.

Taxis & Tuk-tuks: Taxis cost ฿35 base fare + ฿5.50 per km. Tuk-tuks are cheaper for short distances (฿40–150). Always use the meter or negotiate upfront.

Buses: Extensive network, very cheap (฿5–30). Less comfortable but good for locals and budget travelers.

Inter-city Transport: Buses and trains connect Bangkok to other cities. Overnight sleeper trains popular and affordable (฿500–1,500).

Domestic Flights: Budget airlines (Thai AirAsia, Nok Air) offer cheap flights between major cities (from ฿300).

Travel Essentials

Visa for Indians: Visa-free stay for 30 days. Alternatively, apply for 60-day tourist visa at Thai embassy (₹850 approx). Valid for 60 days.

Money: Thai Baht (฿). 1 USD = ~33–35 THB (varies). Very affordable. ATMs everywhere accept international cards. Most places cash-only, but cards work in malls and hotels.

SIM Card: Buy at airport (AIS, DTAC, True Move). Unlimited data plans ฿300–500 for 7 days. Excellent 4G coverage.

Apps: Google Maps, Grab (Uber equivalent), ThaiVisa, Agoda (hotels), Line (messaging).

Language: English limited outside tourist areas. Learn basic Thai phrases. Download Google Translate for help.

Accommodation: Budget hostels/guesthouses ฿300–600/night. Mid-range hotels ฿800–1,500. Airbnb available.

Cultural Tips: Respect the Thai royal family. Cover shoulders and knees in temples. Remove shoes before entering homes and temples. Never step on Thai currency.
