Why Visit Greece?

Greece is where ancient history meets Mediterranean beauty. Explore iconic ruins in Athens, island-hop through the Aegean Sea, and experience world-class food and hospitality. From Santorini's sunset views to Rhodes' sandy beaches, Greece offers perfect combinations for 3-10 day adventures. Crystal waters, whitewashed villages, and legendary Greek hospitality make every visit unforgettable.

Greece Food Guide

Greek cuisine is Mediterranean magic—simple, fresh, and unforgettable. Olive oil, feta cheese, fresh seafood, and herbs define the food culture. Every meal is a celebration of local ingredients and generations of tradition.

Must-Try Greek Dishes

  • Moussaka - Layered eggplant, meat sauce, béchamel. Cost: €8-15
  • Souvlaki - Grilled meat skewers (chicken, pork, lamb). Cost: €5-10
  • Gyros - Rotating meat in pita, tzatziki sauce. Cost: €3-6
  • Spanakopita - Spinach and feta pastry. Cost: €4-8
  • Saganaki - Fried cheese, often flambéed. Cost: €6-10
  • Pastitsio - Greek lasagna with meat and béchamel. Cost: €8-13

Seafood Specialties

  • Grilled Octopus (Chtapodi) - Tender grilled octopus with lemon. Cost: €12-18
  • Shrimp Saganaki - Fried shrimp in tomato sauce. Cost: €10-15
  • Whole Grilled Fish - Sea bass, bream, or grouper. Cost: €15-25
  • Mussels & Clams (Midya) - Steamed or sauted. Cost: €8-14

Street Food & Snacks

  • Greek Salad (Horiatiki) - Tomato, cucumber, feta, olives. Cost: €4-8
  • Falafel/Souvlaki Wrap - Street vendor favorite. Cost: €3-5
  • Baklava - Phyllo, nuts, honey pastry. Cost: €2-4
  • Loukoumades - Honey donuts, fried goodness. Cost: €3-5

Best Places to Eat

  • Varkiza Beach Taverns (Athens) - Fresh seafood by the sea
  • Plaka District (Athens) - Traditional tavernas with live music
  • Mykonos Town - High-end restaurants and seafood
  • Santorini Oia - Sunset dining with views
  • Local Tavernas - Family-run, authentic, cheapest option €5-10

Dining Tips

Greeks eat dinner late (9-10pm). Tipping 5-10% is appreciated but not required. Tap water is safe. Ouzo (anise spirit) is the national drink. Ask for "kalispera" (good evening) and locals will warm up. Lunch is the main meal (2-3pm); dinner is lighter.

Best Time to Visit Greece

Spring (April - May)

Weather: 18-25°C, sunny and mild. Crowds: Moderate, increasing towards May.

Highlights: Wildflowers bloom, Easter celebrations (major religious festival), perfect walking weather, fewer tourists than summer.

Costs: Mid-range. Hotels €50-100. Flights moderate pricing.

Best for: Nature lovers, those avoiding peak summer crowds, Easter travelers.

Summer (June - August)

Weather: 28-35°C, hot and dry. Crowds: PEAK tourist season.

Highlights: Perfect beach weather, swimming conditions ideal, island ferries frequent, summer festivals, nightlife thriving.

Costs: HIGHEST of the year. Hotels €100-200. Book early or accept premium prices.

Best for: Beach lovers, island hoppers, those seeking guaranteed good weather and social scene.

Autumn (September - October)

Weather: 22-28°C, sunny and warm. Crowds: Declining, still pleasant.

Highlights: Sea temperature still warm (24-25°C), fewer crowds than summer, good for island hopping, fewer rain showers than spring.

Costs: Moderate. Hotels €60-120. Good value compared to summer.

Best for: Budget-conscious travelers, those seeking good weather with fewer tourists.

Winter (November - March)

Weather: 10-18°C, occasional rain. Crowds: Very low.

Highlights: Many island ferries reduced, museums less crowded, authentic Greek village experience, lowest prices.

Costs: LOWEST of year. Hotels €30-60. 50% cheaper than summer.

Best for: Budget travelers, those avoiding crowds, cultural exploration over beach time.

Recommendation

Best overall: May-June or September-October (perfect weather, manageable crowds, moderate prices).

Budget travelers: November-March (lowest prices, authentic experience).

Beach lovers: July-August (warmest sea, guaranteed swimming).

Getting Around Greece

Ferries - Island Hopping Essential

Main Ferry Routes: Athens (Piraeus Port) to Mykonos (3-5 hours, €25-45), Santorini (7-9 hours, €30-50), Hydra (1 hour, €8-12). Fast ferries (catamarans) are 2x cost but 50% faster. Book online or at ports. Summer ferries frequent; winter reduced significantly.

Domestic Flights

Aegean Airways and Ryanair connect major islands and Athens. Cost: €30-80 one-way. Useful for longer island distances (Athens to Rhodes: 1h flight vs 12h ferry). Book in advance for best prices.

Public Transport in Athens

  • Metro: 3 lines cover most of city. Cost: €1.40 per ride. Day pass: €4.50
  • Buses & Trams: Extensive network. Cost: €1.40 per ride. Tourist card (3 days): €22
  • Taxis: Reasonable. Flag drop: €1.19 + €0.68/km. Uber available.

Island Transport

Most islands have local buses connecting villages and beaches. Cost: €1-3. Scooter/motorbike rental on larger islands (Mykonos, Santorini, Crete) €20-40/day. Taxis expensive on islands (no meters, negotiate beforehand).

Trains

TrainOSE operates limited routes (mostly useful for Delphi from Athens). Cost: €10-20. Buses faster and cheaper for most routes.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Ferry tickets cheaper if booked 1-2 days ahead
  • Eat lunch as main meal (cheaper set menus €6-10)
  • Stay in smaller islands or Airbnb outside tourist centers
  • Island-hop 2-3 islands max to avoid ferry fatigue and costs

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: €40-120/night (hostels €20-30, mid-range €60-100, hotels €120+)
  • Food: €20-40/day (street food €3-5, local taverna €8-15, fine dining €25+)
  • Ferries: €20-50 per journey, daily island hopping €50-100
  • Activities: €10-30/day (museums €5-15, temples €3-10, boat tours €30-50)
  • Total daily budget: €40-75/day for budget travelers

Visa & Entry Requirements

EU/Schengen Citizens: Passport or ID card sufficient. No visa needed for EU nationals.

Indian Passport Holders: 90-day Schengen visa required. Apply at Greek embassy (online processing available). Processing: 5-15 days. Cost: €80. Proof of funds: €2,000+, return ticket, accommodation proof, travel insurance.

US/UK/Australia: Visa-free for 90 days within Schengen area.

Currency: Euro (€). 1 USD ≈ €0.92. ATMs widely available. Debit cards accepted most places.

Getting a SIM: Buy from Cosmote, Vodafone, or Wind at airport (€20-40 for 10-day plan with data).

Travel Insurance

Recommended for Schengen area. Cost: €1-2/day. Covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost luggage.
