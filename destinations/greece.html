Why Visit Greece?

Greece is a land of ancient history, stunning natural beauty, and island paradise. From the Acropolis overlooking Athens to Santorini's legendary blue-domed sunset views, from ancient ruins of Delphi to island-hopping adventures, Greece captivates with myths, history, and Mediterranean charm. Perfect for history buffs, beach lovers, culture seekers, and romantics.

Explore ancient temples, swim in crystal-clear turquoise seas, enjoy legendary Greek hospitality, taste Mediterranean cuisine, and experience timeless island life. Greece is unforgettable.

1 Athens: Acropolis & Ancient Ruins

Morning
Arrive at Athens airport. Metro to hotel (EUR 10). Visit Acropolis (EUR 20). See Parthenon, Erechtheion - ancient temples. Grab Greek coffee and loukoumades (fried pastry, EUR 4-6).
Afternoon
Explore Acropolis Museum (EUR 9). Walk historic Plaka neighborhood - charming streets, tavernas (free). Visit Temple of Olympian Zeus (EUR 6). Browse local shops and galleries.
Evening
Traditional Greek dinner at taverna (EUR 15-25) - moussaka, souvlaki, Greek salad. Greek wine (EUR 4-8 per glass). Evening walk through Syntagma Square. Sunset from Areopagus Hill (free).
EUR 65-110/day

2 Delphi Day Trip: Ancient Oracle

Early Morning
Coach/rental car to Delphi (EUR 20-40 round trip, 2 hours). Visit Delphi Archaeological Site (EUR 12) - ancient sanctuary of Apollo. Dramatic clifftop setting. Museum included (EUR 6 separate). Breakfast en route (EUR 6-10).
Afternoon
Walk Theater of Delphi ruins (included). Visit Delphi Museum to see treasures. Explore nearby town of Delphi (free wandering). Lunch at local taverna (EUR 12-18).
Evening
Return to Athens. Dinner (EUR 15-25). Rest from travel day or explore Psyrri district for nightlife.
EUR 75-135/day

3 Santorini: Sunset & Island Life

Morning
Ferry Athens to Santorini (EUR 40-60, 7 hours or fly EUR 50-80, 1 hour). Arrive, check into Oia or Fira. Relax at volcanic black sand beach (free). Grab Greek yogurt with honey (EUR 4-6).
Afternoon
Explore Fira town - whitewashed buildings, blue domes (free wandering). Cable car down to beach (EUR 4) or walk volcanic steps (free). Swimming in Aegean Sea (free). Wine tasting at winery (EUR 15-25).
Evening
Watch famous Santorini sunset from Oia (free, arrive early). Dinner overlooking caldera (EUR 20-40). Feta saganaki, fresh fish, Santorini wine. Late-night walk through village squares.
EUR 100-180/day

Best Time to Visit Greece

Best overall: May-June and September-October (sunny, warm 22-28°C, fewer crowds than summer)

Summer swimming: July-August (hot 28-35°C, crowded, expensive, perfect for islands)

Avoid: July-August peak (extremely crowded, very hot, highest prices)

Budget tip: November-March (mild 10-16°C, rainy, lowest prices and fewer tourists)

Getting Around Greece

Metro & Buses

Athens metro well-developed. Single ticket: EUR 1.40. Day pass: EUR 4.50. Island buses are affordable (EUR 1-3 per ride).

Ferries

Extensive ferry network. Athens to Santorini: EUR 40-80. Many ferry companies - book ahead in peak season to save 20-30%.

Taxis & Ride-share

Taxis metered in cities. Grab available in Athens. Within cities: EUR 5-12 typical fare.

Organized Tours

Island hopping tours: EUR 50-80. Walking tours: EUR 15-25. Sunset boat cruises: EUR 30-50.

Travel Essentials

Important Notes

  • Greek siesta culture - many shops close 2-5pm.
  • Dinner typically 8-11pm. Don't expect restaurants full before 9pm.
  • Islands can be windy - pack light layers.
  • Ferry schedules change seasonally - check in advance.
  • Greek hospitality warm and welcoming - locals appreciate effort to speak Greek.
