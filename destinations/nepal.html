Why Visit Nepal?

Nepal is home to eight of the world's ten highest mountains, ancient Hindu and Buddhist temples, medieval Newari towns and jungle safaris, all close to India. Indians don't need a visa or even a passport, Indian rupee notes are widely accepted and the food feels familiar, which makes it one of the easiest international trips you can take.

The classic first trip combines Kathmandu Valley (Pashupatinath, Boudhanath, Bhaktapur), the lakeside town of Pokhara with Himalayan sunrise views, and wildlife in Chitwan National Park.

Nepal Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Kathmandu Durbar Square & Thamel

Morning
Land at Tribhuvan International Airport and check in around Thamel, the lively traveller area.
Afternoon
Explore Kathmandu Durbar Square, with its palaces, temples and the Kumari Ghar, home of the living goddess (entry fee for foreigners; SAARC nationals pay less).
Evening
Walk through Asan and Indra Chowk markets, then a dal bhat or momo dinner in Thamel.
NPR 3,500–6,000/day
2

Pashupatinath, Boudhanath & Swayambhunath

Morning
Visit Pashupatinath Temple on the Bagmati river, one of the holiest Shiva temples. Only Hindus may enter the main temple courtyard.
Afternoon
Walk around the giant stupa of Boudhanath, one of the largest in the world, and have lunch at a rooftop café overlooking it.
Evening
Climb to Swayambhunath (the Monkey Temple) for sunset views over the valley. Watch your bags around the monkeys.
NPR 3,500–6,000/day
3

Bhaktapur & Nagarkot

Morning
Drive to Bhaktapur (about 45 minutes), the best-preserved medieval town in the valley: Durbar Square, Nyatapola Temple and potters' square.
Afternoon
Try juju dhau, Bhaktapur's famous "king of curds", then continue to Nagarkot (about 1 hour).
Evening
Watch the sunset over the Himalaya from Nagarkot and stay overnight for sunrise, or return to Kathmandu.
NPR 4,000–7,000/day
4

To Pokhara & Phewa Lake

5-day plan
Morning
Take a short flight to Pokhara (about 25 minutes, mountain views on the right side) or a tourist bus (about 6–8 hours).
Afternoon
Rent a boat on Phewa Lake and visit the Tal Barahi temple on its island.
Evening
Stroll Lakeside with the Annapurna range glowing at sunset, and have dinner by the water.
NPR 5,000–9,000/day (incl. travel)
5

Sarangkot sunrise & Pokhara

5-day plan
Early Morning
Drive up to Sarangkot before dawn to watch the sun rise over Machhapuchhre and the Annapurnas.
Afternoon
Visit the World Peace Pagoda, Davis Falls and Gupteshwor Cave. Adventure lovers can paraglide from Sarangkot.
Evening
Relax in Lakeside. Short treks such as Australian Camp or Poon Hill can extend your stay if you have more days.
NPR 4,000–8,000/day
6

Chitwan National Park

7-day plan
Morning
Drive or take a tourist bus to Chitwan (about 5 hours) and check into a lodge in Sauraha.
Afternoon
Take a canoe ride on the Rapti river looking for crocodiles and birds, and walk to the riverbank for sunset.
Evening
Watch a Tharu cultural dance performance.
NPR 5,000–9,000/day
7

Jungle safari & departure

7-day plan
Morning
Go on a jeep safari in Chitwan National Park to look for one-horned rhinos, deer and, if you're lucky, a tiger.
Afternoon
Fly from Bharatpur to Kathmandu (about 25 minutes), or drive to the Indian border crossings if you're heading home by road.
Evening
Last-minute shopping for pashmina, singing bowls and tea before your flight home.
NPR 5,000–10,000/day

Nepali Food to Try

  • Dal bhat — Lentil soup, rice, vegetable curry and pickles, refilled until you're full. "Dal bhat power, 24 hour!"
  • Momos — Steamed or fried dumplings with spicy tomato achar; vegetarian versions are everywhere.
  • Thukpa — Tibetan-style noodle soup, perfect in the mountains.
  • Newari khaja set — Beaten rice with spiced potatoes, beans, pickles and more, a Kathmandu Valley speciality.
  • Sel roti — Ring-shaped sweet rice bread, popular during festivals.
  • Juju dhau — Rich, creamy curd from Bhaktapur.

For Indian travellers: vegetarian food is easy to find and very familiar, and many restaurants in Thamel and Pokhara serve North and South Indian dishes.

Best Time to Visit Nepal

Autumn (October–November): the best time, with clear skies, mountain views and festivals such as Dashain and Tihar.

Spring (March–April): warm days and blooming rhododendrons; a little hazier.

Monsoon (June–September): green and quiet but rainy, with landslides on mountain roads and clouds hiding the peaks.

Winter (December–February): cold nights in Kathmandu and Pokhara but often clear views; high trails can be snowed in.

Getting Around Nepal

Domestic flights: the fastest way between Kathmandu, Pokhara and Bharatpur (Chitwan). Mountain weather causes delays, so leave a buffer day before your flight home.

Tourist buses: cheap and reliable between Kathmandu, Pokhara and Chitwan, though slow on winding roads.

Taxis and apps: agree fares before getting in, or use the Pathao or inDrive apps in Kathmandu and Pokhara.

By road from India: main crossings include Sunauli–Bhairahawa, Raxaul–Birgunj and Panitanki–Kakarbhitta.

Travel Essentials

Entry for Indians: Indian citizens don't need a visa for Nepal. Carry a valid passport or voter ID card (Aadhaar is generally not accepted as the main travel document for air travel). Children and minors have separate document rules, so check with your airline. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you travel.

Money: Nepali rupee (NPR). ₹1 is about NPR 1.6, and Indian ₹100 notes are widely accepted; check current rules before carrying larger Indian notes. ATMs work in cities; carry cash for smaller towns.

Daily budget (per person): a mid-range hotel share, food, local transport and sightseeing come to about NPR 4,000–7,000 a day, plus domestic flights and safaris.

SIM card: Ncell and Nepal Telecom (NTC) sell SIMs at the airport and in town with your passport or ID and a photo.

Plugs: types C, D and M, 230V, so most Indian plugs fit. Power cuts are less common than before but carry a power bank.

Etiquette and safety: walk clockwise around stupas, remove shoes in temples and ask before photographing people. For treks above about 3,000 m, go slowly to avoid altitude sickness and buy insurance that covers trekking.

Nepal FAQs

How much does a Nepal trip cost per day?

Plan for roughly NPR 4,000–7,000 per person per day (about ₹2,500–4,400) for a mid-range trip, covering accommodation, food, local transport and sightseeing. Prices are approximate as of October 2026.

How many days do you need in Nepal?

3 days covers the highlights: Kathmandu Durbar Square & Thamel, Pashupatinath, Boudhanath & Swayambhunath and Bhaktapur & Nagarkot. With 5 days you can add To Pokhara & Phewa Lake and Sarangkot sunrise & Pokhara, and a full week adds Chitwan National Park and Jungle safari. Our 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries build on each other, so you can stop at any point.

When is the best time to visit Nepal?

Oct–Nov, Mar–Apr. Autumn (October–November): the best time, with clear skies, mountain views and festivals such as Dashain and Tihar.
