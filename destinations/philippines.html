Why Visit Philippines?

The Philippines is more than 7,000 islands of white-sand beaches, limestone lagoons, coral reefs and some of the friendliest people in Asia. English is widely spoken, island-hopping is the main event and costs are reasonable once you're there.

A first trip usually combines Manila with the countryside and beaches of Bohol and the dramatic lagoons of El Nido, Palawan. Domestic flights make it easy to hop between islands.

Philippines Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Manila: Intramuros & Binondo

Morning
Arrive at Manila's Ninoy Aquino International Airport (NAIA). Traffic is heavy, so allow plenty of time and use Grab or an airport taxi.
Afternoon
Explore Intramuros, the old walled city: Fort Santiago and San Agustin Church (a UNESCO site). Rent a bamboo bike or ride a kalesa.
Evening
Food crawl through Binondo, the world's oldest Chinatown, or watch the sunset over Manila Bay.
₱3,000–5,000/day
2

Bohol: Chocolate Hills & tarsiers

Morning
Fly to Bohol (Panglao airport, about 1.5 hours).
Afternoon
Join a countryside tour: the Chocolate Hills, the tiny tarsiers at a conservation sanctuary (keep quiet and don't use flash), and a lunch cruise on the Loboc River.
Evening
Relax at Alona Beach in Panglao.
₱3,500–5,500/day
3

Panglao island hopping

Morning
Early island-hopping trip for dolphin watching and snorkelling at Balicasag Island, known for turtles.
Afternoon
Stop at Virgin Island's sandbar, then relax at Dumaluan Beach.
Evening
Seafood dinner on Alona Beach. If your trip ends here, fly back to Manila tomorrow.
₱3,500–6,000/day
4

To El Nido, Palawan

5-day plan
Morning
Fly to El Nido, either direct to the small Lio airport or via Puerto Princesa followed by a 5–6 hour van ride.
Afternoon
Check in and walk around El Nido town and Las Cabanas beach.
Evening
Watch the sunset at Las Cabanas, then dinner in town. Pay the environmental fee and book tomorrow's island tour.
₱3,500–6,500/day (excl. flights)
5

El Nido Tour A: lagoons

5-day plan
Morning
Join Tour A: kayak or swim into the Big and Small Lagoons, surrounded by limestone cliffs. Lagoon access is limited daily, so book a day ahead.
Afternoon
Visit Secret Lagoon and Shimizu Island for snorkelling and lunch on the boat.
Evening
Rest; island hopping takes a lot of energy in the sun.
₱3,500–6,000/day
6

El Nido Tour C or Nacpan Beach

7-day plan
Morning
Take Tour C to Hidden Beach and Matinloc Shrine, or rent a scooter or tricycle to Nacpan Beach, a long, quiet stretch of sand.
Afternoon
Swim, snorkel and enjoy a beachside lunch.
Evening
Final dinner in El Nido.
₱3,000–5,500/day
7

Back to Manila & departure

7-day plan
Morning
Fly from El Nido to Manila.
Afternoon
If time allows, visit Rizal Park and the National Museum of Fine Arts (free), or the malls of Makati and BGC.
Evening
Head to NAIA, allowing extra time for Manila traffic.
₱2,500–4,500/day

Filipino Food to Try

  • Adobo — Chicken or pork braised in vinegar, soy sauce and garlic; the unofficial national dish.
  • Sinigang — Sour tamarind soup with vegetables and pork or prawns.
  • Lechon — Whole roast pig with crackling skin, especially famous in Cebu.
  • Kare-kare — Oxtail and vegetables in peanut sauce with shrimp paste.
  • Pancit — Stir-fried noodles, often served at celebrations.
  • Halo-halo — Shaved ice with sweet beans, fruit, ube and leche flan: the ultimate dessert.

For Indian travellers: Filipino food is meat-heavy and vegetarian options are limited outside big cities. Manila (Makati, BGC) has several Indian restaurants; on the islands, tell restaurants clearly what you don't eat.

Best Time to Visit Philippines

Dry season (December–May): the best time for beaches, with January to April the driest and calmest. March to May is hot.

Wet season (June–November): rain and the risk of typhoons, which can cancel flights and boat trips. Travel is still possible but keep plans flexible.

Temperature: warm all year (about 26–33°C).

Getting Around Philippines

Domestic flights: Philippine Airlines, Cebu Pacific and AirAsia connect Manila, Cebu, Bohol and Palawan. Book early and check baggage allowances.

Ferries and boats: link many islands; check weather advisories before travelling.

In cities: use Grab in Manila and Cebu. Jeepneys and tricycles are cheap local options; agree tricycle fares first.

Vans: shared vans connect Puerto Princesa and El Nido (about 5–6 hours).

Travel Essentials

Visa for Indians: at the time of writing, Indian tourists can enter the Philippines visa-free for a short stay, with a longer visa-free stay for those holding a valid visa or residence permit from countries such as the USA, Japan, Australia, Canada, Schengen states, Singapore or the UK. Others can apply for a Philippine e-visa online. Everyone must complete the free eTravel registration online within 72 hours before arrival. These rules have changed recently, so check the Philippine embassy website for the current length of stay. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you travel.

Money: Philippine peso (₱). ₱1 is roughly ₹1.5. Cards work in cities, but carry cash for islands, tricycles and island tours; ATMs in El Nido can run out.

Daily budget (per person): a mid-range room share, food, local transport and an island tour come to about ₱3,000–5,500 a day, plus domestic flights.

SIM card: Globe and Smart sell tourist SIMs at the airport; registration with your passport is required by law.

Plugs: types A, B and C, 220V. Indian round-pin plugs may not fit type A/B sockets, so bring a universal adapter.

Tips: use reef-safe sunscreen, don't touch coral, and keep plans flexible in the wet season.

How much does a Philippines trip cost per day?

How many days do you need in Philippines?

With 5 days you can add To El Nido, Palawan and El Nido Tour A: lagoons, and a full week adds El Nido Tour C or Nacpan Beach and Back to Manila.
