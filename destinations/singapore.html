Why Visit Singapore?

Singapore is a fascinating blend of ultra-modern city-state and cultural traditions. From Marina Bay's iconic skyline to authentic hawker centers, tropical gardens, and diverse neighborhoods, Singapore offers incredible experiences in a compact, easy-to-navigate package. Perfect for 3-10 day trips with excellent public transport and world-class food.

We live here and film it every week, and it still surprises us. The energy, the food, the accessibility for first-time travelers... it's the perfect base for exploring Southeast Asia.

Singapore Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Arrival & Marina Bay

Morning
Arrive at Changi Airport. Take MRT to your hotel (SGD 2–4, 30 mins). Check in and rest or explore your neighborhood.
Afternoon
Head up to the Marina Bay Sands SkyPark Observation Deck (about S$32–40). Walk around Gardens by the Bay and the Supertree Grove (free), and snap photos at the Merlion.
Evening
Dinner at a hawker centre (S$5–8). Watch the free Garden Rhapsody light show at the Supertrees (usually 7:45 PM and 8:45 PM) and the Spectra show at Marina Bay. Explore Boat Quay or Clarke Quay for drinks.
S$60–90/day
2

Neighborhoods & Culture

Morning
Explore Chinatown. Visit Thian Hock Keng Temple (oldest Chinese temple, free). Browse souvenir shops and tea houses. Breakfast at Maxwell Food Centre (Hainanese chicken rice, SGD 4).
Afternoon
Take MRT to Orchard Road (Singapore's main shopping street, free to walk). Visit Arab Street (colorful buildings, Malay culture) and Little India (spice shops, temples).
Evening
Dinner at Zion Riverside Food Centre (authentic, less touristy). Optional: Visit a local bar or lounge.
S$50–80/day
3

Nature & Islands

Full Day
Take MRT to Sentosa Island (SGD 4 round trip). Relax at Palawan Beach or adventure at Skyline Luge (SGD 10–25). Visit S.E.A. Aquarium or try Wing Suit Flying if adventurous. Dinner back at Lau Pa Sat (chili crab or satay).
S$70–110/day
4

Zoo, River Wonders & Night Safari

5-day plan
Morning
Head to the Mandai Wildlife Reserve (bus from Khatib or Springleaf MRT, or a Grab). Start at Singapore Zoo early, when the animals are most active (about S$45–50).
Afternoon
Visit River Wonders next door for the giant pandas and a gentle Amazon river ride, or rest at your hotel during the hottest hours.
Evening
Take the tram through the Night Safari (about S$55–60; book a time slot in advance) and catch the Creatures of the Night show.
S$120–180/day
5

Little India, Katong & East Coast

5-day plan
Morning
Explore Little India: Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple, flower-garland stalls and Tekka Centre. Have a dosa or banana-leaf meal for breakfast.
Afternoon
Ride to Katong and Joo Chiat for colourful Peranakan shophouses and a bowl of Katong laksa.
Evening
Rent a bike or walk along East Coast Park, then have seafood or satay at East Coast Lagoon Food Village.
S$50–80/day
6

Botanic Gardens & Orchard Road

7-day plan
Morning
Walk the UNESCO-listed Singapore Botanic Gardens (free) and the National Orchid Garden (small fee). Families can stop at the free Jacob Ballas Children's Garden.
Afternoon
Shop or window-shop along Orchard Road; ION Orchard's food hall is good for lunch. Alternatively, visit the National Gallery or ArtScience Museum.
Evening
Take a Singapore River cruise from Clarke Quay and have dinner by the river.
S$50–90/day
7

Jewel Changi & departure

7-day plan
Morning
Pick up last souvenirs (Bengawan Solo, kaya, TWG tea) and check out. Mustafa Centre in Little India is open around the clock for budget shopping.
Afternoon
Go to Changi early and spend time at Jewel: the HSBC Rain Vortex, Shiseido Forest Valley and Canopy Park (small fee for some attractions).
Evening
Check in for your flight. Allow at least 3 hours before an international departure, and use your last dollars on a hawker meal at the airport.
S$40–80/day

Singapore Food Guide

Singapore is a food lover's paradise where incredible flavors blend across Chinese, Malay, and Indian cuisines. Hawker centers serve authentic, world-class meals at budget prices, making Singapore an unmissable culinary destination.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Hainanese Chicken Rice — Poached chicken, fragrant rice, ginger sauce. Cost: SGD 3–5
  • Laksa — Spicy curry noodle soup with coconut milk, seafood. Cost: SGD 4–7
  • Chili Crab — Mud crab in sweet, spicy tomato-chili sauce. Cost: SGD 25–50 (sharing)
  • Char Kway Teow — Stir-fried rice noodles with soy, wok hei. Cost: SGD 3–5
  • Kaya Toast — Butter toast with kaya (coconut jam) and soft-boiled eggs. Cost: SGD 3–5
  • Roti Prata — Flaky Indian flatbread, curry dipping. Cost: SGD 1–3

Best Food Centers

  • Maxwell Food Centre (Chinatown) — Historic hub, famous Tian Tian Hainanese chicken rice
  • Lau Pa Sat (Financial District) — Historic wet market, satay stalls at night
  • Zion Riverside Food Centre — Hidden gem, authentic local spot, less touristy
  • Tekka Centre (Little India) — Indian and Muslim food, excellent roti prata

Best Time to Visit Singapore

Best overall: February–April (dry, 24–31°C, Chinese New Year festivities)

Budget travelers: September–October (lowest prices, fewer crowds, 24–30°C)

Avoid: May–August (humid, afternoon rain, peak school holidays, higher prices)

Getting Around Singapore

MRT (Mass Rapid Transit) — Best Option

Cost: SGD 1.50–3.50 per journey. Buy an EZ-Link card (rechargeable, SGD 12) for slight discounts.

All-day pass: SGD 11 for unlimited rides (best if taking 4+ journeys).

Why use it: Fastest, cheapest, air-conditioned, safe, covers all major attractions.

Taxis & Grab (Ride-Share)

Taxis: Metered, flag drop SGD 3.60. Good for late nights or luggage, but expensive for longer distances.

Grab (app-based): Cheaper than taxis, surge pricing during peak hours (7–9am, 5–7pm).

Walking

Singapore is walkable. Chinatown to Marina Bay is a pleasant 1km walk. Downtown areas are pedestrian-friendly.

Travel Essentials

Visa & Entry

For Indian passport holders: a visa is required, applied for through an authorised visa agent (usually ₹2,500–3,500 all-in, a few working days). Submit the free SG Arrival Card within 3 days before arrival. Full walkthrough: Singapore visa for Indians, step by step. Check ica.gov.sg for the latest rules.

Travelling with family or adding Malaysia? See Singapore with parents or kids and our 7-day Singapore and Malaysia itinerary.

Currency & Money

Singapore Dollar (SGD). Exchange rate: 1 USD ≈ 1.35 SGD.

ATMs: Widespread, accept Visa/Mastercard. No forex needed.

Tipping: NOT expected in hawker centers or casual restaurants. 10% optional in fine dining.

Budget Breakdown (per day, budget traveler)

  • Accommodation: SGD 40–80 (hostels, budget hotels)
  • Food: SGD 20–40 (mostly hawker centers)
  • Transport: SGD 5–10 (MRT/buses)
  • Activities: SGD 10–30 (gardens free/cheap, museums SGD 10–20)
  • Total: SGD 75–160/day (~$55–120 USD)

Getting a SIM Card

Buy at Changi Airport or any convenience store. Prepaid plans: SGD 10–30 for 3–7 days unlimited data.

Singapore FAQs

How much does a Singapore trip cost per day?

Plan for roughly S$95–150 per person per day (about ₹6,200–9,800) for a mid-range trip, covering accommodation, food, local transport and sightseeing. Prices are approximate as of October 2026.

How many days do you need in Singapore?

3 days covers the highlights: Arrival & Marina Bay, Neighborhoods & Culture and Nature & Islands. With 5 days you can add Zoo, River Wonders & Night Safari and Little India, Katong & East Coast, and a full week adds Botanic Gardens & Orchard Road and Jewel Changi. Our 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries build on each other, so you can stop at any point.
