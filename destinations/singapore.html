Why Visit Singapore?

Singapore is a fascinating blend of ultra-modern city-state and cultural traditions. From Marina Bay's iconic skyline to authentic hawker centers, tropical gardens, and diverse neighborhoods, Singapore offers incredible experiences in a compact, easy-to-navigate package. Perfect for 3-10 day trips with excellent public transport and world-class food.

Singapore Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: SGD 30-60 (dorms/budget hotels)
  • Food: SGD 10-30/day (hawker to mid-range)
  • Transport: SGD 5-15/day (public transit pass)
  • Activities: SGD 20-50/day (attractions)
  • Total/day: SGD 65-155

Best Time to Visit

December-March is ideal - cooler temps, less rain. Avoid November-January monsoon season. Singapore is tropical year-round (24-32°C).

Visa Information

Most Indian passport holders get 30-day visa-free entry. Check the latest requirements on the Singapore ICA website.

Getting Around

Singapore's MRT system is world-class. Buy an EZ-Link card for convenient public transport. Grab (ride-share) is cheaper than taxis.

Food Scene

Hawker centers offer authentic, cheap (SGD 3-8) meals. Must-try: Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, chili crab. Fine dining available but expensive.

Popular Neighborhoods

Marina Bay

Ultra-modern, iconic sights, expensive accommodation/food

Chinatown

Historic, authentic, budget-friendly, great food

Arab Street (Kampong Glam)

Cultural heritage, mosques, traditional shops, medium budget

Sentosa Island

Beaches, theme parks, upscale resorts

Local Tips from Travelers

  • 🌶️ Don't miss hawker food - it's authentic and incredibly cheap
  • 🎫 Buy attractions passes/tickets online to skip queues
  • 🚇 EZ-Link card is essential for public transport
  • 🏖️ Sentosa Island can get crowded on weekends
  • 📸 Gardens by the Bay light show is free after 7:45pm
  • 🍜 Visit hawker centers during lunch hours for best experience
