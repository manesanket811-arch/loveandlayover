Why Visit Spain?

Spain pulses with energy, history, and culinary passion. Barcelona captivates with Gaudí's masterpieces and Mediterranean beaches. Madrid offers world-class art and vibrant nightlife. Seville showcases Andalusian soul with flamenco and historic plazas. The Costa del Sol tempts with sunshine and seaside charm. Perfect for 3-10 day trips with excellent trains, delicious food at every corner, and a warm, welcoming culture.

Spain Food Guide

Spanish cuisine celebrates bold flavors, fresh ingredients, and communal dining. From Mediterranean seafood to Andalusian stews, from Barcelona's molecular gastronomy to village taverns serving jamón ibérico, Spain's food culture is diverse and deeply regional. Meals are a social event—long, leisurely, and centered on family and friends.

Must-Try Spanish Dishes

  • Paella - Saffron rice with seafood or meat. Cost: €12-18 in restaurants, €15-25 for premium versions
  • Tapas - Small plates meant for sharing. Cost: €2-5 per tapa at bars, €20-35 for a full dinner
  • Jamón Ibérico - Dry-cured Iberian ham, sliced thin. Cost: €3-8 per portion at markets or bars
  • Gazpacho - Cold tomato soup, refreshing in summer. Cost: €4-7 as starter or light lunch
  • Seafood Especialties - Gambas al ajillo (shrimp in garlic), pulpo a la gallega (Galician octopus). Cost: €10-20
  • Croquetas - Fried creamy croquettes (ham, cheese, mushroom). Cost: €1.50-3 per piece

Regional Foods & Drinks

  • Sangria - Wine punch with fruit. Cost: €3-5/glass, €12-20/pitcher
  • Spanish Wine - Rioja, Tempranillo, Albariño. Wine bars: €4-7/glass, bottles €10-20
  • Churros con Chocolate - Fried dough with thick hot chocolate (breakfast/snack). Cost: €3-5
  • Tortilla Española - Potato and egg omelet (iconic Spanish dish). Cost: €3-6 per slice
  • Ajoblanco - Cold almond soup, Andalusian specialty (€4-6)
  • Fideuà - Barcelona specialty like paella with noodles instead of rice (€12-18)

Street Food & Snacks

  • Bocadillos - Spanish sandwiches, filled with jamón or chorizo (€3-6)
  • Pan con Tomate - Bread rubbed with tomato and olive oil (€2-3)
  • Empanadas - Stuffed pastries (€2-4)
  • Torrijas - Spanish French toast, especially popular during Easter (€3-5)
  • Jamón Bocadillo - Simple but perfect, jamón between bread (€4-8)

Best Places to Eat

  • Barcelona's La Boqueria Market - Fresh produce, jamón counters, seafood stalls
  • Tavernas (Tapas Bars) - Madrid's neighborhoods (Chueca, La Latina) packed with authentic spots
  • Sevilla Flamenco Districts - Eat in Triana neighborhood, lively atmosphere
  • Beachfront Chiringuitos - Costa del Sol chiringuito bars serve fresh seafood paella right on the sand (€10-15)
  • Wine Bars (Bodegas) - Sherry wine bars in Jerez; cost €4-8/glass, €15-25 for tapas

Dining Tips

Spaniards eat lunch 2-3pm (main meal) and dinner 8-10pm (lighter). Restaurants rarely open before 1pm or 8pm. Menus del día (lunch specials) offer 3 courses + drink for €10-15—excellent value. Tapas culture: order several plates to share, drink while enjoying bites. Coffee after dinner aids digestion. Tipping: 5-10% appreciated but not mandatory. Water is free but small bottles at bars cost €2.

Best Time to Visit Spain

Spring (March - May)

Weather: 12-22°C, mild and sunny. Crowds: Moderate (Easter peak).

Highlights: Perfect weather for sightseeing. Easter celebrations, parades. Orange blossoms bloom. Outdoor cafes open. Regional festivals begin.

Costs: Mid-range pricing. Hotels 15-25% more than winter. Book 6-8 weeks ahead.

Best for: Ideal balance of weather and crowds.

Summer (June - August)

Weather: 25-35°C, hot and dry. Crowds: EXTREMELY HIGH (peak season).

Highlights: Perfect beach weather. Fiestas and festivals everywhere. Long daylight (sunset at 9:30pm). Outdoor concerts and flamenco.

Costs: HIGHEST prices. Hotels 40-50% more expensive. Beaches and cities packed. Book early.

Best for: Beach lovers comfortable with crowds and heat.

Autumn (September - October)

Weather: 18-28°C, warm and sunny—excellent. Crowds: Lower than summer (schools resume).

Highlights: Still warm enough for beach, but fewer tourists. Wine harvest season. Clear skies for sightseeing. Comfortable walking weather.

Costs: High but less than summer. Book 1 month ahead.

Best for: Best overall—excellent weather, fewer crowds, reasonable prices.

Winter (November - February)

Weather: 8-18°C in north, 12-22°C in south. Mild but rainy north. Crowds: Low.

Highlights: Fewer tourists, authentic Spain. Southern coast (Granada, Málaga) still pleasant. Christmas markets. Winter sales in January. Ski season in Sierra Nevada.

Costs: LOWEST prices, 30-40% cheaper than summer. Best value.

Best for: Budget travelers, those avoiding crowds, southern destinations (Andalusia sunny).

Monthly Cost Comparison

Most Expensive: July-August. Moderately Expensive: April-May, June, September-October. Cheapest: November-February. Budget note: November-February offers the best value, with southern Spain staying warm.

Festival Calendar

  • La Tomatina (August) - Tomato-throwing festival in Valencia (booking required €10)
  • Running of the Bulls - San Fermín (July) - Pamplona's wild festival (book accommodations 6 months ahead)
  • Flamenco Festivals - Throughout Andalusia, especially Jerez, Seville, Granada
  • Las Fallas (March) - Valencia's fiery festival with giant paper sculptures

Recommendation

Best overall: September-October (perfect weather, fewer crowds, good prices). Budget travelers: November-February (lowest prices, south stays warm). First-timers: May-June (spring beauty, manageable crowds).

Getting Around Spain

Renfe (Spanish Railways) - Efficient Network

AVE (High-Speed Trains) - Spain's fastest trains. Madrid to Barcelona: 2.5 hours, €30-80. Madrid to Seville: 2.5 hours, €25-70. Book online via Renfe.com or Trainline.eu for 30-50% discounts. Travel off-peak for cheaper fares.

Regional Trains - Slower but 40-60% cheaper. Scenic routes like Andalusia perfect for exploring. Cost: €15-35 depending on distance.

Renfe Pass: Spain Rail Pass (3-10 days unlimited): €200-450. Only worth if taking 4+ long journeys.

City Transport

  • Barcelona Metro: 11 lines, efficient. Single ride: €2.45. T-10 card (10 rides): €11.35. Day pass (T-día): €9.40
  • Madrid Metro: Extensive network. Single ride: €2.45. 10-ride ticket: €12. Day pass: €9
  • Seville Buses: Main transport. Single ride: €1.40. Day pass: €6
  • Walking: Barcelona's Gothic Quarter, Madrid's Centro, Seville's Santa Cruz walkable and charming

Long-Distance Buses

FlixBus, Alsa, and regional carriers offer budget alternatives. Cost: €10-30 depending on distance. Slower but significantly cheaper than trains. Useful for small towns and southern regions. Book online for best prices.

Regional Transport

  • Andalusia: Granada, Málaga, Córdoba connected by buses and trains
  • Costa del Sol: Trains and buses connect beach towns (€2-6 per ride)
  • Islands: Balearic Islands (Mallorca, Ibiza, Menorca) reached by ferry from mainland (€30-50)
  • Domestic Flights: Iberia, Vueling offer cheap routes if booked early (€20-50)

Car Rental

International Driving Permit required. Useful for Andalusia wine regions, Balearic islands, Costa del Sol. Cost: €30-50/day. Tolls on highways: €5-20. Fuel expensive (€1.50+/liter). Road signs in English. Parking in cities expensive (€15-25/day) and complicated.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Book AVE trains 4-6 weeks ahead for 50% discount
  • Travel Tuesday-Thursday for 30% cheaper fares
  • Regional trains cost 60% less than AVE for scenic routes
  • Barcelona: Get T-mobilitat card (rechargeable, can share between people)
  • Bus passes often cheaper than metro/train for multi-day stays

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: €25-60/night (hostels €15-30, mid-range €40-60, hotels €80-120+)
  • Food: €20-40/day (street food €3-5/meal, tapas/menus del día €10-18, fine dining €20+)
  • Trains: €15-40/journey (regional), €25-70 (AVE high-speed)
  • Activities: €10-25/day (museum entry €5-15, metro day pass €9)
  • Total daily budget: €45-80 (~$49-87 USD) for budget travelers

Visa & Entry Requirements

For Indian Passport Holders: Spain is part of the Schengen Area. Indian nationals need a Schengen visa. Tourist visas valid for 90 days within a 180-day period. Apply at Spanish embassy/consulate 4-6 weeks before travel. Documents: Passport (valid 6+ months), proof of funds (€40-50/day), accommodation bookings, return flight, and travel insurance (required for Schengen).

For EU/EEA Citizens: No visa needed. Bring passport or national ID card.

Travel Insurance: Required for Schengen visa. Cost: €50-120 for 7-10 days. Must be purchased before visa application.

Currency & Money

Currency: Euro (€). Exchange rates: 1 USD ≈ €0.92-0.95. ATMs widely available. Card payments work almost everywhere.

Withdrawals: ATMs at banks, train stations, tourist areas. Fees minimal (€1-3). Visa/Mastercard work. Avoid airport money changers (poor rates).

Payments: Card accepted in restaurants, shops, metros. Small establishments may prefer cash. Carry €50-100 for street vendors and small bars.

SIM Cards & Mobile

Local SIM Options: Buy at airport or Telefónica/Orange stores. Cost: €15-25 with 10GB data valid 1 month.

Pocket Wi-Fi: €5-8/day from airport. Unlimited data, connects 5+ devices.

eSIM: Download apps like Airalo or Holafly before arrival. Cost: €10-20 for 10GB.
