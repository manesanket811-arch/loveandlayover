Why Visit Spain?

Spain offers vibrant culture, stunning architecture, passionate flamenco dancing, and some of Europe's best beaches. From Gaudi's whimsical structures in Barcelona to Madrid's world-class museums and Spain's golden coastal shores, the country exudes energy and romance. Perfect for art lovers, architecture enthusiasts, beach seekers, and culture immersion travelers.

Experience the energy of Madrid, explore Barcelona's Gothic Quarter and Sagrada Familia, dance to flamenco rhythms, enjoy Mediterranean beaches, and taste world-class Spanish wine and tapas. Spain is unforgettable.

3-Day Spain Itinerary

A 3-day plan with morning, afternoon and evening ideas and an approximate daily cost per person. Use it as a starting point and adjust to your pace.

1

Barcelona: Gaudí & Gothic Quarter

Morning
Arrive at Barcelona airport. Metro to hotel (EUR 5.15). Visit Sagrada Familia - reserve pre-booked tickets (EUR 26 basic entry). Marvel at Gaudí's masterpiece. Grab café con leche and churros (EUR 5-8).
Afternoon
Explore Park Güell (EUR 14 with guide route). See colorful mosaics, panoramic city views. Walk Las Ramblas - famous tree-lined boulevard (free). Browse street performers, shops, cafes. Visit Gothic Quarter - medieval architecture (free walking).
Evening
Flamenco show with dinner (EUR 50-80, book ahead). Alternatively: tapas crawl in El Born (EUR 20-35 for multiple small plates). Experience lively Spanish nightlife. Walk Montjuïc at dusk for sunset city views.
EUR 85-150/day
2

Barcelona Beaches & Montjuïc

Morning
Relax at Barceloneta Beach (free entry). Swim in Mediterranean. Grab paella or seafood lunch at beachfront (EUR 15-25). Try Spanish cava or horchata (EUR 3-6).
Afternoon
Visit Montjuïc fortress (EUR 5 entry or free during hours). Explore Joan Miró Museum (EUR 12) or MNAC art museum (EUR 12). Cable car rides for views (EUR 7-9). Gardens are free to walk.
Evening
Magic Fountain light show at Montjuïc (free viewing, 8-10pm May-Sep). Dinner at seafood restaurant (EUR 20-40). Enjoy vermouth in El Xampanyet bar (EUR 3-4 per glass).
EUR 70-130/day
3

Madrid: Museums & Royal Palace

Morning
Train Barcelona to Madrid (EUR 25-50, 2.5 hours). Metro to hotel (EUR 3). Visit Prado Museum (EUR 15, one of world's finest). See masterpieces by Velázquez, Goya. Coffee and ensaimada pastry (EUR 5-8).
Afternoon
Walk Retiro Park - peaceful oasis (free). Royal Palace tour (EUR 12 with audio guide). Plaza Mayor - historic central square (free). Explore Sol district for shopping (free browsing).
Evening
Traditional Spanish dinner with wine (EUR 25-45). Tapas bar crawl on Cava Baja street (EUR 15-30 for multiple) or late-night churros (EUR 4-6). Experience Madrid's vibrant nightlife.
EUR 95-155/day

Spain Food Guide

Spanish cuisine is regional, vibrant, and celebrates fresh ingredients. Tapas culture allows trying many dishes affordably. Spanish wine (especially Rioja and Tempranillo) pairs perfectly with food.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Paella Valenciana – Saffron rice with seafood or chicken. Cost: EUR 14-25
  • Tapas Platter – Mixed small plates: jamón, queso, aceitunas. Cost: EUR 8-15
  • Patatas Bravas – Crispy fried potatoes with spicy sauce. Cost: EUR 5-8
  • Gambas al Ajillo – Garlic shrimp. Cost: EUR 12-18
  • Churros con Chocolate – Fried pastry with hot chocolate. Cost: EUR 4-6
  • Jamón Ibérico – Cured Iberian ham. Cost: EUR 4-8 per serving
  • Gazpacho – Cold tomato soup (summer). Cost: EUR 5-9
  • Flan – Caramel custard dessert. Cost: EUR 5-7

Best Food Spots

  • Tapas Bars – Traditional social eating experience
  • Mercados (Markets) – Fresh produce, local specialties, casual dining
  • Marisquerías – Seafood specialist restaurants
  • Churrerías – For churros and hot chocolate breakfasts

Best Time to Visit Spain

Best overall: April-May and September-October (pleasant 15-25°C, fewer crowds than summer)

Summer: June-August (hot 28-35°C, crowded, expensive, ideal for beaches)

Avoid: July-August peak (extremely crowded, very hot, highest prices)

Budget tip: November-February (cold 5-15°C, rainy, lowest prices and fewer tourists)

Getting Around Spain

Metro & Buses

Modern metro systems in Barcelona and Madrid. Metro tickets: EUR 2-3 per ride. T-Casual 10-ticket card: EUR 11-12. Buses also available (EUR 2-2.45).

Trains

Renfe operates intercity trains. Barcelona-Madrid: EUR 25-50. Book in advance for discounts. Scenic routes available.

Taxis & Ride-share

Taxis metered in major cities. Uber available. Within cities: EUR 5-15 typical fare.

Organized Tours

Walking tours: EUR 10-20. Food tours: EUR 40-65. Skip-the-line museum tickets: EUR 15-30 extra.

Travel Essentials

Visa & Entry

Visa for Indians: a Schengen short-stay visa is required. Apply through the Spain visa centre in India (VFS Global or the appointed provider) if Spain is your main destination, up to 6 months and at least 15 days before you travel. The fee is €90 for adults, processing usually takes about 15 days (longer in summer), and you need travel insurance with at least €30,000 medical cover. The visa allows up to 90 days in any 180-day period across the Schengen area. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you apply.

Currency & Money

Euro (EUR). Exchange rate: 1 EUR ≈ 1.10 USD. ATMs widely available. Tipping not mandatory but appreciated (5-10% at restaurants).

Budget Breakdown (per day, budget traveler)

  • Accommodation: EUR 30-60 (hostels, budget hotels)
  • Food: EUR 20-35 (tapas bars, casual dining)
  • Transport: EUR 5-10 (metro tickets)
  • Activities: EUR 25-50 (museums, attractions)
  • Total: EUR 80-155/day (~$88-171 USD)

Important Notes

  • Meal times: lunch 2-3pm, dinner 8-11pm. Restaurants may close 3-7pm.
  • Siesta culture - many shops close early afternoon.
  • Pickpocketing in tourist areas - keep valuables secure.
  • Museums often free during certain hours - check schedules.
  • Flamenco shows book ahead - they sell out, especially weekends.
