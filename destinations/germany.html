Why Visit Germany?

Germany blends cutting-edge modernity with fairy-tale tradition. Berlin's vibrant culture and history contrasts with Bavaria's alpine charm and beer culture. Explore medieval castles along the Rhine, charming Bavarian villages, and world-class museums. Germany has excellent trains, efficient infrastructure, and affordable prices for Western Europe. Perfect for 3-10 day trips combining cities, countryside, and mountain scenery. World-class beer, pretzels, and sausages are bonuses.

Germany Food Guide

German cuisine is hearty, satisfying, and deeply rooted in regional traditions. Meat, bread, potatoes, and cabbage form the foundation. Beer culture is integral to food enjoyment. Each region has specialties—Bavaria differs from Berlin differs from Rhine Valley.

Must-Try German Dishes

  • Schnitzel - Breaded pork cutlet, crispy and tender. Cost: €10-15
  • Bratwurst - Grilled sausage, regional variations everywhere. Cost: €3-7
  • Pretzels (Bretzel) - Soft, oversized, salty. Cost: €2-4
  • Pork Knuckle (Schweinshaxe) - Bavarian specialty, massive and delicious. Cost: €18-28
  • Sauerbraten - Pot roast marinated in vinegar, tender and flavorful. Cost: €12-18
  • Sauerkraut (Sauerkohl) - Fermented cabbage, accompaniment and side. Cost: €2-5

Seafood & Fish

  • Fischbrötchen - Fish sandwich, North German specialty. Cost: €5-8
  • Trout (Forelle) - Fresh and smoked versions. Cost: €12-18
  • Pike Perch (Zander) - Delicate white fish. Cost: €14-20

Street Food & Snacks

  • Currywurst - Sausage with curry ketchup, Berlin icon. Cost: €3-5
  • Döner Kebab - Turkish sandwich, everywhere in Germany. Cost: €4-7
  • Leberkäse - Bavarian meatloaf, breakfast specialty. Cost: €2-4
  • Apfelstrudel - Apple pastry, Vienna's famous export adopted in Germany. Cost: €3-5

Best Places to Eat

  • Beer Halls (Hofbräuhaus Munich) - Traditional, lively, sausages and pretzels
  • Marienplatz Markets (Munich) - Casual food stalls, affordable
  • Street Food Stalls (Berlin) - Currywurst, döner, kebab €3-7
  • Restaurants (Gasthof/Gasthaus) - Family-run, authentic, €8-15
  • Bakeries (Bäckerei) - Cheap, quality bread, sausages, pastries €2-5

Beer & Drinks

Germany is beer paradise. Cost: €3-6 per liter beer hall, €4-7 regular restaurants. Pilsner, Weizen (wheat beer), Dunkel (dark) are main styles. Biergarten culture in Bavaria. Wine from Rhine Valley also excellent (Riesling). Coffee strong and good—€1.50-3 per cup.

Dining Tips

Lunch often has cheaper set menus (€7-12) than dinner. Bread basket at table costs if you eat (€1-3). Water is free if you ask for tap water. Tipping 5-10% appreciated but not required. Food portions generous—come hungry.

Best Time to Visit Germany

Spring (April - May)

Weather: 10-18°C, mild and increasing. Crowds: Moderate, increasing towards May.

Highlights: Mild weather perfect for walking. Biergartens open. Easter celebrations. Longer daylight (14+ hours by May). Flowers blooming in gardens and parks.

Costs: Mid-range. Hotels €60-120. Fair value season.

Best for: Outdoor enthusiasts, biergarten season starters, shoulder season travelers.

Summer (June - August)

Weather: 18-28°C, warm and mostly sunny. Crowds: Peak season.

Highlights: Warmest weather, biergartens packed, beer festivals (Oktoberfest in September), open-air concerts, Rhine wine festivals.

Costs: HIGHEST. Hotels €100-180. Book early or pay premium.

Best for: Oktoberfest planning (book in July for September), festival enthusiasts, warm weather seekers.

Autumn (September - October)

Weather: 12-22°C, crisp and clear. Crowds: Declining, still pleasant.

Highlights: Oktoberfest (September 21-October 6 2026 in Munich). Wine harvest in Rhine Valley. Fall colors in forests. Still warm enough for outdoor activities.

Costs: Moderate-High during Oktoberfest (hotels booked 1 year ahead). Rest of autumn: €70-130/night.

Best for: Oktoberfest celebrants, wine enthusiasts, photographers, perfect weather without summer crowds.

Winter (November - March)

Weather: 0-8°C, wet with occasional snow. Crowds: Very low.

Highlights: Christmas markets (November-December) magical and charming. Festive season atmosphere. Ski season in Bavarian Alps. Museums less crowded. Winter clarity.

Costs: LOWEST. Hotels €40-80. 40-50% cheaper than summer.

Best for: Christmas market visitors, budget travelers, those seeking authentic experience without tourists.

Recommendation

Best overall: May-June or September-October (perfect weather, manageable crowds, fair prices).

Budget travelers: November-March (lowest prices, Christmas markets), excluding Oktoberfest

Oktoberfest: September (book 1 year ahead, premium prices).

Getting Around Germany

Trains (Deutsche Bahn - DB)

Berlin to Munich: 10 hours by day train or 11 hours overnight. Cost: €50-120 depending on advance booking. Scenic journey. Book online at Bahn.de for discounts (cheapest 12 weeks ahead).

Berlin to Rhine: 6-8 hours to Cologne or Frankfurt. Cost: €40-90.

Munich to Neuschwanstein: 2 hours to Füssen, then bus/car. Cost: €15-25.

German Rail Pass: Unlimited travel for 3/7/10/15 days (€200-400). Only worth it if taking 4+ long journeys.

Metro & U-Bahn (Subway)

  • Berlin U-Bahn: 9 lines, efficient. Cost: €2.90-3.00 per ride. Day pass: €9
  • Munich U-Bahn: 6 lines. Cost: €2.80 per ride. Day pass: €8.60
  • Frankfurt U-Bahn: 9 lines. Cost: €2.80 per ride

Buses

Flixbus operates long-distance routes (Berlin to Munich €20-40). Cheaper than trains but slower (12+ hours). Local city buses excellent and cheap (€2-3 per ride).

Taxis & Ride-Sharing

Taxis expensive. Flag drop: €3-4 + €1.50-2/km. Uber available in major cities (Berlin, Munich). Often only slightly cheaper than taxis.

Car Rental

International Driving Permit required. Useful for Rhine Valley, Bavarian countryside. Cost: €40-80/day. Roads excellent. Parking easy in towns, expensive in city centers (€2-5/hour).

Money-Saving Tips

  • Book trains 12 weeks ahead for 50-70% discounts
  • Night trains save accommodation (Berlin-Munich overnight)
  • Flixbus for budget intercity (cheaper than trains if booked early)
  • City U-Bahn passes save money if staying 3+ days

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: €50-150/night (hostels €20-35, mid-range €60-100, hotels €120+)
  • Food: €20-40/day (street food €3-7, beer hall €8-15, restaurants €15-25)
  • Trains: €15-90 per journey (book ahead for discounts)
  • Activities: €10-30/day (museums €5-12, castle visits €8-15, beer halls free)
  • Total daily budget: €40-75/day for budget travelers

Visa & Entry Requirements

EU/Schengen Citizens: Passport or ID card. No visa needed.

Indian Passport Holders: 90-day Schengen visa required. Apply at German embassy in New Delhi or consulate in Mumbai/Bengaluru/Chennai. Processing: 5-15 days. Cost: €80. Proof: €2,000+ funds, return flight, accommodation, travel insurance. Official source: German Federal Foreign Office - Visa Information Last verified: July 2026.

US/UK/Australia: Visa-free for 90 days within Schengen area.

Currency: Euro (€). 1 USD ≈ €0.92. ATMs widely available. Debit cards accepted.

Getting a SIM: Buy from Deutsche Telekom, Vodafone, or O2 at airport (€15-40 for 8-10 days data).

Travel Insurance

Recommended for Schengen area. Cost: €1-2/day. Covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost luggage.
