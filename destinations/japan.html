Why Visit Japan?

Japan is a mesmerizing blend of ultra-modern technology and ancient tradition. From Tokyo's electric energy and neon-lit streets to Kyoto's serene temples and bamboo forests, Mount Fuji's iconic peak, and pristine beaches, Japan delivers unforgettable experiences. It's one of the most traveler-friendly countries globally with excellent public transit, incredible food, and a unique culture that feels both futuristic and timeless.

Whether you're exploring bustling city centers, quiet mountain villages, or relaxing in onsen (hot springs), Japan offers something for every traveler. The food aloneâ€”ramen, sushi, tempura, okonomiyakiâ€”is worth the trip.

Getting Around Japan

Japan Railways (JR): The backbone of Japan's transport. Shinkansen (bullet train) connects major cities. Fast, reliable, and punctual (average delay: 1 minute!). Single journey tickets or JR Pass for multiple trips.

Tokyo Metro: Excellent subway system in Tokyo. A single ride costs ¥150â€“220 depending on distance. Get a Suica card (rechargeable, ¥2,000 with ¥1,500 balance) from convenience stores or ticket machines.

Buses: Useful for exploring rural areas. Most accept Suica cards. Check local schedules before boarding.

Taxis: Expensive but abundant. Base fare ¥500, then ¥80 per 1 km. Use only if necessary or for short distances.

IC Cards: Suica (Tokyo area) and Icoca (Kyoto area) work on trains, buses, and at convenience stores. Highly convenient.
