Why Visit Japan?

Japan seamlessly blends ancient tradition with cutting-edge technology. From bustling Tokyo to serene Kyoto temples, snow-capped Mount Fuji to peaceful gardens, Japan offers unforgettable experiences. Perfect for 3-10 day trips with excellent rail systems connecting all major cities.

Travel Essentials

Budget

  • Accommodation: ¥3,000-8,000/night
  • Food: ¥2,000-5,000/day
  • JR Pass: ¥7,650-29,650 (multi-day trains)
  • Activities: ¥1,000-3,000/day

Best Time

Spring (March-May) for cherry blossoms, Autumn (Sept-Nov) for pleasant weather and foliage.

Visa & Entry

Indian passport holders get 90-day tourist visa exemption. Register with embassy before travel.
