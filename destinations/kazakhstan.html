Why Visit Kazakhstan?

Kazakhstan has become one of the hottest trips for Indians: snow-capped mountains right above a modern city, alpine lakes, canyons and ski slopes, all about 4 hours from Delhi, and visa-free for short stays.

Base yourself in Almaty, a green, leafy city at the foot of the Tian Shan mountains. In minutes you're up at Medeu and Shymbulak; longer trips add the red-rock Charyn Canyon and the turquoise Kaindy and Kolsai lakes.

Kazakhstan Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Almaty city & Kok-Tobe

Morning
Arrive and check in near Panfilov Park or Abay Avenue.
Afternoon
See the wooden Zenkov Cathedral in Panfilov Park, then the Green Bazaar for dried fruit, nuts and local snacks.
Evening
Take the cable car up Kok-Tobe for sunset views over the city and mountains.
KZT 25,000–40,000/day
2

Medeu & Shymbulak

Morning
Drive up to Medeu, the high-altitude skating rink (open in winter).
Afternoon
Ride the gondolas to Shymbulak at about 3,200 m for snow views all year; ski or snowboard in winter.
Evening
Back in town, dinner on Panfilov Street or the Arbat (Zhibek Zholy).
KZT 35,000–55,000/day
3

Big Almaty Lake

Morning
Morning trip to Big Almaty Lake, a turquoise reservoir high in the mountains. Access rules change by season, so go with a licensed tour or driver.
Afternoon
Lunch at a mountain café, then relax at the Arasan baths or browse the city's malls.
Evening
Try Kazakh and Uyghur dishes such as lagman and plov for dinner.
KZT 30,000–50,000/day
4

Charyn Canyon to Saty village

5-day plan
Morning
Drive about 3–4 hours east to Charyn Canyon and walk the Valley of Castles.
Afternoon
Continue to Saty village through the steppe.
Evening
Overnight in a village guesthouse; the night skies are spectacular.
KZT 35,000–55,000/day (incl. travel)
5

Kaindy & Kolsai lakes

5-day plan
Morning
Visit Kaindy Lake, with its sunken forest of tree trunks (a 4×4 for the last stretch).
Afternoon
Walk along the first Kolsai lake through pine forest, or ride a horse.
Evening
Drive back to Almaty in the evening.
KZT 35,000–55,000/day (incl. travel)
6

Turgen Gorge & the Assy plateau

7-day plan
Morning
Day trip to Turgen Gorge for waterfalls and short hikes.
Afternoon
Continue to the Assy plateau for wide alpine meadows (summer).
Evening
Relaxed dinner in Almaty.
KZT 30,000–50,000/day
7

Shopping & departure

7-day plan
Morning
Buy souvenirs: felt crafts, Kazakh chocolates (Rakhat) and dried fruit.
Afternoon
A last coffee in one of Almaty's cafés or a walk in the Botanical Garden.
Evening
Head to the airport, about 30–40 minutes from the centre.
KZT 20,000–35,000/day

Food to Try in Kazakhstan

  • Beshbarmak — The national dish: boiled meat (often horse or lamb) on flat noodles with onion broth.
  • Plov — Rice cooked with carrots, onions and meat in a big kazan.
  • Lagman — Hand-pulled Uyghur noodles with vegetables and meat; ask for a vegetable version.
  • Manti and samsa — Steamed dumplings and baked pastries; pumpkin fillings are vegetarian.
  • Baursak — Fried dough puffs served with tea at every meal.
  • Kurt and kumis — Dried salty cheese balls and fermented mare's milk, for the adventurous.

For Indian travellers: Kazakh food is meat-heavy, so vegetarians should look for salads, vegetable lagman, pumpkin samsa and Uyghur vegetable dishes. Almaty has several Indian restaurants, and many hotels serve breakfast buffets.

Best Time to Visit Kazakhstan

Late spring to early autumn (May–September): the best time for lakes, canyons and hikes; mountain roads to Kolsai and Big Almaty Lake are open.

September–October: golden autumn colours and comfortable city weather.

Winter (December–March): snowy and cold (often below –10°C) but perfect for skiing at Shymbulak, a big draw for Indian travellers.

April: mixed weather; some mountain areas are still snowbound.

Getting Around Kazakhstan

Flights from India: direct flights from Delhi to Almaty take about 4 hours; check current routes and other cities when you book.

In Almaty: Yandex Go taxis are cheap and easy (pay in the app or cash). The city has a short metro line and buses using an Onay card.

Mountains and lakes: book tours or a private driver for Charyn, Kaindy, Kolsai and Big Almaty Lake; distances are long and some roads need a 4×4.

Astana: the capital is a 1½-hour flight away if you want to add its futuristic architecture.

Travel Essentials

Entry for Indians: Indian passport holders can visit Kazakhstan visa-free for up to 14 days per visit (up to 42 days in any 180 days) for tourism. Longer stays need an e-visa. Carry a return ticket and hotel booking; hotels register you automatically. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you travel.

Money: Kazakhstani tenge (KZT). 1,000 KZT is about ₹170. Cards work almost everywhere in Almaty; carry cash for villages.

Daily budget (per person): a mid-range hotel share, food, local transport and sightseeing come to about KZT 30,000–50,000 a day, plus tours to the lakes and canyons.

SIM card: Beeline, Kcell and Tele2 sell SIMs with your passport; an eSIM is the easiest option.

Plugs: types C and F, 220V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; bring an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Etiquette and safety: Almaty is safe and modern. Pack warm layers even in summer for the mountains, and go with licensed guides for remote hikes.

Kazakhstan FAQs

How much does a Kazakhstan trip cost per day?

Plan for roughly KZT 30,000–50,000 per person per day (about ₹5,100–8,500) for a mid-range trip, covering accommodation, food, local transport and sightseeing. Prices are approximate as of October 2026.

How many days do you need in Kazakhstan?

3 days covers the highlights: Almaty city & Kok-Tobe, Medeu & Shymbulak and Big Almaty Lake. With 5 days you can add Charyn Canyon to Saty village and Kaindy & Kolsai lakes, and a full week adds Turgen Gorge & the Assy plateau and Shopping. Our 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries build on each other, so you can stop at any point.

When is the best time to visit Kazakhstan?

May–Sep (snow Dec–Mar). Late spring to early autumn (May–September): the best time for lakes, canyons and hikes; mountain roads to Kolsai and Big Almaty Lake are open.
