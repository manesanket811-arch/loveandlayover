Why Visit Mexico?

Mexico is a treasure trove of ancient civilizations, pristine beaches, and vibrant culture. From world-class Riviera Maya resorts to mysterious Mayan ruins, historic Mexico City to colorful colonial towns, Mexico offers incredible diversity. Perfect for beach lovers, history buffs, food enthusiasts, and adventure seekers on 3-10 day trips.

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: MXN 500-1,500/night
  • Food: MXN 300-800/day
  • Transport: MXN 200-500/day
  • Activities: MXN 500-1,500/day

Best Time to Visit

November-April (dry, 25-32°C) is ideal. May-October is hurricane season (hot, humid). December-January is peak but expensive.

Visa Requirements

Indian nationals get 180-day tourist visa on arrival. Just need a valid passport. Quick and hassle-free entry.

Highlights

Cancun: Beaches, nightlife, water sports. Riviera Maya: Tulum ruins, Playa del Carmen, cenotes. Mexico City: Museums, colonial architecture, street food. Chichen Itza: Iconic Mayan pyramid.
