Why Visit Vietnam?

Vietnam is a land of contrasts with ancient traditions, stunning natural landscapes, and vibrant cities. Explore chaotic Hanoi with its street food culture, cruise through the limestone karsts of Halong Bay, trek through rice terraces in the north, and experience the electric energy of Ho Chi Minh City. Vietnam offers incredible value with diverse experiences across 3-10 days.

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: VND 150,000-400,000/night
  • Food: VND 100,000-300,000/day
  • Transport: VND 50,000-150,000/day
  • Activities: VND 200,000-600,000/day

Best Time to Visit

October-April (cool, dry season, 15-25°C) is ideal. Avoid May-September (hot, humid, monsoon). September-October offers good value with fewer tourists.

Visa Requirements

Indian nationals require a visa. E-visa available online (easy process, ~24-48 hours). Alternatively, visa on arrival in Hanoi/Ho Chi Minh.

Highlights

Hanoi: Old Quarter, temples, Hoan Kiem Lake, street food. Halong Bay: UNESCO limestone karsts, overnight cruises. Ho Chi Minh: War museums, Ben Thanh Market, Mekong Delta day trips.
