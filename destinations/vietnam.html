Why Visit Vietnam?

Vietnam is a land of contrasts with ancient traditions, stunning natural landscapes, and vibrant cities. Explore chaotic Hanoi with its street food culture, cruise through the limestone karsts of Halong Bay, trek through rice terraces in the north, and experience the electric energy of Ho Chi Minh City. Vietnam offers incredible value with diverse experiences across 3-10 days.

Vietnam Food Guide

Vietnamese cuisine is among Asia's finest—fresh herbs, bold flavors, and incredible variety at unbeatable prices. From steaming bowls of pho to crispy spring rolls and aromatic street food, Vietnam is a food lover's dream destination. Every meal is an adventure.

Must-Try Vietnamese Dishes

  • Pho (Phở) - Rice noodle soup with broth, herbs, meat. Cost: VND 30,000-60,000
  • Banh Mi (Bánh Mì) - Vietnamese sandwich with pâté, cold cuts, pickled vegetables, cilantro. Cost: VND 20,000-40,000
  • Spring Rolls (Cuon) - Fresh rice paper rolls with shrimp, herbs, dipping sauce. Cost: VND 20,000-35,000
  • Bun Cha (Bún Chả) - Grilled pork with rice noodles, herbs, dipping sauce. Cost: VND 40,000-70,000
  • Vietnamese Iced Coffee (Cà Phê Sữa Đá) - Strong coffee with sweetened condensed milk over ice. Cost: VND 15,000-30,000
  • Cao Lau (Cao Lâu) - Hoi An specialty: noodles with pork, greens, crispy croutons. Cost: VND 40,000-60,000
  • Com Tam (Cơm Tấm) - Broken rice with grilled pork chop, egg, pickled vegetables. Cost: VND 35,000-55,000
  • Cha Ca La Vong (Chả Cá La Vọng) - Hanoi specialty: turmeric and dill fish. Cost: VND 80,000-150,000

Street Food & Snacks

  • Bánh Xèo - Crispy pancake with shrimp and pork (VND 30,000-50,000), popular in Hoi An
  • Fried Spring Rolls (Nem) - Crispy rolls with meat and vegetables (VND 15,000-25,000 for 2)
  • Sticky Rice with Mango - Sweet coconut sticky rice with fresh mango (VND 30,000-50,000)
  • Egg Coffee (Cà Phê Trứng) - Hanoi's famous coffee topped with whisked egg (VND 25,000-40,000)
  • Tapioca Pearls with Coconut Milk - Refreshing dessert drink (VND 15,000-25,000)

Best Places to Eat

  • Hanoi Old Quarter - Street food paradise, narrow alleys packed with local vendors, best for authenticity and budget
  • Ben Thanh Market (Ho Chi Minh City) - Fresh produce, street food stalls, local dining experience
  • Hoi An Town - UNESCO historic site with charming restaurants, famous for Cao Lau and Bánh Xèo
  • Hanoi Night Markets - Street carts and vendors set up in evenings, prime dining culture
  • Mekong Delta Villages - Family-run restaurants serving river fish specialties and regional dishes

Dining Tips

Eat where you see queues of local Vietnamese—they know the best spots. Most street vendors have menus displayed (often in Vietnamese; point at photos). Pho is usually breakfast, Banh Mi for lunch. Fresh herbs are served on the side; add to your taste. Tap water is NOT safe; drink bottled water. Most family-run spots accept cash only. Bargain respectfully for street food; quoted prices are often negotiable.

Best Time to Visit Vietnam

October - April (Cool and Dry Season - Peak Season)

Weather: 15-25°C in the north, 22-30°C in the south, dry and pleasant. Crowds: High (peak tourism season).

Highlights: Ideal climate perfect for exploring. Clear skies, low humidity. Halong Bay cruises most enjoyable. Rice paddies golden in north. Cooler temperatures in Hanoi and northern mountains.

Costs: Peak pricing. Hotels 20-30% more expensive. Book 2-3 months ahead for accommodations and Halong cruises.

Best for: First-time visitors, those prioritizing ideal weather, Halong Bay cruises.

May - September (Hot and Humid - Monsoon Season - Low Season)

Weather: 25-35°C, humid with afternoon rain showers. Crowds: Low (fewest international tourists).

Highlights: Lush green landscapes from rain, rice paddies at their most beautiful, local life returns to normal. Fewer tourists mean authentic experiences. Mangoes and tropical fruits at peak.

Costs: LOWEST prices of the year. Hotels 30-40% cheaper. Flights discounted. Tours less crowded.

Concerns: Afternoon rain common (short showers, not all-day rain). Halong Bay can be choppy. High humidity. Northern mountains slippery on trails.

Best for: Budget travelers, experience-focused explorers, photographers seeking green landscapes, those avoiding crowds.

December - January (Winter Peak)

Weather: 10-20°C in the north (coldest months), 25-28°C in the south, very pleasant. Crowds: VERY HIGH (holiday season).

Highlights: Ideal weather in both north and south. Comfortable for trekking in mountains. Year-end celebrations, festive atmosphere. Lunar New Year preparations (late January/early February).

Costs: High. Peak pricing Dec 20-Jan 2. Book 3+ months ahead.

Best for: Those prioritizing perfect weather and willing to book early.

February - March (Shoulder Season - Good Value)

Weather: 15-28°C, mild and pleasant in most regions. Crowds: Moderate (after peak season, before summer).

Highlights: Still pleasant weather. Lower crowds than winter months. Better accommodation availability. Lunar New Year celebrations (late January/early February).

Costs: Mid-range pricing. Good value between peak and low season.

Best for: Budget travelers seeking decent weather, those valuing authenticity over perfect conditions.

Recommendation

Best overall: October-November or March-April (ideal weather, manageable crowds, good value).

Best weather: December-January (coolest and driest).

Budget travelers: May-September (lowest prices, green landscapes, authentic experiences).

First-timers: October-April (reliable weather, best for Halong Bay).

Vietnam Neighborhoods & Regions

Hanoi: The Chaotic Capital

Hanoi is Vietnam's cultural and political heart—a city where colonial French architecture blends with socialist monuments, ancient temples sit beside motorbike chaos, and street food stalls feed millions from sidewalk seats. The Old Quarter pulses with energy and chaos; the atmosphere is authentically Vietnamese.

What makes it special: Colonial French influence, ancient temples, street food capital of Vietnam, motorbike culture, Hoan Kiem Lake, authentic Old Quarter energy.

Best for: First-time Vietnam visitors, foodies, culture explorers, photographers of street life, those seeking authentic chaos.

Must-see attractions: Hoan Kiem Lake, Temple of Literature, Ho Chi Minh Mausoleum, Old Quarter (36 streets, temples, street food), Thang Long Water Puppet Theater (Vietnamese traditional art form).

Budget: ₫300,000-600,000/day (accommodations ₫100,000-300,000, meals ₫50,000-150,000).

Local vibe: Chaotic, energetic, authentic, crowded with motorbikes, spiritual temples hidden in busy streets, genuine local feel.

Getting there: Noi Bai Airport (30km north). Airport bus to city center (₫30,000, 45 mins) or taxi (₫300,000-400,000).

Best time to visit: October-November or March-April (ideal weather). December-January coolest, February-March warming up.

Halong Bay: UNESCO Heritage Wonder

Halong Bay is one of the world's most spectacular natural sites—a UNESCO World Heritage Area of thousands of limestone karsts jutting from emerald waters. Cruising through the bay, exploring caves, and waking to sunrise over the karsts is magical and unforgettable.

What makes it special: UNESCO World Heritage Site, dramatic limestone karsts, emerald waters, sunrise cruises, cave exploration, natural beauty unmatched in Vietnam.

Best for: Nature lovers, photographers, cruise enthusiasts, those seeking natural beauty, bucket-list travelers.

Must-see attractions: Halong Bay boat cruise, Sung Sot Cave (Surprise Cave), Ti Top Island, Dau Go Cave, sunrise over karsts, swimming in the bay.

Budget: ₫800,000-2,000,000/day (2-3 day cruises ₫1,500,000-3,000,000 including meals and lodging).

Local vibe: Touristic but spectacular, floating village communities, peaceful mornings, scenic beauty everywhere.

Getting there: From Hanoi by bus or train (3-4 hours, ₫200,000-400,000). Arrange cruise from Hanoi or at Halong Bay port.

Best time to visit: October-April (calm waters, clear skies). Avoid May-September (monsoon, rough seas, poor visibility).

Ho Chi Minh City: Modern South Vietnam

Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) is Vietnam's vibrant south—a modern, dynamic metropolis of skyscrapers, street energy, war museums, and fast-paced life. It's less touristy than Hanoi, more upbeat, and the gateway to the Mekong Delta.

What makes it special: Modern Vietnamese energy, war history sites, street food scene, nightlife, less touristy than Hanoi, cosmopolitan vibe.

Best for: History enthusiasts (war museums), nightlife lovers, modern city explorers, those wanting post-war Vietnam history.

Must-see attractions: War Remnants Museum, Reunification Palace, Ben Thanh Market, Bitexco Financial Tower (views), Nguyen Hue Walking Street, Jade Emperor Pagoda.

Budget: ₫350,000-700,000/day (accommodations ₫120,000-400,000, meals ₫80,000-200,000).

Local vibe: Fast-paced, modern, entrepreneurial, less touristic than Hanoi, genuinely Vietnamese hustle.

Getting there: Tan Son Nhat Airport (7km from city). Airport bus to city (₫20,000, 45 mins) or taxi (₫300,000-400,000).

Best time to visit: November-April (dry season, comfortable). May-October hot and humid.

Hoi An: The Ancient Town & Coastal Gem

Hoi An is Vietnam's most charming town—a perfectly preserved ancient trading port on the coast with colorful shophouses, lantern-lit streets, tailor tradition, and nearby beaches. It's peaceful, romantic, and a welcome respite from Vietnam's chaotic cities.

What makes it special: Ancient architecture (15th-19th centuries), lantern-lit streets, tailor culture, coastal location, romantic atmosphere, pristine beaches nearby.

Best for: Couples, photographers, culture lovers, those seeking slower pace, tailor enthusiasts (bespoke clothing).

Must-see attractions: Ancient Town (UNESCO site), Japanese Bridge, lantern-lit streets, Cua Dai Beach, An Bang Beach, tailor shops, local cooking classes.

Budget: ₫300,000-500,000/day (accommodations ₫100,000-250,000, meals ₫50,000-150,000).

Local vibe: Romantic, peaceful, preserved-in-time feeling, genuinely welcoming locals, slow pace, artistic.

Getting there: From Hanoi by train or flight (20-22 hour train, ₫300,000; 2-hour flight ₫500,000-1,500,000). 30km from Da Nang airport.

Best time to visit: October-April (dry, comfortable). Avoid May-September (monsoon, humidity).

Getting Around Vietnam

Sleeper Trains - The Local Way

Hanoi to Ho Chi Minh City: Overnight train journey (30-33 hours). Cost: VND 400,000-1,200,000 depending on class (hard sleeper to soft sleeper). Saves accommodation cost. Popular with backpackers. Book at Hanoi Central Station or online.

Advantages: Scenic journey, saves hotel night, unique experience, meets other travelers. Disadvantages: Long journey, basic amenities, noise at night.

Domestic Flights

Fast but Less Scenic: Hanoi to Da Nang (1 hour), Da Nang to Ho Chi Minh City (1.5 hours). Cost: VND 800,000-2,000,000 depending on airline (budget carriers like VietJet, AirAsia).

Tip: Often cheaper to combine sleeper train + domestic flight than flying both segments. Book 2-3 weeks in advance for best prices.

Long-Distance Buses

Network: Extensive bus routes connecting all cities and towns. Cheap but slow. VIP buses available (sleeper bunk beds).

Cost: Hanoi to Halong Bay (3-4 hours): VND 150,000-250,000. Hanoi to Da Nang (12-14 hours): VND 300,000-500,000.

Popular Routes: Open-tour buses (Hanoi-HCMC) specifically designed for backpackers, stopping in Halong, Hoi An, Da Nang. Cost: VND 600,000-800,000 for entire trip.

Local Transport in Cities

  • Hanoi/HCMC Taxis: Metered, reliable, inexpensive. Flag drop: VND 10,000-12,000 + VND 12,000-14,000 per km. Use reputable companies (Vinasun, Mai Linh) to avoid overcharging.
  • Grab (Ride-Share): Available in major cities, cheaper than taxis, app-based. Surge pricing during peak hours.
  • Motorbike Taxis (Xe Om) - Quick for short distances, risky. Cost: VND 20,000-50,000 per journey. Helmet legally required.
  • Buses: Extensive city bus networks (cheap VND 5,000-10,000 per ride) but slow and crowded. Useful for budget travelers.
  • Cyclos: Pedal-powered tricycles in Hanoi and Hoi An. Slow but scenic and iconic. Cost: VND 50,000-100,000 per journey (negotiate price).

Halong Bay & Cruises

Overnight Cruises: UNESCO limestone karsts explored by multi-deck junks. Cost: VND 1,500,000-3,500,000 per person (budget to mid-range). Includes accommodation, meals, guides. Book through hotels or travel agencies.

Day Cruises: Budget option if overnight is too expensive. Cost: VND 800,000-1,500,000. Less immersive but saves accommodation cost.

Money-Saving Tips

  • Take overnight sleeper trains to save hotel cost
  • Open-tour buses offer best value for covering long distances cheaply
  • Use Grab instead of metered taxis for accurate, transparent pricing
  • Book long-distance transport 2-3 days in advance for discounts
  • Walk within cities—Hanoi and Hoi An are pedestrian-friendly with surprises in alleys
  • Fly budget airlines between distant cities (often cheaper than sleeper train + hotel combined)

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: VND 150,000-400,000/night
  • Food: VND 100,000-300,000/day
  • Transport: VND 50,000-150,000/day
  • Activities: VND 200,000-600,000/day

Visa & Entry Requirements

For Indian Passport Holders: E-visa required (easy online process, ~24-48 hours approval). Cost: approximately USD 20-25 (varies by service provider, typically VND 500,000-600,000). Alternatively, visa on arrival available in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City (slightly more expensive, slightly slower). Official source: Vietnam E-Visa Portal Last verified: July 2026.

Requirements: Valid passport (6+ months validity), return flight ticket, proof of funds.

Travel Insurance: Highly recommended. Cost: VND 300,000-700,000 for 7-10 days. Covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost luggage.

Currency & Money

Currency: Vietnamese Dong (VND). Exchange rates: 1 USD ≈ VND 24,000-25,000. ATMs widely available (BIDV, Vietcombank). Accept Visa/Mastercard but charge VND 20,000-50,000 fee per withdrawal.

Practical: Carry VND cash for street food and small vendors. Credit cards accepted in hotels, malls, upscale restaurants. Get a SIM card at airport (VND 300,000-500,000 for 8-10 days unlimited data).

Highlights
