Why Visit Georgia?

Georgia packs snowy Caucasus peaks, hilltop monasteries, 8,000 years of winemaking and one of the friendliest food cultures in the world into a small, affordable country. Direct flights from Delhi take about five hours, and many Indian couples now pick it over Europe for honeymoons and snow trips.

Most first trips combine the old streets and sulphur baths of Tbilisi, the mountain views around Kazbegi and Gudauri, and the wine villages of Kakheti, with the Black Sea resort of Batumi on longer trips.

Georgia Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Tbilisi Old Town & Narikala

Morning
Arrive and check in around the Old Town or Rustaveli Avenue.
Afternoon
Ride the cable car up to Narikala Fortress and the Mother of Georgia statue, then walk down through the Old Town's wooden balconies.
Evening
Soak in a private room at the domed Abanotubani sulphur baths, then cross the lit-up Peace Bridge.
GEL 130–220/day
2

Mtskheta & Tbilisi's modern side

Morning
Day trip to Mtskheta, the old capital: Jvari Monastery on the hilltop and Svetitskhoveli Cathedral (UNESCO).
Afternoon
Back in Tbilisi, browse the Dry Bridge flea market and walk Rustaveli Avenue.
Evening
Supper at a supra-style restaurant, or a drink at the Fabrika hostel courtyard.
GEL 140–230/day
3

Kazbegi & the Georgian Military Road

Morning
Drive north past the Ananuri Fortress on its turquoise reservoir.
Afternoon
Stop at the Gudauri viewpoint (Friendship Monument; paragliding in season), then hike or ride a 4×4 to Gergeti Trinity Church under Mount Kazbek.
Evening
Return to Tbilisi, or stay overnight in Stepantsminda for sunrise views.
GEL 160–260/day
4

Kakheti wine country & Sighnaghi

5-day plan
Morning
Drive about 2 hours east to Kakheti, Georgia's wine region.
Afternoon
Visit a family winery to see wine made in clay qvevri jars, then explore hilltop Sighnaghi, the "city of love", and its town walls.
Evening
Sunset over the Alazani Valley; return to Tbilisi or stay in Sighnaghi.
GEL 150–250/day
5

Uplistsikhe & Tbilisi's neighbourhoods

5-day plan
Morning
Visit the ancient cave town of Uplistsikhe near Gori.
Afternoon
Explore Fabrika and the Sololaki district's painted entrance halls, or the Georgian National Museum.
Evening
Ride the funicular up Mtatsminda Park for night views over the city.
GEL 130–220/day
6

Train to Batumi on the Black Sea

7-day plan
Morning
Take the morning train from Tbilisi to Batumi (about 5 hours).
Afternoon
Walk the seaside boulevard and see the moving Ali and Nino statue.
Evening
Dinner by the Alphabetic Tower and the dancing fountains.
GEL 150–240/day (incl. travel)
7

Batumi & departure

7-day plan
Morning
Visit the Batumi Botanical Garden above the sea.
Afternoon
Take the Argo cable car for views, then a last Adjarian khachapuri.
Evening
Fly out from Batumi, or take the train back to Tbilisi for your flight.
GEL 120–200/day

Food to Try in Georgia

  • Khachapuri — Cheese-filled bread; the boat-shaped Adjarian version comes with butter and an egg in the middle.
  • Khinkali — Big twisted dumplings; eat them by hand and sip the broth first. Mushroom, potato and cheese fillings are vegetarian.
  • Badrijani nigvzit — Fried aubergine rolls filled with spiced walnut paste: vegetarian and delicious.
  • Lobio — Spiced red bean stew baked in a clay pot, often with cornbread (mchadi).
  • Pkhali — Minced spinach, beetroot or beans with walnuts and herbs.
  • Churchkhela — Walnuts dipped in thickened grape juice: the classic road-trip snack.

For Indian travellers: Georgia is easy for vegetarians thanks to its many walnut, bean and vegetable dishes (Orthodox fasting food). Tbilisi and Batumi have Indian restaurants too.

Best Time to Visit Georgia

Late spring (May–June): green valleys, wildflowers and pleasant city weather.

Autumn (September–October): the grape harvest (rtveli) in Kakheti and golden mountains: our favourite time.

Summer (July–August): hot in Tbilisi, ideal in the mountains and on the Black Sea.

Winter (December–March): snow and skiing at Gudauri, popular with Indian travellers; the Kazbegi road can close in heavy snow.

Getting Around Georgia

Flights from India: direct flights from Delhi take about 5 hours; from other cities, connect through the Gulf or Istanbul. Check current routes when you book.

In Tbilisi: the metro and buses are cheap with a Metromoney card or a contactless bank card. Bolt and Yandex Go taxis are inexpensive.

Day trips: shared tours and private drivers cover Kazbegi, Kakheti and Mtskheta. Minibuses (marshrutkas) are cheapest but less comfortable.

Tbilisi–Batumi: comfortable trains take about 5 hours; book a day or two ahead in summer.

Travel Essentials

Entry for Indians: Indians holding a valid visa or residence permit from the US, UK, a Schengen country and some other countries can usually enter Georgia visa-free; check the current list. Everyone else needs the e-visa from the official portal (evisa.gov.ge), which takes about a week; there's no visa on arrival. New 2026 rules have been reported on mandatory travel insurance. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you travel.

Money: Georgian lari (GEL). 1 GEL is about ₹31. Cards work widely in Tbilisi and Batumi; carry some cash for villages and markets.

Daily budget (per person): a mid-range hotel share, food, local transport and sightseeing come to about GEL 150–250 a day, plus day tours.

SIM card: Magti, Silknet and Cellfie sell tourist SIMs at the airport and in town with your passport.

Plugs: types C and F, 220V. Indian two-pin plugs usually fit; bring an adapter for three-pin chargers.

Etiquette and safety: Georgia is very safe and welcoming. Cover shoulders and knees in churches (women usually cover their hair; scarves are often provided). Don't try to visit the breakaway regions of Abkhazia or South Ossetia.

Georgia FAQs

How much does a Georgia trip cost per day?

Plan for roughly GEL 150–250 per person per day (about ₹4,700–7,800) for a mid-range trip, covering accommodation, food, local transport and sightseeing. Prices are approximate as of October 2026.

How many days do you need in Georgia?

3 days covers the highlights: Tbilisi Old Town & Narikala, Mtskheta & Tbilisi's modern side and Kazbegi & the Georgian Military Road. With 5 days you can add Kakheti wine country & Sighnaghi and Uplistsikhe & Tbilisi's neighbourhoods, and a full week adds Train to Batumi on the Black Sea and Batumi. Our 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries build on each other, so you can stop at any point.

When is the best time to visit Georgia?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct. Late spring (May–June): green valleys, wildflowers and pleasant city weather.
