Why Visit Australia?

Australia is the world's largest island-continent offering jaw-dropping natural wonders, world-class cities, and incredible wildlife. From the iconic Sydney Opera House to the turquoise waters of the Great Barrier Reef, ancient Aboriginal culture in the Outback, and Melbourne's vibrant laneways—Australia has something for every traveler. Perfect for nature lovers, beach enthusiasts, and adventure seekers.

Whether you're diving on the reef, hiking through rainforests, or exploring cosmopolitan cities, Australia delivers unforgettable experiences with friendly locals, stunning beaches, and some of the world's most unique animals.

1 Sydney: Opera House & Bondi Beach

Morning
Arrive at Sydney International Airport. Take train into the city (A$19.80, 13 mins). Check in and head to Circular Quay. Walk around Sydney Opera House, explore the forecourt (free). Take photos from different angles—no entry fee needed.
Afternoon
Walk to The Rocks (historic neighborhood) and Harbour Bridge. Have lunch at a local cafe (A$15-25). Relax at Bondi Beach or nearby Tamarama. Watch sunset over the water with a coffee (A$5-7).
Evening
Dinner at Chinatown or Darling Harbour (A$20-40). Walk along Barangaroo Reserve. Explore the vibrant neighborhoods and street art in Surry Hills or Newtown.
A$100-150/day

2 Blue Mountains & Scenic World

Early Morning
Take train from Central Station to Katoomba in the Blue Mountains (A$15.20 return, 2 hours). The blue haze from eucalyptus trees makes these mountains stunning. Pick up coffee from a local cafe (A$5).
Afternoon
Visit Scenic World (A$49): ride the Scenic Railway (steepest passenger railway), Cableway, and Railway. Walk Echo Point trail to see the Three Sisters rock formation (free). Explore Prince Henry Cliff Walk for panoramic views (free, 1-hour round trip).
Evening
Return to Sydney by train. Dinner in Surry Hills or inner-city neighborhood (A$25-40). Early night to recover from travel.
A$90-130/day

3 Manly Beach & Coastal Walk

Morning
Catch the ferry from Circular Quay to Manly (A$8.80, 30 mins, scenic route). Breakfast at one of the beachfront cafes (A$12-18). Relax on Manly Beach—perfect for swimming and sunbathing.
Afternoon
Walk the Manly to Shelly Beach coastal trail (10km, 3 hours). Spot sea cliffs, rock pools, and beaches. Stop at quieter beaches like Shelly (great snorkeling if you have gear). Lunch at a beachside cafe (A$15-25).
Evening
Take the ferry back to the city. Final dinner and explore more of Sydney's neighborhoods. Visit local pubs or night markets (A$20-40).
A$80-120/day

Australia Food Guide

Australian cuisine is diverse and fresh, featuring world-class seafood, indigenous ingredients, and multicultural influences. From coastal fish to hearty meat pies, coffee culture, and Asian fusion—Australia's food scene is excellent and surprisingly affordable.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Barramundi Fish – Grilled or pan-fried with lemon and herb. Cost: A$20-35
  • Chicken Parmigiana – Breaded chicken with tomato sauce and melted cheese. Cost: A$18-25
  • Lamingtons – Sponge cake coated in chocolate and coconut. Cost: A$4-6
  • Vegemite on Toast – Australian breakfast staple (acquired taste!). Cost: A$5-8
  • Meat Pie – Traditional Australian pastry with beef filling. Cost: A$6-10
  • Pavlova – Meringue dessert with cream and berries. Cost: A$8-12
  • Fresh Oysters – Especially in coastal areas like Sydney. Cost: A$3-5 each
  • Anzac Biscuits – Oat, coconut, and golden syrup cookies. Cost: A$4-6 per pack

Best Food Spots

  • Sydney Fish Market (Pyrmont) – Fresh seafood, lunch spots, sushi, oyster bars
  • Queen Victoria Market (Melbourne) – Produce, food stalls, international cuisine
  • Paddington Markets (Sydney Saturdays) – Local goods, street food, fresh produce
  • Chinatown (Sydney & Melbourne) – Authentic Asian cuisine, budget-friendly

Best Time to Visit Australia

Best overall: October-April (spring/summer, 25-30°C, perfect weather, fewer crowds than peak season)

Budget travelers: May-September (winter, cooler, 15-20°C, cheaper flights and accommodation)

Great Barrier Reef: June-October (dry season, calm seas, excellent visibility)

Avoid: December-January (peak season, higher prices, summer holidays, cyclone risk in northern areas)

Getting Around Australia

Public Transport (Best in Cities)

Sydney: Opal card for trains, buses, ferries. A$9.80 daily cap on weekdays (unlimited travel). Ferries are scenic and fun.

Melbourne: Myki card. A$12.20 daily cap. Trams are iconic and free within the CBD (central business district).

Domestic Flights

For long distances: Qantas, Virgin, or budget airlines like Jetstar. Book in advance for better rates (A$50-150 one-way).

Route examples: Sydney to Melbourne (A$60-100), Sydney to Cairns for reef access (A$100-180).

Rental Cars

Useful for: Great Barrier Reef drives, Outback exploration, smaller towns. A$40-80/day.

Note: Australians drive on the LEFT. Roads are generally excellent. Long distances between towns require planning.

Taxis & Rideshare

Uber available in major cities. Yellow Cabs in Sydney and Melbourne. Generally 20-30% more expensive than public transport.

Travel Essentials

Visa & Entry

For Indian passport holders: eVisitor (ETA) REQUIRED. Apply online at immi.homeaffairs.gov.au. Cost: A$20. Processing: Instant to a few hours. Valid for 12 months, allow multiple entries of up to 3 months each. Last verified: July 2026.

Currency & Money

Australian Dollar (A$). Exchange rate: 1 USD ≈ 1.50 A$.

ATMs: Widespread in cities and towns. Most accept Visa/Mastercard. No forex fees if using local bank ATMs.

Tipping: NOT expected. 10% optional in fine dining. Service staff are well-paid compared to other countries.

Budget Breakdown (per day, budget traveler)

  • Accommodation: A$40-80 (hostels, budget hotels, airbnb)
  • Food: A$30-50 (mix of cheap eats, cafes, occasional restaurants)
  • Transport: A$15-25 (public transport, some regional trains)
  • Activities: A$20-40 (museums, national parks, scenic experiences)
  • Total: A$105-195/day (~$70-130 USD)

Getting a SIM Card

Buy at Sydney Airport or any Woolworths/Coles supermarket. Prepaid plans: A$15-40 for 7-14 days unlimited data. Providers: Vodafone, Telstra, Optus.

Important Notes

  • Sunscreen is ESSENTIAL. UV index is very high. Pack SPF 50+.
  • Australia has strict biosecurity. Don't bring fresh food, plants, or animal products.
  • Debit cards work almost everywhere. Tipping is not a culture—don't feel obligated.
  • Check wildlife warnings before swimming (sharks, stingrays in some areas).
