Why Visit Australia?

Australia is a vast continent of natural wonders. Experience iconic Sydney, world-class diving at the Great Barrier Reef, ancient Uluru in the Outback, and vibrant Melbourne. With unique wildlife and diverse landscapes, Australia offers unforgettable adventures for 3-10 day trips.

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: AUD 50-120/night
  • Food: AUD 40-100/day
  • Transport: AUD 30-80/day (flights, trains)
  • Activities: AUD 50-150/day

Best Time to Visit

December-February is summer (hot), April-September is autumn/winter (best weather overall, particularly May-August). Avoid January-February heat in Outback.

Visa Requirements

Indian nationals need an eVisa (ETA). Processing is quick online. Valid for 1 year, multiple entries.

Highlights

Sydney: Opera House, Harbour Bridge, beaches. Great Barrier Reef: World's largest coral reef system. Uluru: Sacred red monolith, stunning sunsets. Melbourne: Art, coffee culture, street art.
