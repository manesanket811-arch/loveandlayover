Why Visit Bali?

Bali seamlessly blends spiritual tradition with tropical paradise. Explore ancient temples, rice paddies, and vibrant culture in Ubud, then relax on pristine beaches in Seminyak. World-class food, affordable prices, and warm hospitality make Bali perfect for 3-10 day adventures. Whether you seek yoga, adventure, culture, or beaches, Bali delivers unforgettable experiences on any budget.

Bali Food Guide

Balinese cuisine is aromatic, layered, and deeply connected to the island's Hindu-Buddhist heritage. Spices, coconut milk, fresh herbs, and rice form the foundation of every meal. Food is spiritual—prayers of gratitude precede dining.

Must-Try Balinese Dishes

  • Gado-Gado - Vegetable salad with peanut sauce. Cost: $1.50-3
  • Satay (Sate Lilit) - Grilled meat skewers with spice paste. Cost: $2-5
  • Nasi Goreng - Fried rice with egg, meat, vegetables. Cost: $2-4
  • Lumpia - Spring rolls, crispy and savory. Cost: $1-2
  • Lawar - Traditional mix of minced meat, coconut, spices (acquired taste). Cost: $3-5
  • Soto Ayam - Turmeric chicken soup, aromatic. Cost: $2-3

Seafood Specialties

  • Grilled Fish (Ikan Bakar) - Fresh catch with sambal. Cost: $5-8
  • Shrimp Paste Squid (Sambal Sotong) - Tender squid in spicy paste. Cost: $4-7
  • Tinutuan - Rice porridge with seafood/chicken. Cost: $2-4
  • Ceviche-style Fish - Fresh fish with lime and spice. Cost: $5-8

Street Food & Snacks

  • Pisang Goreng - Fried banana, often with chocolate. Cost: $0.50-1
  • Soto Banjar - Spicy beef soup, street vendor specialty. Cost: $1-2
  • Gorengan - Fried snacks (tofu, tempeh, veggies). Cost: $0.50-1.50
  • Jamu - Traditional herbal drink, believed to aid digestion. Cost: $0.50-1

Best Places to Eat

  • Ubud Market (Pasar Seni) - Fresh produce, local lunch spots $1.50-3
  • Warung (Small Local Restaurants) - Cheapest authentic food, $2-5 per meal
  • Seminyak Beachfront - Upscale dining, fresh seafood, $10-20
  • Cooking Classes (Ubud) - Learn to cook traditional dishes, $15-25
  • Sukawati Market - Night food stalls, local favorites, $1-3

Dining Tips

Eating with right hand is polite. Try "nasi campur" (mixed rice with sides) for variety and value. Avoid ice made from tap water in cheap stalls; bottled water recommended. Restaurants popular with locals are safest and cheapest. Balinese people are vegetarian-friendly; many dishes have vegetarian options.

Best Time to Visit Bali

Dry Season (April - October)

Weather: 22-28°C, sunny and dry. Crowds: Moderate to High.

Highlights: Clear skies, perfect beach weather, ideal for water sports and temple visits. Hiking Mount Batur clearest in early morning. Rice paddies green in June-July.

Costs: Mid to High. Hotels $30-80. Peak in June-August.

Best for: Beach lovers, water sports enthusiasts, those seeking guaranteed sunshine.

Rainy Season (November - March)

Weather: 25-32°C, humid with afternoon rain. Crowds: Low to Moderate.

Highlights: Lush green landscapes, fewer tourists, cheaper accommodation, occasional beach days (rain often brief). Yoga and wellness retreats thrive.

Costs: LOWEST of year. Hotels $15-40. 50% cheaper than dry season.

Best for: Budget travelers, yoga seekers, those avoiding crowds, January-February can still have good stretches.

Shoulder Seasons (March-April, September-October)

Weather: 24-30°C, mostly dry with occasional showers. Crowds: Moderate.

Highlights: Good balance of weather and prices. March sees surfing swells. October clear and sunny. Perfect compromise.

Costs: Moderate. Hotels $25-60. Good value.

Best for: Flexible travelers seeking good weather without peak season crowds or prices.

Recommendation

Best overall: April-May or September-October (dry, sunny, fewer crowds, moderate prices).

Budget travelers: November-February (lowest prices, still swimmable).

Beach lovers: June-August (warmest, clearest water).

Getting Around Bali

Scooter/Motorbike Rental

Most popular and cheapest option. Cost: $5-15/day. International Driving Permit or hire driver (recommended if unfamiliar with traffic). Drive left side. Traffic chaotic but manageable. Helmet mandatory. Insurance often included—verify with rental.

Hired Drivers

Very affordable and safe option. Cost: $25-50/day for private driver (8 hours). Available through hotels, travel agents, or Grab app. Drivers know routes, can help with cultural insights, no stress driving.

Taxis & Ride-Sharing

  • Grab/Gojek: App-based, safer than street taxis. Cost: $2-5 within Bali.
  • Blue Bird Taxis: Metered, reliable. Flag drop: $1 + $0.40/km
  • Negotiate upfront if using informal taxis

Public Buses (Bemos)

Cheap and adventurous. Cost: $0.50-2. No fixed schedules—depart when full. Routes can be unpredictable for tourists. Fun experience but time-consuming.

Inter-Island Transport

Fast Boats to Gili Islands: Seminyak to Gili Trawangan (2 hours, $30-40). Daily ferries. Book night before or early morning.

Domestic Flights: Bali to Lombok or Java islands with Garuda or Batik Air ($40-80, 1-2 hours).

Money-Saving Tips

  • Use Grab instead of street taxis (safer, cheaper)
  • Rent scooter only if confident rider
  • Hire driver for multiple destinations (often cheaper than taxis)
  • Walk in Ubud and Seminyak town centers

Travel Essentials

Budget Breakdown

  • Accommodation: $10-50/night (hostels $8-15, mid-range $25-40, hotels $50+)
  • Food: $8-20/day (street food $1-3, local warung $3-8, fine dining $15+)
  • Transport: $2-10/day (scooter $5-10/day, Grab $2-5 per ride, driver hire $25-50)
  • Activities: $5-20/day (temples $3-5, cooking classes $15-25, yoga $10-15)
  • Total daily budget: $15-28/day for budget travelers, $30-50 for mid-range

Visa & Entry Requirements

For Indian Passport Holders: Visa on Arrival (VOA) at airport. Cost: 250,000 IDR (~$15). Valid 30 days. Bring: Passport (6+ months validity), return flight, proof of funds, accommodation address. Can extend once for another month. Official source: Indonesian Immigration Office (Imigrasi) Last verified: July 2026.

US/UK/Australia/EU: Visa-free for 30 days (Visa Exemption).

Currency: Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). 1 USD ≈ 15,000-16,000 IDR. ATMs everywhere. Debit cards accepted in mid-range restaurants and hotels. Small warungs cash only.

Getting a SIM: Buy from Telkomsel, Indosat, or XL at airport (30,000-100,000 IDR for 7-10 days data).

Travel Insurance

Recommended for activities (surfing, diving, scooter riding). Cost: $0.50-1/day. Covers medical emergencies, trip cancellations, lost luggage, adventure activities.
