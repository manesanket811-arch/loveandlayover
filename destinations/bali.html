Why Visit Bali?

Bali is a tropical paradise offering ancient temples, stunning rice terraces, pristine beaches, and vibrant culture. From the spiritual heart of Ubud to coastal charm of Kuta and Seminyak, Bali delivers affordable luxury and authentic experiences. Perfect for beach lovers, culture seekers, and digital nomads.

Explore ornate temples like Tanah Lot perched on cliffs, trek through emerald rice fields, dive into crystal-clear waters, and immerse yourself in Balinese traditions. With exceptional hospitality and incredible value, Bali is one of Asia's most beloved destinations.

1 Ubud: Culture & Rice Terraces

Morning
Arrive at Denpasar Airport. Take shuttle to Ubud (IDR 400k, 1.5 hours). Check in. Visit Ubud Palace - free entry, beautiful courtyard. Walk Ubud Market for local crafts and souvenirs (IDR 50-150k for items).
Afternoon
Explore Tegallalang Rice Terraces (free, 10 mins from town). Walk through iconic emerald-green rice paddies. Grab lunch at local warung (IDR 40-60k). Relax at a cafe overlooking fields. Sunset visit to get best lighting.
Evening
Attend Balinese dance performance (IDR 100-150k) at Ubud Palace. Dinner at restaurant - try Nasi Campur, Gado-gado, or Indonesian curry (IDR 50-100k). Evening walk through Ubud streets.
IDR 750k-1M/day

2 Tanah Lot Temple & Beach Day

Early Morning
Wake before sunrise. Take organized tour or taxi to Tanah Lot (IDR 300k round trip). Watch sunrise from clifftop temples. The iconic Tanah Lot temple sits on volcanic rock in the seaâ€”stunning photography opportunity.
Afternoon
Explore nearby attractions: Batukaru Temple, Jatiluwih Rice Terraces (UNESCO site, free). Lunch at seaside warung (IDR 50-80k). Head to Kuta Beach for swimming and sunset. Relax on black sand beach, watch surfers.
Evening
Dinner at Kuta's beach clubs or traditional restaurants (IDR 80-150k). Watch sunset from beach. Explore Kuta nightlife or beach shacks. Return to Ubud or stay coastal.
IDR 800k-1.2M/day

3 Monkey Forest & Spiritual Sites

Morning
Visit Sacred Monkey Forest Sanctuary (IDR 100k). Walk through ancient temples with playful monkeys. Beautiful forested paths and peaceful atmosphere. Breakfast at Ubud cafe (IDR 50-80k). Keep bags secure from monkeys!
Afternoon
Visit Goa Gajah (Elephant Cave Temple) - ancient carvings (IDR 50k). Explore Tegenungan Waterfall (free). Visit local artisan studios and galleries. Lunch at family-run warung (IDR 40-60k).
Evening
Cooking class option (IDR 150-250k, 2-3 hours with meal) or sunset yoga (IDR 100-150k). Final dinner at rooftop restaurant (IDR 80-150k). Pack for departure or extend trip.
IDR 700k-1M/day

Bali Food Guide

Balinese cuisine is flavorful, spiced, and uses fresh local ingredients. Food is incredibly cheap and delicious. Street food and warungs offer authentic experiences at budget prices.

Best Time to Visit Bali

Best overall: April-October (dry season, 25-28°C, sunny, perfect for beaches and temples). This is peak season with most tourists, but the weather is ideal for all activities.

Budget travelers: June-August (high season but stable weather, excellent for hiking and water sports). Prices slightly higher but infrastructure is optimal.

Low season bargains: January-March (rainy but fewer tourists, hotels offer 30-50% discounts). Still possible to visit with rain jackets and flexibility.

Avoid: November-March (monsoon/rainy season, flooding in some areas, lower diving visibility, cyclone risk in northern islands)

Shoulder season: March-April, September-October (fewer crowds than peak, good weather, cheaper flights and accommodation than June-August)

Rainfall patterns: October-April is wet; May-September is dry. Morning rain in wet season usually clears by afternoon.

Getting Around Bali

Taxis & Ride-share

Gojek & Grab: App-based services. Cheap: IDR 20-60k per ride within cities.

Traditional Taxi (Blue Bird): Metered, reliable. IDR 7k start + IDR 4k per km.

Organized Tours

For temples, rice terraces: IDR 300-500k for full-day private tours. Usually includes hotels, sites, meals, driver.

Rental Motorbikes

Popular in Bali. IDR 60-100k/day. Requires international driving permit. Wear helmetâ€”mandatory and accident risk is real.

Buses & Public Transport

Cheap buses connect main towns (IDR 20-50k). Local buses are very cheap (IDR 10-30k) but slow.

Travel Essentials

Visa & Entry

For Indian passport holders: On-Arrival Visa (VOA) available. Cost: IDR 500k (~USD 30). Passport valid 6+ months required. Visa valid 30 days. No exit visa needed. Last verified: July 2026.

ATMs: Widespread in cities. Fee: IDR 20-30k per transaction.

Credit Cards: Accepted in hotels and restaurants but not at small warungs. Use cash for meals.

Tipping: NOT expected. Service charge (10-15%) often included. Round up if you wish (optional).

Budget Breakdown (per day, budget traveler)

  • Accommodation: IDR 200-400k (budget hotels, hostels, guesthouses)
  • Food: IDR 150-300k (warungs, street food, occasional nicer meals)
  • Transport: IDR 50-100k (Gojek, taxis, buses)
  • Activities: IDR 100-250k (temples, tours, experiences)
  • Total: IDR 600k-1.2M/day (~$40-80 USD)

Getting a SIM Card

Buy at airport or convenience stores (Indomaret). Providers: Telkomsel, Indosat, XL Axiata. 7-day plans: IDR 100-200k.
