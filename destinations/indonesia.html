Why Visit Indonesia?

Indonesia is the world's largest archipelago with over 17,000 islands. From Borobudur's ancient Buddhist temples to Yogyakarta's cultural richness and Jakarta's modern energy, Indonesia showcases Asia's spiritual and contemporary sides. Explore UNESCO World Heritage sites, trek volcanic landscapes, and experience authentic Indonesian hospitality.

Indonesia Itineraries: 3, 5 or 7 Days

Pick 3, 5 or 7 days. Each longer plan builds on the shorter one: the first days stay the same and the extra days add more places. Costs are approximate per person per day; prices and timings change, so check before you book.

1

Yogyakarta: City & Old Town

Morning
Arrive at Yogyakarta Airport. Taxi to city center (IDR 150k). Explore Malioboro Street tourist hub with shops and street food (IDR 30-50k). Visit Kraton Palace - Sultan's residence, free/IDR 50k.
Afternoon
Explore Water Castle (Taman Sari) - IDR 80k. Grab lunch at local restaurant (IDR 40-80k). Walk traditional neighborhoods.
Evening
Attend Ramayana Ballet performance (IDR 150-250k). Traditional puppet shadow play (Wayang). Dinner at street vendors (IDR 50-100k).
IDR 700k-1M/day
2

Borobudur Sunrise & Temples

Pre-dawn
Wake 3:30 AM. Organized tour to Borobudur (IDR 400k, 1 hour). Hike for sunrise viewing over one of world's largest Buddhist temples.
Afternoon
Explore Borobudur complex (IDR 400k) - Buddha statues, stone carvings, five terraces. Visit Mendut and Pawon temples. Lunch at warung (IDR 40-60k).
Evening
Return to city. Visit batik workshops - watch artisans create patterns. Batik-making class optional (IDR 100-200k). Dinner (IDR 60-100k).
IDR 900k-1.3M/day
3

Mount Merapi & Villages

Early Morning
Guided Mount Merapi volcanic trek (IDR 300-400k with guide). Hike black sand landscape with crater views. 2-3 hour trek. Coffee break (IDR 20k).
Afternoon
Visit traditional Javanese village. Meet locals, see traditional homes. Lunch with family (IDR 40-70k). Learn about agriculture - rice paddies, coconut trees.
Evening
Return to city. Visit Beringharjo Market for crafts and souvenirs. Dinner at restaurant (IDR 60-100k).
IDR 750k-1M/day
4

Prambanan & Ratu Boko

5-day plan
Morning
Visit Prambanan, the largest Hindu temple complex in Indonesia, about 40 minutes from Yogyakarta. Go early to beat the heat.
Afternoon
Explore the smaller Sewu and Plaosan temples nearby, then relax at your hotel.
Evening
Watch the sunset from the hilltop Ratu Boko ruins, or see the open-air Ramayana Ballet at Prambanan if you missed it earlier (seasonal schedule).
IDR 800k–1.2M/day
5

Jomblang Cave & the south coast

5-day plan
Morning
Descend by rope into Jomblang Cave with a guided group to see the "light from heaven" beam around midday (book ahead; limited daily spots).
Afternoon
Drive to the beaches of the Gunungkidul coast such as Timang or Indrayanti.
Evening
Return to Yogyakarta and have dinner at an angkringan, a cheap street-side food cart.
IDR 900k–1.5M/day
6

Travel to Mount Bromo

7-day plan
Morning
Take a train from Yogyakarta to Malang or Probolinggo (about 6–9 hours), or fly to Surabaya and drive.
Afternoon
Enjoy Java's countryside from the train. Book ahead and bring snacks.
Evening
Overnight near Bromo (Cemoro Lawang) or in Malang, with a very early start the next day. It gets cold at night, so pack a warm layer.
IDR 600k–1M/day
7

Bromo sunrise

7-day plan
Early Morning
Jeep tour around 3 AM to a viewpoint such as Penanjakan for sunrise over Mount Bromo and the Tengger caldera.
Afternoon
Cross the "sea of sand" and climb the steps to the Bromo crater rim, then head back to Malang or Surabaya.
Evening
Fly home from Surabaya or onward to Bali. Check volcano activity alerts before you go.
IDR 900k–1.5M/day

Indonesia Food Guide

Indonesian cuisine is diverse and uses coconut, chilies, turmeric, and ginger. Each region has unique specialties. Food is affordable at local warungs.

Must-Try Dishes

  • Rendang – Slow-cooked meat in coconut and spice paste. Cost: IDR 60-120k
  • Nasi Kuning – Yellow rice with coconut and turmeric. Cost: IDR 40-70k
  • Perkedel – Fried potato croquettes. Cost: IDR 15-30k
  • Soto Betawi – Jakarta beef soup. Cost: IDR 40-80k
  • Bakso – Meatball soup. Cost: IDR 30-60k
  • Tahu Goreng – Fried tofu. Cost: IDR 10-20k
  • Lumpia – Spring rolls. Cost: IDR 20-40k
  • Pisang Goreng – Fried banana with chocolate. Cost: IDR 10-20k

Getting Around Indonesia

Domestic Flights

Best for island hopping. Garuda, Lion Air, Batik Air. Book in advance. IDR 200-600k one-way.

Buses & Taxis

Long-distance buses connect cities (IDR 100-300k). Gojek and Grab in major cities (IDR 20-60k).

Organized Tours

Full-day private tours: IDR 400-600k including transportation and guide.

Travel Essentials

Visa & Entry

Visa for Indians: a Visa on Arrival (about IDR 500,000, 30 days, extendable once) is available for tourism, or buy it in advance online as an e-VoA on the official Indonesian immigration website. If you visit Bali, there is also a tourist levy of IDR 150,000. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you apply.

Currency & Money

Indonesian Rupiah (IDR). Exchange rate: 1 USD ≈ 15,500 IDR. ATMs widespread in cities.

Budget Breakdown (per day, budget traveler)

  • Accommodation: IDR 200-400k (hostels, budget hotels)
  • Food: IDR 150-300k (warungs, street food)
  • Transport: IDR 50-100k (local taxis)
  • Activities: IDR 150-300k (temples, tours)
  • Total: IDR 650k-1.2M/day (~$42-78 USD)

Important Notes

  • Sunscreen is essential - extreme tropical UV.
  • Respect mosque customs - remove shoes, cover shoulders/knees.
  • Indonesia mostly Muslim - avoid eating/drinking in public during Ramadan.
  • Tap water not potable - drink bottled water.
  • Volcanoes are active - check status before trekking.

Indonesia FAQs

Do Indians need a visa for Indonesia?

A Visa on Arrival (about IDR 500,000, 30 days, extendable once) is available for tourism, or buy it in advance online as an e-VoA on the official Indonesian immigration website. If you visit Bali, there is also a tourist levy of IDR 150,000. Your passport must be valid for at least 6 months. Rules as of October 2026; always check the official website before you apply.

How much does a Indonesia trip cost per day?

Plan for roughly IDR 600k-1M per person per day (about ₹3,200–5,300) for a mid-range trip, covering accommodation, food, local transport and sightseeing. Prices are approximate as of October 2026.

How many days do you need in Indonesia?

3 days covers the highlights: Yogyakarta: City & Old Town, Borobudur Sunrise & Temples and Mount Merapi & Villages. With 5 days you can add Prambanan & Ratu Boko and Jomblang Cave & the south coast, and a full week adds Travel to Mount Bromo and Bromo sunrise. Our 3, 5 and 7-day itineraries build on each other, so you can stop at any point.

When is the best time to visit Indonesia?

Apr-Oct.
