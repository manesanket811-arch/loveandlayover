Things to do in Singapore in November 2026

Here's what to expect in Singapore in November: the weather, the festivals that usually happen and our tips. We're confirming this year's exact event dates and will add them here soon.

Event dates coming soonBy Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
This year's event dates are on the way. We research each month's events before it starts. Meanwhile, here's what usually happens in November.

Weather in November

Start of the northeast monsoon: 24–31°C, wetter, with frequent showers.

What usually happens in November

  • Deepavali in some years
  • Orchard Road Christmas light-up begins in mid to late November
  • Singapore school holidays begin in late November

Tips for November

  • Year-end sales start
  • Book December trips and tickets early

Coming up in December 2026

  • 17 Nov – 3 Jan: Orchard Road Christmas Light-Up
  • 27 Nov – 3 Jan: Christmas Wonderland
  • 27 Nov – 3 Jan: Season of Joy, A Universal Christmas and Christmas Glowcean
  • 31 Dec: ONE Countdown 2027 and Marina Bay fireworks
