Schools for Indian Kids in Singapore

CBSE and IB schools, international school fees, the AEIS route into local schools, and our tips.

8 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Zairon, CC BY-SA 4.0

For Indian families moving to Singapore, school is often the biggest decision and the biggest cost. Here are the options, what they cost, and what to think about before you accept a job offer.

As of November 2026Rules change, and this is a general guide from our experience, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Always confirm with the schools and the Ministry of Education (MOE) for your own case.

Your three main options

Fees vary by grade and change every year, so check each school's current fee schedule, and remember application, enrolment and building fees on top.

Indian-curriculum schools

Several international schools offer CBSE alongside IB or Cambridge, which makes it easy to move back to India later. They're popular, so apply early, check waitlists, and look at the campus location: the bus ride matters with Singapore's long school days. Visit or attend an online open day before deciding.

Local schools: the AEIS route

  • Foreign children usually enter local primary and secondary schools through MOE's Admissions Exercise for International Students (AEIS), a test in English and Maths held once a year, with a supplementary round (S-AEIS) for some levels.
  • Places are limited and not guaranteed; you'll be offered a school based on results and vacancies, not necessarily near your home.
  • The school year starts in January, and the AEIS calendar is fixed, so check MOE's dates well before you move.
  • Indian languages: Tamil is taught as a Mother Tongue, and other Indian languages such as Hindi, Gujarati, Punjabi, Bengali and Urdu are offered through approved centres.

Preschool and childcare

Preschools and childcare centres are widely available, with monthly fees that vary a lot by provider and hours. Many Indian families use centres close to home or the office; visit a few and check waitlists early.

Our tips

  • Factor school fees into your salary. Two children at international schools can cost as much as rent. See what's a good salary in Singapore.
  • Negotiate an education allowance if you're a senior hire; some employers offer it.
  • Choose home and school together: a short commute makes a big difference to family life.
  • Start early: popular schools fill up, especially for August and January intakes.

Your children will need a Dependant's Pass first. For the whole move, see our complete guide to moving to Singapore from India.

FAQs

Are there CBSE schools in Singapore?

Yes. Several international schools offer CBSE, often alongside IB or Cambridge, including GIIS, DPS International and NPS International.

How much are international school fees in Singapore?

Indian-curriculum international schools often cost S$20,000–40,000+ a year, and other international schools S$30,000–50,000+, depending on the grade. Check each school's current fees.

Can foreign children study in Singapore government schools?

Yes, if places are available. Most enter through MOE's AEIS test in English and Maths. Fees for international students are higher than for citizens but much lower than international schools.
