Christmas and New Year in Singapore 2026: What's On

Christmas Wonderland, the countdown fireworks and the best of December in Singapore, with dates, tickets and tips from locals.

News · Events5 min readUpdated 26 November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Mustang Joe, CC0

December is one of the most fun months to be in Singapore: Christmas lights, festive markets, concerts and New Year fireworks over Marina Bay. Here's what's on this year, with dates and tips.

Updated 26 November 2026Dates and prices are from organisers' announcements and news reports. Check the official event pages before you go, as details can change.

Christmas Wonderland at Gardens by the Bay

Christmas Wonderland 2026 27 November 2026 – 3 January 2027

Gardens by the Bay's festive carnival is back, with light displays, a Santa's Mission Control, a 20-metre elf treehouse, rides and festive food. Advance adult tickets start at about S$9 on off-peak dates (27 November–3 December and 7–10 December) and about S$13 on super-peak dates (4–6, 11–13 and 18–27 December).

New Year's Eve 2026: countdown to 2027

  • Marina Bay: the ONE Countdown 2027 fireworks at Marina Bay are a four-part display starting at 10.30pm and building up to midnight on Thursday 31 December 2026.
  • Other countdown sites: celebrations are planned across the city and in heartland neighbourhoods, including The Kallang, Sentosa's Siloso Beach and Clarke Quay.
  • Tips: arrive early for a spot around the bay, expect crowds and road and MRT changes near Marina Bay, and plan your way home before midnight.

More in December

  • Orchard Road Christmas lights: the annual light-up turns Singapore's shopping street into a festive walk; best after dark.
  • Singapore Comic Con: 4–6 December 2026.
  • PGL CS2 Major: Counter-Strike 2 playoffs at the Singapore Indoor Stadium, 10–13 December 2026.
  • Festive markets and malls: most malls have Christmas decorations, markets and sales through December.

Tips for December in Singapore

  • Weather: it's the rainy season, so carry an umbrella; showers usually pass quickly.
  • Book ahead: hotels, attraction tickets and Christmas dinners fill up, especially around the 25th and 31st.
  • School holidays: attractions are busier, so go early in the day or on weekdays.
  • Heading to Malaysia after Christmas? Visa-free entry for Indians ends on 31 December 2026; see our Malaysia visa guide.

FAQs

When is Christmas Wonderland 2026 in Singapore?

27 November 2026 to 3 January 2027 at Gardens by the Bay. Advance adult tickets start at about S$9 on off-peak dates and about S$13 on super-peak dates.

What time are the New Year fireworks at Marina Bay?

The ONE Countdown 2027 fireworks at Marina Bay are a four-part display starting at 10.30pm and building to midnight on 31 December 2026.

Yes, for festive lights, events and the New Year fireworks, though it's rainy and busy. Book hotels and tickets early.
