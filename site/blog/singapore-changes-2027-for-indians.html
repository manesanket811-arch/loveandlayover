What's Changing in 2027 for Indians in Singapore

Work pass salaries, a new tech pass, the Malaysia visa and a new border train: every change in one place, with what to do about each.

News · Visas & passes6 min readUpdated 26 November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Basile Morin, CC BY-SA 4.0

2027 brings a cluster of changes that affect Indians working in, moving to or visiting Singapore. Here's every one in one place, with the dates and what you should do now.

Updated 26 November 2026Based on announcements by MOM, the Immigration Department of Malaysia, LTA and EDB. Always confirm details on the official websites.

1. Work pass salaries go up

The Employment Pass minimum qualifying salary rises from S$5,600 to S$6,000 for new applications from 1 January 2027, reaching S$11,500 for applicants aged 45 and above (S$12,700 in financial services). The S Pass minimum rises from S$3,300 to S$3,600. COMPASS salary benchmarks are also updated.

What to do: if you have an offer close to the current minimum, ask your employer to submit the EP application before 31 December 2026. Current pass holders have until renewals of passes expiring from 1 January 2028. Full details in our guide to getting a job in Singapore, and you can check an offer against both tables with our salary & EP calculator.

2. Malaysia visa for Indians

From 1 January 2027, Indian passport holders need a visa approved before arrival in Malaysia. Applications open on the official MyVISA portal on 1 December 2026. This affects holidays and also quick trips to Johor Bahru from Singapore.

What to do: plan December trips while they're still visa-free, and apply early for 2027 travel. Read our Malaysia visa guide for 2027.

3. ONE Pass AI and Tech track

A new ONE Pass (AI and Tech) track opens to applications from 28 January 2027, and the Tech.Pass stops taking new and renewal applications from that date. It's aimed at senior tech leaders and founders with very high salaries or strong track records. Existing Tech.Pass holders keep their pass until it expires.

4. The RTS Link train to JB

The 5-minute train between Woodlands North and Bukit Chagar in JB is targeted to open in February 2027, with immigration for both countries at the departure station. Fares haven't been announced. More in our RTS Link guide.

Already in effect: rules to know

  • Vaping: since 1 May 2026, users face fines of up to S$10,000. Don't bring a vape into Singapore. See our rules and fines guide.
  • S Pass levy: S$650 a month since September 2025.

Your 2027 checklist

  1. Job offer near S$6,000? Get the EP filed before 31 December.
  2. Planning Malaysia or JB in 2027? Apply on MyVISA from 1 December.
  3. Renewing your pass in 2028? Check that your salary meets the new table for your age.
  4. Converting your driving licence? Book the Basic Theory Test before 13 March to pay the lower fee.

We'll keep this page updated as dates and details are confirmed. For all the latest, see Singapore news.

FAQs

What is the Employment Pass salary in 2027?

From 1 January 2027, the minimum is S$6,000 a month for new applications (S$6,600 in financial services), rising with age to S$11,500 (S$12,700) for applicants aged 45 and above.

Do Indians need a visa for Malaysia in 2027?

Yes. From 1 January 2027, Indian passport holders need a Malaysia visa approved before arrival, applied for on the MyVISA portal from 1 December 2026.

When does the ONE Pass AI and Tech track open?

Applications open from 28 January 2027, when the Tech.Pass stops accepting new and renewal applications.
