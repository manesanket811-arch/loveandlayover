India to Singapore Flights: Flight Time, Direct Routes & Cheapest Months

How long the flight is from your city, what a return ticket typically costs, which airlines fly direct and how to book it cheaper.

8 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore

How long is the flight from India to Singapore? About 4 hours from Chennai, Kolkata, Kochi and Bengaluru and about 5½ hours from Delhi and Mumbai, with direct flights from every major metro and a time difference of just 2½ hours. Below: flight time and distance from your city, which cities fly direct, the cheapest months and days to fly, what tickets cost and how to plan your first day.

Cities in the south and east, like Chennai, Kolkata and Tiruchirappalli, are closest, which is why their fares are often lower. From cities without a direct flight, such as many in the north and west, you'll usually connect through Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru or Chennai, or via Kuala Lumpur or Bangkok.

Direct flights to Singapore from India

All the big metros have non-stop flights to Singapore: Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Hyderabad and Kochi, usually several a day. Some smaller cities, especially in the south, have direct flights on certain days too.

  • Direct or one-stop? A direct flight is 4 to 6 hours. A one-stop route via Kuala Lumpur, Bangkok or an Indian metro can take 8 to 14 hours door to door, but is sometimes much cheaper in peak season.
  • From a city without a direct flight, compare a cheap domestic flight to Chennai, Bengaluru or Kolkata plus a direct flight, against a single one-stop ticket. Book them on one ticket where you can, so a delay doesn't strand you.
  • Airlines on these routes include Singapore Airlines, its low-cost arm Scoot, Air India and IndiGo. Search your exact route to see who flies from your city and on which days.

Baggage: check before you book

Full-service tickets usually include checked bags and meals. Low-cost base fares often include only a small cabin bag of around 7–10 kg, with checked bags, seats and meals extra. Check the allowance on your fare, and weigh your bags at home, especially on the way back with shopping.

Full-service or low-cost?

Low-cost fares look cheaper, but checked bags, seat selection and meals cost extra. A family with suitcases can end up paying close to a full-service fare. Always compare the final price with bags, not the headline fare.

  • Full-service (Singapore Airlines, Air India): baggage and meals included, more flexible tickets. Worth it for families and long overnight flights.
  • Low-cost (Scoot, IndiGo): great for couples and solo travellers with cabin bags only.

Overnight flights: how to plan day one

Many flights from Delhi and Mumbai leave around midnight and land in Singapore early in the morning, while hotel check-in is usually around 3 PM. Make the first day easy:

  • Freshen up and explore Jewel Changi (the Rain Vortex waterfall is free to see) before leaving the airport.
  • Leave your bags at the hotel and do something gentle: Gardens by the Bay's free outdoor gardens or a hawker breakfast.
  • Paying for early check-in is worth it with kids or elderly parents.
Before you flyIndian passport holders need a Singapore visa (see our visa guide), and every visitor must fill in the free SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before arrival.

How to find cheaper flights to Singapore

The cheapest fares to Singapore don't come from one magic website. They come from booking at the right time, watching for sales and comparing the full price. Here's what works for us.

1. Fly in the cheaper months

WhenFaresWhy
Late January to MarchUsually lowerAfter the New Year rush and before Indian summer holidays
July to September (outside festivals)Often good valueBetween summer holidays and the Diwali season
April to JuneHigherIndian school summer holidays
Diwali, and mid-December to early JanuaryHighestFestival travel, Christmas and New Year in Singapore

2. Book 6–8 weeks ahead

For off-season trips, 6–8 weeks ahead is usually the sweet spot. For Diwali, December and the summer holidays, book 3–4 months ahead, since fares climb as seats fill.

3. Watch for airline sales

Singapore Airlines, its low-cost arm Scoot, IndiGo and Air India run sales several times a year, often with big discounts for travel a few months later. Sign up for their newsletters and follow them on social media so you hear about sales first.

4. Set fare alerts and use flexible dates

  • Use the fare calendar or "flexible dates" view on Google Flights or Skyscanner to see the cheapest days in a month at a glance.
  • Set price alerts for your route and book when the fare drops into the usual range (see the fare table above).
  • Mid-week departures (Tuesday and Wednesday) are often cheaper than Fridays and Sundays.

5. Compare nearby airports

Fares from Chennai, Bengaluru, Kochi and Tiruchirappalli can differ a lot, and so can Delhi vs a nearby city. If you live between two airports, check both, including the cost of getting to the airport.

6. Compare the full price with bags

A low-cost fare plus checked bags, seat and meal can end up close to a full-service ticket. Always compare the final price for your luggage, especially for families.

7. Consider one-stop routes in peak season

Flights via Kuala Lumpur or Bangkok are sometimes much cheaper around holidays. Check the layover time and transit rules: from 1 January 2027, Indians need a visa to enter Malaysia, so avoid self-transfers in Kuala Lumpur that require passing immigration (see our Malaysia visa guide).

8. Use points and cards

Credit card and airline points go a long way on India–Singapore routes, and Singapore is a popular redemption route for Indian frequent flyers.

Our quick ruleIf an off-season return fare from your city is in the ₹18,000–28,000 range, it's a good deal: book it. Above ₹35,000 outside peak weeks, set an alert and wait a little.

From Changi Airport to the city

  • MRT: about S$2.50 and roughly 45 minutes to the centre. Tap in with a contactless card.
  • Grab or taxi: about S$25–35 to most central hotels, 20–30 minutes.
  • Private transfer: handy for families with lots of luggage; book in advance.

FAQs

Which Indian cities have direct flights to Singapore?

Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Kolkata, Hyderabad and Kochi all have non-stop flights to Singapore, usually several a day, and some smaller southern cities have direct flights on certain days. From other cities you connect through an Indian metro, Kuala Lumpur or Bangkok.

What is the cheapest day to fly from India to Singapore?

Mid-week departures, usually Tuesday and Wednesday, are often cheaper than Fridays and Sundays. Use the flexible-dates calendar on Google Flights or Skyscanner to see the cheapest days for your month.

What are the cheapest flights from India to Singapore?

Fares are usually lowest from Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Kochi, which are closest to Singapore. Booking 6–8 weeks ahead for an off-season, mid-week trip typically gets a return fare of about ₹18,000–28,000, often on a low-cost airline if you only need a cabin bag.

How far is Singapore from India?

Singapore is about 2,900 km from Chennai and Kolkata, about 3,900 km from Mumbai and about 4,160 km from Delhi by air. Direct flights take about 4 to 6 hours.

What is the distance from Lucknow to Singapore?

The air distance from Lucknow to Singapore is about 3,750 km (2,330 miles). Many travellers fly from Lucknow with a connection through a major Indian airport if there's no direct flight on their dates.

Where can I find cheap flight deals to Singapore from India?

Watch for airline sales from Singapore Airlines, Scoot, IndiGo and Air India, set fare alerts on Google Flights or Skyscanner, use flexible-date calendars, fly mid-week in late January to March or July to September, and book 6–8 weeks ahead.

How long is the flight from India to Singapore?

About 4 hours from Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kochi, and about 5½ to 6 hours from Mumbai and Delhi for direct flights.

What is the cheapest month to fly to Singapore from India?

Outside Indian school holidays and festival weeks, typically February to April and mid-July to September, avoiding long weekends. Booking 6 to 8 weeks ahead and flying mid-week helps most.

How much is a return flight to Singapore from India?

Roughly ₹18,000–28,000 booked ahead off-season, and ₹30,000–45,000 or more in peak weeks like Diwali, Christmas and summer holidays.

Which Indian cities have direct flights to Singapore?

All major metros, including Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Kochi, plus several other cities. Routes change, so check a flight search site for your city.

What is the time difference between India and Singapore?

Singapore is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of India.
