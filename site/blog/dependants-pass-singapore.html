Dependant's Pass in Singapore: The Complete Guide

Eligibility, the S$6,000 rule, documents, processing time and the honest truth about working on a DP.

8 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: kallerna, CC BY-SA 4.0

If you're moving to Singapore on an Employment Pass, the Dependant's Pass (DP) is how your spouse and children come with you. Here's who qualifies, how to apply, and the honest truth about working on a DP.

As of November 2026Rules change, and this is a general guide from our experience, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Always confirm with the Ministry of Manpower (MOM) for your own case.

Who can get a Dependant's Pass

  • Your legally married spouse
  • Your unmarried children under 21, including legally adopted children

The main pass holder must hold an Employment Pass or S Pass (or certain other passes) and earn a fixed monthly salary of at least S$6,000. Parents aren't eligible for a DP; see our guide to bringing parents to Singapore.

How to apply

  1. Your employer applies online, either with your Employment Pass application or later.
  2. Documents: passport copies, your marriage certificate (for your spouse) and birth certificates (for children). Documents not in English need official translations.
  3. Processing usually takes about three weeks, sometimes longer if MOM asks for more documents.
  4. After approval: your family enters Singapore with the approval letter, does any required registration, and receives their pass cards.
Get your documents ready in IndiaKeep originals and scans of your marriage certificate and your children's birth certificates. Getting them reissued or translated from Singapore is much slower.

How long the DP lasts

The DP is tied to the main pass. It usually lasts as long as your Employment Pass and is renewed with it. If your EP is cancelled, for example when you change jobs, your family's DPs are cancelled too, so your new employer needs to apply for them again.

Can you work on a Dependant's Pass?

Not on the DP itself. A DP holder who wants to work needs their own work pass, such as an Employment Pass, S Pass or Work Permit, applied for by an employer, and must meet the same salary and COMPASS rules as any foreign candidate. A Letter of Consent is only available in limited cases, such as DP holders who own and run a business here.

The honest reality

Finding a job on a DP is hard, and the reason is sponsorship. Many employers prefer candidates who don't need a work pass, and S Pass roles are limited by quotas. We cover what helps (starting early, targeting companies that sponsor, aiming for roles that clear the EP bar) in our job guide.

Life on a DP

  • Children can study at international schools or apply for local schools; see our schools guide for Indian kids.
  • Bank accounts, Singpass and phone plans are all available to DP holders.
  • Healthcare: check whether your employer's insurance covers your family, and buy cover if it doesn't.
  • Community: Indian associations, temple groups and Facebook groups for spouses are great for friendships and job leads.

FAQs

Who is eligible for a Dependant's Pass in Singapore?

The legally married spouse and unmarried children under 21 (including adopted children) of Employment Pass or S Pass holders earning a fixed monthly salary of at least S$6,000.

Can a Dependant's Pass holder work in Singapore?

Not on the DP itself. They need their own work pass, such as an EP or S Pass, applied for by an employer. A Letter of Consent is only for limited cases, such as DP holders who own a business.

How long does a Dependant's Pass take?

Usually about three weeks after the employer applies, but it can take longer if MOM needs more documents.

What happens to my family's DP if I change jobs?

The DP is tied to your Employment Pass. When your EP is cancelled, the DPs are too, so your new employer needs to apply for them again.
