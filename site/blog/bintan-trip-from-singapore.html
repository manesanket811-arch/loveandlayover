Bintan Trip from Singapore: Our Complete 2-Day Guide

Ferry, visa for Indians, where to stay, how to get around, what to do and what our weekend really cost.

9 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Jpatokal, CC BY-SA 4.0

Bintan is the easiest island escape from Singapore: one hour on a ferry and you're in a quieter world of beaches, mangroves and laid-back resorts. We spent a weekend there on 21–22 November 2026, and this guide has everything we learnt: the ferry, the visa for Indians, where we stayed, how we got around, what we did, what we ate and exactly what it cost.

Ferry from Singapore to Bintan

We took Bintan Resort Ferries from Tanah Merah Ferry Terminal to Bandar Bentan Telani (BBT), the terminal for the Lagoi resort area. The crossing took about an hour.

  • Price: our return ticket was about S$100 per person (about ₹7,000). Fares change with the day and time, and weekends cost more.
  • Where we booked: on Klook, which made it easy to compare sailings and get a mobile ticket.
  • Luggage: we didn't run into any strict limit for normal bags.
  • Food on board: no meal is included, but you can buy snacks and drinks.
  • Check-in: reach Tanah Merah well before departure and check the latest check-in cut-off on your ticket, as the operator closes check-in before sailing.
Watch the clockBintan is one hour behind Singapore. Ferry times from Bintan are printed in local time, so don't miss your return sailing by an hour.

Visa and arrival card for Indians

Indian passport holders can't enter Indonesia visa-free, but getting in is simple:

  • Visa on arrival (VOA): available at the ferry terminal for IDR 500,000 (about ₹2,600), valid for 30 days.
  • e-VOA: the same visa applied for online before you travel, so you skip the queue on arrival. Use the official Indonesian immigration website.
  • Passport: at least six months' validity from your arrival date.
  • Arrival card: since October 2025, everyone arriving at BBT must fill in the free All Indonesia digital arrival card online within 72 hours before arrival. It covers immigration, customs and health in one form. It's mandatory, so do it the night before.
  • If you live in Singapore: carry your Singapore pass (EP, S Pass, DP or other) along with your passport to come back in.

Where we stayed: Lagoi

We stayed in the Lagoi area in the north of the island, where most of Bintan's resorts are. We paid about ₹5,000 a night. The area is clean, green and quiet, Lagoi beach was close by, and it was always easy to find transport. For a first trip, Lagoi is the most convenient base: it's close to the ferry terminal, the beaches, the mangroves and the spa.

Getting around Bintan

We used Grab at first, from the ferry terminal to our stay. After that we hired the same driver directly, and he offered us a better rate than Grab. Our tip: if you find a good local driver, book them directly instead of Grab.

Our driver: JoeJoe was really nice and helpful. You can WhatsApp him on +62 821 7206 7061. He also does 4–5 hour packages to cover the places you want to see, which is the best way to visit the sand dunes and Tanjung Pinang. A 4–5 hour trip costs up to IDR 500,000 (about ₹2,600) for the car. Agree the places and the price before you set off.

Places we visited

1. Mangrove tour

A boat ride through Bintan's mangrove forest, past tangled roots and quiet river channels, with the chance to spot wildlife along the way. It was a really good experience and our favourite nature activity on the island. Tours start from about US$20 to US$28 per person (about ₹1,700–2,400) on booking sites, depending on length and whether it's a day or firefly tour.

2. Bintan Sand Dunes

Sandy dunes and small lakes left behind by old sand quarrying, now one of Bintan's most photographed spots. It feels like a mini desert in the middle of the island. Go early or late in the day, as it gets very hot and there's little shade.

3. Lagoi Bay

The lively part of the Lagoi resort area, with a walkable promenade, shops, cafés and the beach. It's a lovely place for a relaxed evening stroll and sunset.

4. 500 Lohan Temple

The Vihara Ksitigarbha Bodhisattva, better known as the 500 Lohan Temple, is near Tanjung Pinang in the south of the island, about an hour's drive from Lagoi. It's famous for its rows of 500 life-size statues of Buddhist saints, each with a different face and expression. We went with Joe as part of our taxi package. The published entry fee is about IDR 30,000 (around ₹160).

5. Spa at D'Bintan

We ended the trip with a massage at D'Bintan Salon Day Spa in Lagoi, and it was a wonderful experience, exactly what a relaxing island weekend needs. You can book a full-body massage at D'Bintan on Klook.

A 2-day Bintan itinerary

Here's how to fit it all into a weekend, based on our trip:

DayPlan
Day 1Morning ferry from Tanah Merah → Grab to your stay in Lagoi → beach time → mangrove tour → evening at Lagoi Bay → dinner at SPICE
Day 24–5 hour taxi package: Sand Dunes → 500 Lohan Temple near Tanjung Pinang → back to Lagoi → spa at D'Bintan → ferry back to Singapore

With an extra day, add a slower beach day, water sports or a firefly mangrove tour in the evening.

Food: what we ate

  • Local food: we tried nasi lemak (coconut rice with sides) and nasi goreng (fried rice), both easy wins if you're new to Indonesian food.
  • SPICE restaurant (Indian): our favourite. It's at the Mayang Sari Beach Resort in Nirwana Gardens, Lagoi, with a seaside setting and great ambience. It has good vegetarian options. As it's in a resort, prices are higher than local eateries.

Money and mobile data

  • Currency: we had to pay in Indonesian rupiah everywhere, so carry rupiah for taxis, food and entry fees. 1 rupee is roughly 190 rupiah.
  • Mobile: our Singapore mobile plan worked in Bintan, so we didn't need a local SIM. Check that your plan includes roaming in Indonesia before you go.

Our verdict

Bintan is really cool. It's perfect for couples and families, and ideal when you just want to relax without a long flight. The people were really nice and helpful, and we loved the hospitality. If you live in Singapore, or you're visiting and have a spare weekend, it's one of the easiest and most rewarding trips you can add.

Tips for your first Bintan trip

  • Fill in the All Indonesia arrival card within 72 hours before you travel.
  • Book weekend ferries early, as they fill up and cost more.
  • Remember the one-hour time difference for your return ferry.
  • Carry rupiah in cash for taxis and smaller places.
  • Use a local driver package for the sand dunes and Tanjung Pinang; it's cheaper and easier than separate rides.
  • Pack sunscreen, sunglasses and a hat for the sand dunes, and mosquito repellent for the mangroves.

FAQs

Do Indians need a visa for Bintan?

Yes. Indian passport holders need a visa for Indonesia. You can get a visa on arrival at the Bintan ferry terminal or apply for an e-VOA online before you travel. It costs IDR 500,000 (about ₹2,600) and is valid for 30 days. You also need to fill in the free All Indonesia arrival card online within 72 hours before arrival.

How long is the ferry from Singapore to Bintan?

About an hour from Tanah Merah Ferry Terminal to Bandar Bentan Telani (BBT) with Bintan Resort Ferries. Bintan is one hour behind Singapore, so check your return ferry time in local time.

How much does a Bintan trip from Singapore cost?

Our 2-day weekend cost about ₹40,000 (around S$570) for two people, including the return ferry (about S$100 each), one night in Lagoi (about ₹5,000), a taxi package, a mangrove tour, a spa visit and food. Add about ₹2,600 per person if you pay for your own visa.

Is 2 days enough for Bintan?

Yes. A weekend is enough for Lagoi beach, a mangrove tour, the sand dunes, the 500 Lohan Temple and a spa. Stay a third day if you want more time to relax on the beach.

Can I pay in Singapore dollars in Bintan?

We had to pay in Indonesian rupiah, so carry rupiah for taxis, food and entry fees.

Is there Indian food in Bintan?

Yes. We loved SPICE, an Indian restaurant at Mayang Sari Beach Resort in Nirwana Gardens, Lagoi. It has good vegetarian options and a lovely seaside setting.
