Bringing Your Parents to Singapore

Visit visa or Long-Term Visit Pass, insurance, and tips to make your parents' stay comfortable.

6 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Jakub Hałun, CC BY 4.0

Once you're settled in Singapore, the next question is usually: how do we bring Aai-Baba or Mummy-Papa over? There are two ways, a regular visit visa or a Long-Term Visit Pass, depending on how long they want to stay and how much you earn.

As of November 2026Rules change, and this is a general guide from our experience, not legal, tax or immigration advice. Always confirm with ICA (visas) and MOM (Long-Term Visit Pass) for your own case.

Option 1: a visit visa (most common)

Indian citizens need a visa to visit Singapore. Most parents come on a tourist visa applied for through an authorised visa agent, and the permitted stay is decided at immigration on arrival, often up to 30 days. This works well for holidays, festivals or helping out after a baby arrives.

Option 2: Long-Term Visit Pass (LTVP)

If you hold an Employment Pass and earn a fixed monthly salary of at least S$12,000, your employer can apply for a Long-Term Visit Pass for your parents. It lets them live with you for longer, and it's usually tied to the validity of your own pass.

  • Parents on an LTVP can't work in Singapore.
  • Your employer applies, with documents such as your birth certificate showing your parents' names.
  • If your EP ends, their LTVP ends too.

Health insurance is a must

Healthcare in Singapore is excellent but expensive for foreigners, as visitors pay non-subsidised rates. Before parents travel, buy travel or international health insurance that covers hospital stays and pre-existing conditions if possible, and carry a list of their medicines and prescriptions. See our healthcare guide for costs.

Tips for a comfortable stay

  • At the airport: request wheelchair or buggy assistance through the airline if needed. Changi is very easy to navigate.
  • Getting around: MRT and buses are step-free, and senior-friendly taxis and Grab cars are easy to book.
  • Food: vegetarian and Jain food is easy to find in Little India and across the city; see our Indian restaurants guide.
  • Temples: Sri Srinivasa Perumal, Sri Veeramakaliamman, Sri Mariamman and many more, plus gurdwaras and community events.
  • Weather: it's hot and humid all year, with sudden rain, so pack light clothes, an umbrella and a light jacket for air-conditioned places.
  • Things to do: Gardens by the Bay, the zoo and Night Safari, the Singapore River and Sentosa are all parent-friendly. Our Singapore with parents and kids itinerary has a ready plan.

FAQs

Can I bring my parents to live with me in Singapore?

If you're an Employment Pass holder earning a fixed monthly salary of at least S$12,000, your employer can apply for a Long-Term Visit Pass for your parents. Otherwise, parents usually visit on a tourist visa.

How long can Indian parents stay in Singapore on a tourist visa?

The permitted stay is decided by immigration on arrival, often up to 30 days. Check your parents' visa and arrival details.

Do my parents need health insurance in Singapore?

It's strongly recommended. Visitors pay non-subsidised rates, so buy travel or international health insurance that covers hospital stays before they travel.
