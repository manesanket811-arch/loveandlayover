Singapore news for Indians

Visa and work pass changes, Malaysia and Johor Bahru travel, transport and events: what's new in Singapore, and what it means for you. Updated as things change.

Daily Singapore briefing

Sat 28 Nov 2026
  1. RulesFrom 1 December, holding your phone while driving is an offence

    New Road Traffic Act rules start on 1 December: you can't hold a mobile phone while driving, even if you're not using it. You may still touch a mounted phone or hold it when the car is stopped, but distracted driving can still count as careless or dangerous driving, and penalties for dangerous driving causing serious injury are going up. If you drive or ride here, get a phone mount before Tuesday.

    Source: CNA ↗ · Our guide →
  2. TransportHigher taxi fares stay until end-February 2027

    ComfortDelGro, Strides Premier and GrabCab are keeping their temporary fare increases, first added in March because of fuel costs, until 28 February 2027 instead of ending them on 30 November. On ComfortDelGro flat-fare trips, including app bookings, the extra driver fee stays at 50 cents for fares under S$15 and 80 cents above that.

    Source: The Straits Times ↗
  3. Jobs & moneyMedian income in Singapore rises to S$6,021 a month, but pay growth slows

    MOM's early figures show the median gross monthly income of full-time employed residents rose from S$5,775 in 2025 to S$6,021 in 2026. After inflation that's a 2.2% rise, slower than last year's 4.1%, as firms were more cautious with pay. A handy benchmark if you're weighing up a job offer.

    Source: CNA ↗ · Our guide →
  4. TravelJohor-Singapore economic zone blueprint pushed to early 2027

    Malaysia's Economy Minister says the plan for the Johor-Singapore Special Economic Zone is ready but its launch has moved to early 2027 so feedback from Singapore can be included. Easing the movement of people and goods across the border is one of the key issues it covers.

    Source: The Straits Times ↗ · Our guide →
  5. EventsA baby white rhino makes his debut at Singapore Zoo

    A male white rhino calf, born on 22 October, is now out in the Wild Africa zone at Singapore Zoo and doesn't have a name yet. He naps a lot, so he may not always be out. A sweet extra for a weekend zoo trip with kids.

    Source: The Straits Times ↗
