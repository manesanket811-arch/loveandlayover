Things to do in Singapore in December 2026

December is Singapore's most festive month: Orchard Road lit up for Christmas, festive carnivals at Gardens by the Bay and Sentosa, and New Year's Eve fireworks over Marina Bay. It's also the rainy season and peak travel time, so book early.

9 eventsUpdated 26 Nov 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore

Our top picks for December

17 Nov – 3 JanOrchard Road Christmas Light-Up

The whole shopping street lit up, with festive mall displays and a free street party on Christmas Eve.

27 Nov – 3 JanChristmas Wonderland

Singapore's biggest festive carnival: light displays, a 20-metre elf treehouse, Santa's Mission Control, rides and festive food. Super-peak dates are 4–6, 11–13 and 18–27 December.

27 Nov – 3 JanSeason of Joy, A Universal Christmas and Christmas Glowcean

Festive shows and decorations across Resorts World Sentosa, a Christmas makeover at Universal Studios Singapore and a festive glow at the Singapore Oceanarium.

31 DecONE Countdown 2027 and Marina Bay fireworks

Four-part fireworks over Marina Bay from 10.30pm to midnight, with countdown parties across the island.

Weather in December

Northeast monsoon: 24–31°C, the wettest stretch, with heavy showers that usually pass in an hour or two.

What usually happens in December

  • Orchard Road Christmas light-up and festive events across the city
  • Christmas markets and festive shows at Gardens by the Bay and Sentosa
  • New Year's Eve fireworks at Marina Bay

Public holidays

  • 25 Dec: Christmas Day (public holiday)

Shops and attractions are usually open on public holidays, but they're busier, and some offices and banks close.

Tips for December

  • Book Christmas Wonderland on an off-peak weekday (27 Nov–3 Dec or 7–10 Dec) for cheaper tickets.
  • On 31 December, arrive at Marina Bay by 8–9pm and expect MRT crowd control.
  • Indian passport holders can visit Malaysia visa-free only until 31 December 2026; from 1 January 2027 a visa is needed.
  • Peak season: book flights and hotels early
  • Plan outdoor sights for mornings and keep indoor backups
