Essential Travel Information

💳 Money Management

Currency Tips: Notify your bank of travel dates. Carry mix of cash and cards. Use ATMs for best rates. Avoid currency exchange booths.

📞 Communication

Stay Connected: Get international roaming plan or local SIM. WhatsApp works on WiFi. Download offline maps before traveling.

✈️ Airport Tips

Smooth Travel: Arrive 3 hours early for international flights. Check baggage limits. Have documents ready. Download airline app.

🏨 Accommodation

Stay Smart: Book confirmed accommodations. Share details with family. Read recent reviews. Check cancellation policy.

🍽️ Food Safety

Eat Safely: Eat where locals eat. Avoid raw vegetables in some countries. Drink bottled water. Try street food cautiously.

🚗 Transportation

Get Around: Research local transit options. Use registered taxis/apps. Avoid traveling alone late night. Wear seatbelts.
