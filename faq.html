Singapore Travel FAQ

How many days should I spend in Singapore?
3-5 days is ideal. 3 days covers Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa Island. 5 days adds neighborhoods, food tours, Chinatown, Arab Street, and island hopping.
When is the best time to visit?
February-April and June-August have less rainfall. Avoid November-January (monsoon). Singapore is tropical year-round (24-32°C). December is hot but great for holidays and festivals.
How much should I budget per day?
Budget: SGD 60-100/day. Mid-range: SGD 100-200/day. Luxury: SGD 300+/day. Transport Day Pass: SGD 15. Hawker meals: SGD 3-8. Fine dining is expensive.
Do I need a visa?
Indian passport holders REQUIRE a Singapore visa (e-visa). Cost: SGD 30-50. Processing: 3-7 working days via authorized agents. Valid for single entry, 30-day stay. Documents: passport, return flight, proof of funds (SGD 1,500+), hotel booking, employment letter. Transit Exception: 96-hour visa-free transit with confirmed onward ticket same day. Official: Singapore ICA. Last verified: July 2026.
What are must-try foods?
Hainanese chicken rice, laksa, char kway teow, roti prata, satay, chili crab. Visit hawker centers like Maxwell Food Centre, Tiong Bahru, and Lau Pa Sat. Eat where locals eat - it's cheap and delicious.
Is Singapore safe for solo travelers?
Yes! Singapore is one of Asia's safest cities with excellent infrastructure, low crime rates, and helpful locals. Great for solo travelers of all backgrounds and experience levels.
How do I get around?
MRT (metro) is world-class and efficient. Buy an EZ-Link card for public transport. Grab (ride-sharing) is cheaper than taxis. Buses are reliable. The city is very walkable in neighborhoods.
What should I pack?
Light, breathable clothes, sunscreen, sunglasses, umbrella (for sudden rain), comfortable walking shoes. Singapore is air-conditioned - bring a light jacket. Modest clothing for temples.

Japan Travel FAQ

How many days for Japan?
5-10 days is ideal for first-timers. 5 days covers Tokyo and Mt. Fuji. 7-10 days adds Kyoto temples, traditional culture, and regional exploration. Budget 2-3 days per major city.
Best time to visit?
Spring (March-May) for cherry blossoms - peak season. Fall (September-November) for autumn colors. Winter has snow but is cold. Summer is hot and humid. Avoid Golden Week and New Year.
Daily budget?
Budget: ¥3,000-5,000/day. Mid-range: ¥7,000-12,000/day. Japan is affordable if you eat at local spots and use public transport. Convenience store meals are cheap and good.
Do I need a JR Pass?
For 7+ days visiting multiple cities, a 7-day JR Pass (¥280-320) offers unlimited train travel nationwide. For shorter stays or single-city trips, buy individual tickets - often cheaper.
Must-try Japanese foods?
Ramen, sushi, tempura, okonomiyaki, donburi, tonkatsu, udon noodles. Try local restaurants away from tourist areas. Convenience store food (onigiri, bento) is excellent and cheap.
Visa requirements?
For Indian Passport Holders: Japan visa IS REQUIRED. Apply at the Japanese embassy (Delhi) or consulate (Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai). Processing: 4-5 working days. Tourist visa valid for 90-day stay. Proof of funds (~¥250,000), hotel bookings, return flight, and bank statements required. See our Japan guide for complete details. Last verified: July 2026.
Is Japan safe?
Yes! Japan is very safe with low crime, excellent public transport, helpful locals, and clear signage in English. Great for solo travelers. English is limited outside major cities - download translation apps.
What should I see in Tokyo?
Shibuya Crossing, Senso-ji Temple, Tokyo Tower, Harajuku fashion, Shinjuku nightlife, teamLab Borderless, Meiji Shrine. Book attractions in advance during peak season (March-May, September-November).

Australia Travel FAQ

How many days for Australia?
3 days for Sydney highlights. 5 days for Sydney + Great Barrier Reef or Blue Mountains. 10 days for full experience: Sydney, Reef, Uluru, Melbourne. Australia is vast - plan accordingly.
Best time to visit?
December-February is summer (hot). April-September is autumn/winter (best weather, 15-25°C). Avoid January-February heat in Outback. May-August is ideal with perfect weather statewide.
Daily budget?
Budget: AUD 150-250/day. Mid-range: AUD 200-350/day. Luxury: AUD 400+/day. Accommodation: AUD 50-150/night. Food: AUD 40-100/day. Activities: AUD 50-150/day.
Visa for Indians?
Indian passports are NOT eligible for the ETA. Apply for Visitor visa (subclass 600) online via ImmiAccount. Cost: AUD 200+. Processing: 1-5 weeks. Valid for 3-12 months. Documents: proof of funds (AUD 20,000+), return ticket, employment letter, bank statements. Official: Australian Immigration. Last verified: July 2026.
Top attractions?
Sydney Opera House & Harbour Bridge, Great Barrier Reef (diving/snorkeling), Uluru (Ayers Rock) & Outback, Blue Mountains, Bondi Beach, Taronga Zoo, Melbourne street art.
Is Australia safe?
Yes, very safe. Low crime, good infrastructure, helpful locals. Be cautious of wildlife (snakes, spiders) especially in outback - they're rare in cities. Use reef safety advice when diving.
How do I travel between cities?
Domestic flights are affordable (AUD 50-200). Train and bus options available but slower. Rent a car for Outback and road trips. Public transport is excellent in major cities.
Must-try Australian foods?
Barbie (BBQ), lamingtons, Vegemite on toast, Tim tams, meat pies, fresh seafood. Coastal cities have world-class coffee culture. Aboriginal foods in some restaurants (native fruits, meats).

Thailand Travel FAQ

How many days for Thailand?
5-7 days for Bangkok + islands. 10 days for comprehensive trip: Bangkok, Chiang Mai, islands (Phuket/Krabi). 3 days for quick Bangkok city break. Thailand is perfect for any duration.
Best travel season?
November-February (cool season, 20-30°C) is best with clear skies. March-May is hot (35°C+). June-October is monsoon (rainy, cheap). Avoid September-October for heavy rain.
Daily budget?
Budget: $20-35/day easily. Mid-range: $50-100/day. Luxury: $150+/day. Thailand is incredibly cheap - eat like royalty on a budget. Street food meals: $1-3.
Visa requirements?
60-90 days visa-free for most nationalities including Indians. Get Tourist Visa (60 days) from Thai embassy/consulate. On-arrival visas sometimes available but slower.
Must-try Thai foods?
Pad Thai, Tom Yum soup, Green curry, Satay, Mango sticky rice, fresh spring rolls. Street food is amazing - eat from busy carts with high turnover. Try night markets for variety.
Is Thailand safe?
Yes, very safe for tourists. Use common sense, avoid isolated areas late at night, watch valuables in crowded areas. Health care is good and affordable. Scams exist - stay alert in tourist areas.
Transport options?
Buses cheapest (150-600 baht long trips). Flights affordable (domestic flights 1000-2000 baht). Trains scenic and slow. Taxis, tuk-tuks, and ride-sharing (Grab) very cheap in cities. Rent scooters for island exploration.
Where should I go?
Bangkok (temples, street food, nightlife), Chiang Mai (culture, mountains, elephants), Phuket/Krabi (beaches), Koh Samui/Koh Phangan (islands), Ayutthaya (historical).

Vietnam Travel FAQ

Trip duration?
5-7 days minimum. 10-14 days ideal for north-to-south tour: Hanoi, Halong Bay, Hoi An, Ho Chi Minh, Mekong Delta. 3 days for one city (Hanoi or HCMC).
Best season?
October-April (cool, dry, 15-25°C). Avoid May-September (monsoon, heat, humidity). December-January is ideal. Lunar New Year (Feb/Mar) - crowded but festive.
Budget per day?
Budget: $15-25/day easily. Mid-range: $30-50/day. One of Asia's cheapest! Street food meals: $1-2. Hotels: $8-20/night. You'll eat amazing food super cheap.
Visa process?
Visa on arrival at airport - easy and quick. Cost ~$25. Apply online before arriving or get at airport. 30-day single/multiple entry visas available. Processing straightforward.
Must-try Vietnamese dishes?
Pho (noodle soup), banh mi (sandwich), fresh spring rolls, cha ca (fish), cao lau, egg coffee. Street food vendors - eat what locals eat. Night markets are amazing.
Is it safe for solo travelers?
Yes! Vietnam is safe, welcoming, and full of backpackers. Hanoi and HCMC traffic looks chaotic but is manageable. Avoid walking alone very late night. Keep valuables secure.
Transportation?
Buses are cheapest ($10-20 long trips). Overnight buses save accommodation. Flights affordable ($30-60 between cities). Taxis/ride-sharing (Grab) ultra cheap. Motorbike rentals popular for adventure.
Top destinations?
Hanoi (capital, culture), Halong Bay (UNESCO, limestone karsts), Hoi An (ancient town, tailoring), Ho Chi Minh City (energy, history), Mekong Delta (floating markets, rural).

India Travel FAQ

Best regions to visit?
North: Delhi, Agra (Taj Mahal), Jaipur, Himalayas. South: Kerala, Goa beaches, Bangalore. East: Kolkata, Darjeeling. West: Mumbai, Rajasthan. Choose 1-2 regions for first trip.
Best time to visit?
October-March (cool, dry, 15-30°C). Avoid April-June (heat 40°C+) and July-September (monsoon). December-January is peak but crowded. September-October is best weather.
Daily budget?
Budget: $15-30/day easily. Mid-range: $40-80/day. India is extremely affordable - you'll live like royalty on a budget. Street food: $0.50-2. Hotels: $10-40/night.
Visa needed?
Tourist visa required. Get it from Indian embassy/consulate or eVisa online (recommended, fast, cheap ~$25). Standard visa 6 months. eVisa 1 year multiple entry.
Must-try Indian foods?
Biryani, butter chicken, dosa, samosas, pakora, street chaat (bhelpuri, papdi chaat), lassi. Regional cuisines vary - South Indian, Mughlai, Bengali. Street vendors - it's fresh and delicious.
Is it safe?
Yes, millions of tourists visit safely yearly. Use common sense: avoid isolated areas late night, watch valuables in crowded places. Major tourist areas are very safe. Women solo travelers report good experiences with precautions.
Getting around?
Trains are iconic, cheap, and reliable (book in advance). Buses connect everywhere (cheap but slow). Domestic flights affordable ($40-80). Autos (3-wheelers) and taxis ultra-cheap in cities.
What to pack?
Lightweight clothes, sunscreen, hat. Modest clothing for temples/religious sites (cover shoulders/knees). Comfortable walking shoes. Loose fitting clothes for heat. Dupatta (scarf) for women for temple visits.

Mexico Travel FAQ

Trip length?
7-10 days ideal for Mexico City + beach (Cancun/Playa del Carmen/Tulum). 5 days for Mexico City + nearby ruins. 3 days for beach only. Mexico has diverse experiences - plan accordingly.
Best time?
November-April (cool, 20-30°C). May-October is rainy season but cheaper. Hurricane season June-November in Caribbean. December-January peak (crowded, expensive). September-October best value.
Daily budget?
Budget: $30-50/day. Mid-range: $70-120/day. Street tacos: $1-2. Beach resort hotels: $40-80/night. Food is cheap and incredible. All-inclusives are good value.
Visa?
For Indian Passport Holders: Tourist visa REQUIRED. Apply at Mexican consulate (Delhi, Mumbai, Bangalore) 4-6 weeks ahead. Processing: 5-10 working days. Exception: Visa-free if you hold a valid US, Canada, UK, Japan, or Schengen visa/residence. Documents needed: passport, proof of funds (USD 600+), return ticket, hotel booking, employment letter. Official: Mexico's INM. Last verified: July 2026.
Must-try foods?
Tacos (al pastor, carnitas), quesadillas, tamales, chiles rellenos, ceviche, street elote (corn). Regional cuisines vary - Oaxaca is food heaven. Mezcal and tequila. Try local markets.
Safe to travel?
Tourist areas (Mexico City, Cancun, Playa del Carmen, Oaxaca) are safe with normal precautions. Avoid showing expensive items, don't travel at night alone. Check current advisories for specific regions.
Transportation?
Buses connect all cities (cheap, efficient). Domestic flights affordable. Taxis in major cities - use Uber/Didi for safety. Rent cars for flexibility. First/second class buses recommended.
Top destinations?
Mexico City (culture, food, museums), Cancun/Playa del Carmen (beaches), Tulum (ruins + beach), Chichen Itza (Maya ruins), Oaxaca (indigenous culture, food), Copper Canyon.

Egypt Travel FAQ

How many days?
7-10 days ideal: Cairo (pyramids, museums) + Nile cruise/Luxor/Aswan. 5 days for Cairo highlights. 3 days for quick Cairo visit. Ancient Egypt spans many sites - allow time.
Best season?
October-April (cool, 15-30°C, sunny). Avoid May-September (heat 40°C+, very hot). November-March is peak. February is ideal - warm days, cool nights, few crowds.
Daily budget?
Budget: $20-35/day. Mid-range: $50-100/day. Nile cruises: $30-100/night (good value). Street food: $1-3. Hotels: $15-50/night. Very affordable destination.
Visa requirements?
Visa on arrival at airport (recommended). Cost ~$25 USD. OR get from Egyptian embassy beforehand. Tourist visa 30 days. Easy process - no pre-approval needed.
Must-try Egyptian food?
Koshari (rice, pasta, lentils), ful medames (fava bean stew), falafel, Egyptian bread (aish baladi), molokhia, fresh juice. Street food is authentic and cheap. Try Egyptian coffee.
Safety concerns?
Cairo is safe in tourist areas with normal precautions. Avoid large crowds, don't photograph military sites. Check advisories for remote areas. Nile cruises are safe and popular. Women travelers: dress modestly, use common sense.
How to see pyramids?
Giza pyramids 30km from Cairo (easy day trip). Book tours or hire driver. Early morning best to avoid crowds and heat. Sound & light show at evening. Sphinx also at Giza. Underground museums excellent.
Nile cruise vs other options?
Nile cruises (Luxor-Aswan, 3-5 days) are popular and good value. All-inclusive: meals, transport, tours. Alternatively: flights + hotels in each city. Cruises are romantic, easier for first-timers.

Turkey Travel FAQ

Trip duration?
7-10 days ideal: Istanbul + Cappadocia + Mediterranean coast. 5 days for Istanbul + Cappadocia. 3 days for Istanbul city break. Turkey has diverse experiences - culture, nature, beaches.
Best time?
April-May (spring, 20-28°C) and September-October (fall, 22-30°C) are ideal. Summer (June-August) hot, crowded. Winter (November-March) cold but scenic. Avoid July-August for crowds.
Budget per day?
Budget: $25-40/day. Mid-range: $60-100/day. Turkey is affordable - excellent value. Street kebab: $2-4. Hotels: $20-60/night. Food is delicious and cheap.
Visa?
Indian passport holders need e-Visa. Get online (3 minutes, ~$20). Valid 90 days. Accepted for single/multiple entry. No pre-approval needed - instant online.
Must-try Turkish food?
Kebab (doner, shish, Adana), baklava, mezze (appetizers), Turkish breakfast spreads, pide (Turkish pizza), manti (dumplings), meze platters. Turkish tea everywhere. Authentic street food amazing.
Is it safe?
Turkey is safe in major tourist areas (Istanbul, Cappadocia, Mediterranean). Normal precautions apply. Avoid large crowds/protests. Generally very welcoming to tourists. Women travelers report good experiences.
What to see?
Istanbul (Blue Mosque, Hagia Sophia, bazaars), Cappadocia (fairy chimneys, hot air balloons), Pamukkale (thermal pools), Troy, Ephesus (ancient ruins), Mediterranean coast (Antalya).
Transportation?
Buses are cheap and connect all cities. Domestic flights affordable. Istanbul metro/tram efficient. Minibuses (dolmus) for local travel. Rent car for flexible exploration. Trains scenic.
