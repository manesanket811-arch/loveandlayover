Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visiting Singapore and planning your trip.

How many days should I spend in Singapore?
It depends on your travel style and interests. We offer 3-day itineraries (nature focused), 4-day itineraries (food culture), and 5-day itineraries (complete explorer) to suit different preferences.
When is the best time to visit Singapore?
February to April (Chinese New Year season) and June to August are ideal with less rainfall. Avoid November to January for monsoon season. December is hot and humid but great for holidays.
How much does a day in Singapore cost?
Budget travelers can spend $40-60 SGD/day on food and activities. Mid-range travelers budget $100-150 SGD/day. Luxury travelers spend $200+ SGD/day. Transportation via Tourist Day Pass costs around $15 SGD.
What is the best way to get around Singapore?
The efficient MRT (metro) system is the best option. Buy a Tourist Day Pass for unlimited travel on metros and buses. Grab (ride-hailing) is also convenient for longer distances or after midnight.
What are the must-try foods in Singapore?
Try laksa (spicy noodle soup), hainanese chicken rice, char kway teow (fried noodles), roti prata (Indian bread), and fresh seafood. Visit hawker centers like Maxwell Food Centre, Tiong Bahru, and Newton for authentic local food.
Is Singapore safe for solo travelers?
Yes! Singapore is one of the safest cities in Asia with excellent public infrastructure, low crime rates, efficient police presence, and great public transportation. Women solo travelers report feeling very safe here.
What are the top hawker centers in Singapore?
Must-visit hawker centers include Maxwell Food Centre (Chinatown), Tiong Bahru Market (local favorite), Newton Food Centre (seafood), Lau Pa Sat (historic), and East Coast Seafood Centre (beachside dining).
What should I see at Marina Bay?
Visit Marina Bay Sands for the observation deck and museum, explore Gardens by the Bay with the iconic Supertrees, see the light show nightly, and walk along the esplanade. Visit Singapore Flyer for 360-degree city views.
How do I visit Gardens by the Bay?
Exploring the outdoor gardens is free. You can walk around the Supertrees and enjoy the nightly light show (free). Paid attractions include the Cloud Forest and Flower Dome ($31-35 SGD each) with stunning indoor gardens.
What is Sentosa Island?
Sentosa is Singapore's leisure island with attractions including Universal Studios Singapore, beautiful beaches, S.E.A Aquarium, Sky Bridge, and adventure activities. Take the cable car or monorail from the city center.
What is the weather like in Singapore?
Singapore is hot and humid year-round (24-32°C / 75-90°F). Pack light, breathable clothes, sunscreen, and an umbrella for occasional rain. December to early January is driest but still warm and humid.
Do I need a SIM card in Singapore?
Yes, get a local SIM card for cheap data, calls, and SMS. Buy one at Changi Airport from StarHub, Singtel, or M1 ($15-30 SGD with included credit). Tourist plans offer unlimited data for 4-7 days.
What is the currency in Singapore?
Singapore Dollar (SGD). 1 SGD ≈ 0.74 USD. Most places accept credit cards, but carry some cash for hawker centers and small shops. ATMs are widely available at MRT stations and shopping centers.
Do I need to tip in Singapore?
Tipping is not mandatory in Singapore. It is uncommon and usually not expected. However, tipping for exceptional service at restaurants (5-10%) or for porters ($1-2 SGD) is appreciated but optional.
Do I need a visa to visit Singapore?
Many nationalities get 90 days visa-free (US, UK, EU citizens, Australians, Canadians). Check with Singapore Immigration and Checkpoints Authority (ICA) website for your specific country's requirements before traveling.
Should I get travel insurance for Singapore?
Yes, travel insurance is recommended to protect against flight cancellations, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and trip interruptions. Singapore has excellent healthcare but it can be expensive for tourists without insurance.
What is Changi Airport like?
Changi Airport is consistently ranked as one of the world's best airports. It has excellent facilities, free WiFi, gardens, nap areas, and easy connections to the city via MRT (45 min, $2.50 SGD) or taxi ($25-35 SGD).
What are the best neighborhoods to explore?
Chinatown (culture and history), Little India (food and spices), Kampong Glam (heritage and architecture), Orchard Road (shopping), Clarke Quay (nightlife), and Sentosa Island (beaches and attractions) are must-see areas.
What are Singapore's top attractions?
Top attractions include Marina Bay Sands, Gardens by the Bay, Singapore Flyer, Merlion Park, Singapore Botanic Gardens, Sentosa Island, Universal Studios Singapore, and S.E.A Aquarium.
Can I use Singapore as a layover destination?
Absolutely! Singapore is perfect for 6-hour, 12-hour, 24-hour, or 48-hour layovers. Our dedicated layover guide shows you how to maximize your time, including fast transportation from Changi Airport and must-see attractions near the airport.
