How we got here

We are Singapore-based lifestyle creators who never quite settled into just one lane — one day we're curled up at a cafe hunting for the best flat white in the city, the next we're somewhere new, camera in hand, chasing whatever makes life feel a little more special.

That's the thing about lifestyle content, really: it's less about a niche and more about noticing the good stuff — food, travel, wellness, little everyday moments — and sharing it in a way that feels honest.

We split our world across two spaces: @nikita.lagaskar, where we share everyday lifestyle, dining spots, and things we genuinely love, and @mi.punekar_, our more personal, rooted space that connects back to home and culture.

Together, our two spaces reach a community of 45,000+ people who trust our recommendations — not because we're chasing trends, but because we only talk about what we'd tell our own friends.
— Sanket & Nikita
