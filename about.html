Meet Nikita & Sanket

We are Singapore-based lifestyle creators who never quite settled into just one lane — one day we're curled up at a cafe hunting for the best flat white in the city, the next we're somewhere new, camera in hand, chasing whatever makes life feel a little more special. That's the thing about lifestyle content, really: it's less about a niche and more about noticing the good stuff — food, travel, wellness, little everyday moments — and sharing it in a way that feels honest.

We split our world across two spaces: @nikita.lagaskar, where we share everyday lifestyle, dining spots, and things we genuinely love, and @mi.punekar_, our more personal, rooted space that connects back to home and culture. Together, they reach a community of 45,000+ people who trust our recommendations — not because we're chasing trends, but because we only talk about what we'd tell our own friends.

We've had the chance to collaborate with brands like Scentopia and Imagica, bringing their experiences to life for our audience in a way that felt real rather than scripted — and that's exactly the kind of partnership we love: ones where the brand's story and our voice actually fit together.

If you're a cafe, restaurant, spa, or brand looking for creators who show up with genuine curiosity, bring an engaged community along for the ride, and treat every collaboration like a relationship rather than a transaction — we'd love to hear from you.
