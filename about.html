Hi, I'm Nikita & Sanket 👋

I'm a Singapore-based lifestyle creator who never quite settled into just one lane — one day I'm curled up at a cafe hunting for the best flat white in the city, the next I'm somewhere new, camera in hand, chasing whatever makes life feel a little more special. That's the thing about lifestyle content, really: it's less about a niche and more about noticing the good stuff — food, travel, wellness, little everyday moments — and sharing it in a way that feels honest.

I split my world across two spaces: @nikita.lagaskar, where I share my everyday lifestyle, dining spots, and things I genuinely love, and @mi.punekar_, my more personal, rooted space that connects back to home and culture. Together, they reach a community of 45,000+ people who trust my recommendations — not because I'm chasing trends, but because I only talk about what I'd tell my own friends.

I've had the chance to collaborate with brands like Scentopia and Imagica, bringing their experiences to life for my audience in a way that felt real rather than scripted — and that's exactly the kind of partnership I love: ones where the brand's story and my voice actually fit together.
