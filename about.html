Welcome to Love and Layovers—authentic travel guides created by travelers, for Indian passport holders who want to explore the world without BS.

Our Story

Love and Layovers started with a simple observation: most travel guides weren't made for Indian passport holders. Visa requirements were wrong, budget estimates didn't match reality, and tips assumed you had endless money.

We created this platform to fix that. Every guide is researched, fact-checked, and updated regularly. Every budget is based on real travelers' experiences. Every visa tip is verified against official immigration websites.

We've traveled to 16+ destinations across Asia, Europe, Americas, Africa, and created free, detailed itineraries for each. We share what we learned—the real costs, the best food, the neighborhoods worth visiting, and the mistakes to avoid.

What We Stand For

Authenticity

No fake testimonials, no overblown claims. Just real travel experiences and honest recommendations from travelers who've been there.

Accuracy

Visa info is verified against official embassy sources. Budget estimates are current. Coordinates of attractions are tested. We update regularly.

Accessibility

All guides are 100% free. No paywalls, no sponsorships, no affiliate links pushing overpriced products. Travel planning shouldn't cost money.

Inclusivity

Created specifically for Indian passport holders. We understand visa challenges, currency conversions, and travel norms that are unique to our context.
