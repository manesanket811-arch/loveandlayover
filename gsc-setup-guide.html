Google Search Console Setup Guide

Verify your website ownership, monitor search traffic, and improve SEO performance with Google Search Console.

What is Google Search Console (GSC)?

Google Search Console is a free tool that helps you monitor how your website appears in Google Search results. It shows you:

  • Search traffic: How many times your site appears in search results and how often users click through
  • Indexing status: Which pages Google has indexed and any crawl errors
  • Search performance: Average position, click-through rate, and queries that bring traffic
  • Mobile usability: Issues that may affect mobile users
  • Manual actions: Alerts if Google detects unnatural links or spammy content

Pre-Verification Checklist

  • Domain ownership: You have admin access to loveandlayover.in domain registrar
  • Email account: Access to theloveandlayover@gmail.com Gmail account
  • Sitemaps ready: robots.txt references sitemap (verified ✓)
  • GA4 installed: Google Analytics 4 tracking deployed to all pages (verified ✓)
  • Metadata complete: All pages have proper titles and descriptions (verified ✓)
  • 15-30 minutes: Time to complete setup and verification

Step 1: Access Google Search Console

1Visit Google Search Console

Go to https://search.google.com/search-console and sign in with your Google account (theloveandlayover@gmail.com)

Make sure you're signed into the Gmail account associated with this project.

2Start Adding Your Property

You'll see two options:

  • Domain — Covers all subdomains (www and non-www) and all protocols (HTTP and HTTPS) ← Recommended
  • URL prefix — Specific to the exact URL you enter (less flexible)

Choose: Domain and enter: loveandlayover.in

3Verify Domain Ownership

Google will ask you to verify ownership of the domain. You'll see a DNS record to add:

Record type: TXT

Name: loveandlayover.in (or @ if your provider uses @)

Value: google-site-verification=... (Google will provide the exact code)

Copy the verification code provided by Google Search Console.

Adding DNS Record to Your Domain Registrar

⚠️ Important: You need to add the DNS verification record through your domain registrar (where you registered loveandlayover.in). This is typically done through a hosting control panel like cPanel, Namecheap, GoDaddy, Route 53, etc.

If using common registrars:

  • Namecheap: Dashboard → your domain → Advanced DNS → Add TXT record
  • GoDaddy: My Products → DNS Manager → Add DNS Record → TXT
  • AWS Route 53: Hosted zones → Your domain → Create Record → Type: TXT
  • Hostinger/Bluehost: Control Panel → DNS Records → Add New Record
  • Domain.com: Manage Domain → DNS → Add Record → Text (TXT)

General process (all registrars):

  1. Log in to your domain registrar account
  2. Find "DNS Records," "DNS Settings," or "Advanced DNS" section
  3. Add a new TXT record with:
    • Host/Name: @ or your domain name (registrar-specific)
    • Type: TXT
    • Value: Paste the entire google-site-verification code from GSC
  4. Save/Publish the record
  5. Return to Google Search Console and click "Verify"
✓ DNS propagation note: DNS changes take 15 minutes to 24 hours to propagate worldwide. If verification fails immediately, wait 30 minutes and retry.

Submit each one. Google will show:

✓ Processing time: Google typically processes sitemaps within 24-48 hours. Check back after a day to see indexing progress.

Common Issues & Solutions

Issue: "DNS record not found" after 24 hours

Solution: Verify you added the TXT record in the correct location. Some registrars require "@" as the host, others require the full domain name. Check your registrar's documentation or contact support to confirm the record is published.

Issue: Sitemaps submitted but showing 0 indexed URLs

Solution: This is normal in the first 24-48 hours. Google needs time to crawl and process the sitemap. Check back after 2-3 days. If still at 0, verify robots.txt allows crawling (it does ✓).

Issue: Pages not appearing in search results after 1 month

Solution: New sites typically take 2-4 weeks to appear in search. Ensure:

  • Meta descriptions are unique and compelling
  • Page titles include target keywords (e.g., "Singapore Travel Guide – 5-Day Itinerary")
  • Content is substantive (your 2000+ word guides meet this ✓)
  • Internal linking is good (verified ✓)
  • Site speed is acceptable (use PageSpeed Insights to check)

Issue: Lower CTR than expected (below 3%)

Solution: Improve your meta descriptions. They should be 155-160 characters and include the target keyword. Example:

"Free travel guide to Singapore for Indian passport holders. 5-day itinerary, visa info, budget breakdown, food recommendations, and insider tips."

Use GSC to identify low-CTR pages and update their meta descriptions.
