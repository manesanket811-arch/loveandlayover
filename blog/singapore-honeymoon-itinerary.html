Singapore Honeymoon Itinerary

Five easy, romantic days in Singapore for newly-weds: skyline views, Sentosa sunsets, light shows and great food, with a rupee budget for two.

6 min readUpdated October 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Zairon, CC BY-SA 4.0

We're a couple who moved to Singapore, and it's still where we go for date nights. For a honeymoon it's an easy win: short flights from India, no jet lag, very safe, spotless, and full of skyline views, beaches and food you'll both love, including plenty of Indian and vegetarian options.

Where to stay on your honeymoon

Many couples split the stay: most nights somewhere central and well-priced, plus one or two special nights.

  • Marina Bay: the iconic skyline and the famous rooftop infinity pool at Marina Bay Sands (pool access is for hotel guests only). Rooms often start around S$700 a night, so many couples book just one night here.
  • Sentosa: resort hotels, beaches and quiet evenings, a short ride from the city. Great for the last two nights.
  • Clarke Quay and Robertson Quay: riverside dining, easy walks at night and mid-range boutique hotels.
  • Bugis or Little India: best value, central MRT links and Indian food everywhere.
Mention the honeymoonAdd "honeymoon" in the special requests when you book. Many hotels add a small surprise or a room upgrade when available.

Day 1: Arrive and fall for the skyline

Most flights from India land in the morning. Before leaving Changi, walk through Jewel to see the Rain Vortex, the world's tallest indoor waterfall. Check in and rest. In the evening, walk the Marina Bay waterfront past the Merlion as the lights come on, then watch the free Spectra light and water show in front of Marina Bay Sands.

FreeBest after 7 PM

Day 2: Gardens by the Bay and a river evening

Spend the morning in the cool Flower Dome and Cloud Forest, Singapore's most photogenic indoor spaces. Rest through the afternoon heat. Come back for the free Garden Rhapsody show under the glowing Supertrees, then take a bumboat river cruise and have dinner along Clarke Quay or Boat Quay.

Domes: about S$45–55 eachLight show: free

Day 3: Universal Studios or a slow city day

If you both love rides, spend the day at Universal Studios Singapore on Sentosa; our Universal Studios guide has a plan for the day. If not, keep it slow: brunch in Tiong Bahru, the colourful shophouses of Joo Chiat, and an evening ride on the Singapore Flyer at sunset.

Day 4: Sentosa beaches and sunset

Ride the cable car from Mount Faber to Sentosa for big harbour views. Spend the afternoon on Palawan or Tanjong Beach (quieter), cross the suspension bridge to the "southernmost point of continental Asia", and stay for the sunset with a drink at a beach club. Many evenings end with the Wings of Time show on Siloso Beach (check this year's show times).

Beaches: freeCable car: about S$35

Day 5: Culture, shopping and goodbye

Wander Kampong Glam and the street art of Haji Lane, then Little India for flowers, gold and a final banana-leaf meal. Pick up gifts at Mustafa Centre or Bugis Street before heading to the airport. Leave 3 hours for Changi so you have time for Jewel again.

Romantic extras worth the splurge

  • A rooftop dinner or drinks with a view of the bay (book a window table and ask for the time of the light show).
  • Night Safari: surprisingly romantic, on a tram through the dark jungle.
  • Couples' spa: Sentosa resorts and many city hotels offer packages.
  • A professional photoshoot: an hour at Marina Bay or Gardens by the Bay makes an unbeatable souvenir.

FAQs

Is Singapore good for a honeymoon?

Yes. It is safe, easy to get around, a short flight from India and full of romantic spots like Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay and Sentosa beaches. It suits couples who like cities, food and views more than remote beaches.

How many days are enough for a Singapore honeymoon?

Five days covers Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Sentosa and a slow day. Add three or four days in Bali or Malaysia for a beach extension.

What is the cost of a Singapore honeymoon from India?

A mid-range 5-day trip costs roughly ₹1.8–2.5 lakh for two, including flights, visas, hotel, food and attractions. One night at Marina Bay Sands adds significantly.

Can non-guests use the Marina Bay Sands infinity pool?

No. The rooftop infinity pool is for hotel guests only. Non-guests can visit the SkyPark Observation Deck with a ticket.

Which month is best for a honeymoon in Singapore?

February to April is usually drier and better value. December is festive with Christmas lights and New Year fireworks, but it is rainy and the most expensive time to travel.
