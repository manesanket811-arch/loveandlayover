Where to Buy Indian Groceries in Singapore

Mustafa, Tekka, Little India, supermarkets and online delivery: where to find every taste of home.

6 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Marcin Konsek, CC BY-SA 4.0

One of the best things about living in Singapore as an Indian: you can find almost every dal, masala, pickle and snack from home. Here's where to shop, from the famous Mustafa Centre to your neighbourhood supermarket and online delivery.

Mustafa Centre

The legendary multi-storey store in Little India (Syed Alwi Road) sells everything from groceries and spices to electronics, gold and luggage, and it's open until late. It has huge sections of Indian rice, atta, dal, spices, ready-to-eat food, pooja items and festival supplies. Go on a weekday to avoid the weekend crowds.

Tekka Centre and Little India shops

  • Tekka Centre (Buffalo Road): a wet market with fresh vegetables, curry leaves, drumsticks, banana leaves and Indian vegetables that are hard to find elsewhere, plus a hawker centre with Indian food upstairs.
  • Serangoon Road and side streets: small Indian grocery shops, sweet shops, flower garlands and pooja supplies, especially busy before Deepavali and Pongal.

Supermarkets near you

You don't have to go to Little India every week. NTUC FairPrice, Sheng Siong and Giant stock basics like atta, basmati rice, dal, paneer, spices and Indian brands, especially in areas with many Indian residents. Larger outlets often have dedicated Indian aisles.

Online delivery

  • FairPrice Online, RedMart and Amazon.sg for everyday groceries and Indian brands
  • GrabMart and foodpanda's pandamart for quick delivery
  • Indian grocery delivery shops: several local Indian stores deliver across Singapore; ask in community groups for current favourites

Tips to save

  • Buy staples like rice, atta and dal in bulk at Mustafa or online, and fresh vegetables at wet markets, which are often cheaper than supermarkets.
  • Look out for supermarket weekly deals and member discounts.
  • Stock up on festival items early, as Little India gets very crowded before Deepavali.

FAQs

Where can I buy Indian groceries in Singapore?

Mustafa Centre and Tekka Centre in Little India have the widest range. Supermarkets like NTUC FairPrice and Sheng Siong also stock Indian staples, and FairPrice Online, RedMart and Amazon.sg deliver.

Can you get fresh Indian vegetables in Singapore?

Yes. Tekka Centre's wet market in Little India sells curry leaves, drumsticks, banana leaves and other Indian vegetables.

Is Mustafa Centre open late?

Yes, Mustafa Centre in Little India is open until late at night, which makes it handy for shopping after work.
