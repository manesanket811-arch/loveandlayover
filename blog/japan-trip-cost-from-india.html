How Much Does a Japan Trip Cost from India?

A realistic budget in rupees for a week in Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka: flights, visa, hotels, food and trains.

7 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Martin Falbisoner, CC BY-SA 4.0

Japan has a reputation for being expensive, but with the yen weak against the rupee in recent years it's more affordable than many Indians expect. Here's what a week really costs, line by line, so you can plan with confidence.

The short answer

Budget₹1.2–1.4Lper person, 7 days
Mid-range₹1.4–1.8Lper person, 7 days

These include return flights from a major Indian city, the visa, a hotel shared by two, food, local transport, entry fees and one long-distance train trip. Your actual cost depends mostly on when you book flights and the season you travel in.

Flights

Tokyo (Narita or Haneda) and Osaka (Kansai) are the main airports. Non-stop flights run from Delhi and Mumbai, while one-stop routes via Singapore, Bangkok, Hong Kong or Kuala Lumpur are often cheaper. Book 2 to 3 months ahead, and avoid cherry blossom season (late March to early April) and Golden Week (late April to early May) if you're on a tight budget.

Our tipFly into Tokyo and out of Osaka (an "open-jaw" ticket). It often costs about the same as a return and saves you a train ride back to Tokyo.

Visa

Indians need a visa for Japan; there's no visa on arrival. Apply through the Japan Visa Application Centres (VFS Global) or an accredited travel agency about 3 to 4 weeks before you fly. You'll need bank statements, ITR or employment proof, and your flight and hotel bookings. The fee is small; the bigger cost is time, so apply early.

Hotels

Business hotels are Japan's secret weapon: small but spotless rooms near stations, often ¥8,000–15,000 a night for two. Capsule hotels cost less for solo travellers. Kyoto is pricier in spring and autumn, so book early for those seasons.

Food

Eating well in Japan is surprisingly cheap. Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart) sell good onigiri, sandwiches and salads, and ramen, curry and udon shops serve filling meals at modest prices. Vegetarian food takes more effort: look for Indian restaurants (common in big cities), Buddhist temple food in Kyoto, and use apps to find veg-friendly places.

Trains: is the JR Pass worth it?

The 7-day Japan Rail Pass costs ¥50,000. For a classic Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka trip, single tickets usually work out cheaper, since a Tokyo to Kyoto return is around ¥28,000. The pass pays off when you add longer trips such as Hiroshima or Kanazawa. Within cities, use a Suica or ICOCA card on trains and buses.

Rule of thumbAdd up your Shinkansen journeys. If they come to more than ¥50,000 within 7 days, buy the pass; otherwise buy single tickets.

Ways to spend less

  • Travel in early December, January to mid-March (outside the holidays) or June, when hotels are cheaper.
  • Stay near a JR or metro station so you don't need taxis.
  • Many of the best sights are free: Senso-ji, Meiji Shrine, Fushimi Inari, Shibuya Crossing and the streets of Gion.
  • Buy attraction tickets like teamLab or Universal Studios Japan online in advance.
  • Use day passes in Tokyo and Kyoto if you're taking lots of local rides.

FAQs

How much does a 7-day Japan trip cost from India?

Roughly ₹1.2–1.4 lakh per person on a budget and ₹1.4–1.8 lakh mid-range, including return flights, the visa, a shared hotel, food, local transport, entry fees and a Tokyo to Kyoto bullet train return.

Is Japan expensive for Indians?

Less than many people expect. With the yen weak in recent years, daily costs of about ₹8,000–12,500 per person cover a shared mid-range hotel, food, local transport and entry fees. Flights are the biggest single cost.

Is the Japan Rail Pass worth it for Indians?

For a classic Tokyo, Kyoto and Osaka trip, single tickets usually work out cheaper than the 7-day pass at ¥50,000. It's worth it if you add longer trips such as Hiroshima or Kanazawa.

How early should I apply for a Japan visa from India?

Apply about 3 to 4 weeks before you fly. Processing usually takes around 5 working days, but leave a buffer for busy seasons.
