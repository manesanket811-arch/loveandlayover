Perfect 10-Day Japan Itinerary

Published June 1, 2026 | 12 min read

Complete day-by-day plan covering Tokyo, Kyoto, Mt. Fuji, and Osaka with costs, transport, and insider tips

🗻 Overview: 10 Days in Japan

This itinerary covers the classic Japan route: Tokyo's modern energy, Kyoto's ancient temples, Mt. Fuji's stunning views, and Osaka's food scene. Best for first-timers who want to experience Japan's contrasts.

Best seasons: Spring (March-May) for cherry blossoms, Autumn (Sept-Nov) for perfect weather. Avoid July-August (hot and humid).

Budget (JPY/day): Budget travelers: ¥5,000-7,000. Mid-range: ¥8,000-12,000. Comfortable: ¥15,000+

Days 1-3: Tokyo

Day 1: Arrival & Shinjuku

Transport: Narita/Haneda airport → Shinjuku (Narita Express or Airport Limousine, ¥3,000-8,000). Pick up Suica/Pasmo card at airport.

Do: Check into hotel, explore Shinjuku's bright lights, grab ramen or tonkatsu for dinner.

Cost: ¥1,500-2,000 (food). Hotel: ¥4,000-8,000 (budget) or ¥12,000+ (mid-range)

Day 2: East Tokyo

Route: Senso-ji Temple → Asakusa (explore shops) → Tsukiji Outer Market for sushi lunch → teamLab Borderless digital art experience (¥3,200)

Eat: Street food at Asakusa, conveyor belt sushi, matcha ice cream

Cost: ¥2,500-4,000 (including teamLab)

Day 3: West Tokyo

Route: Meiji Shrine → Yoyogi Park → Harajuku's quirky fashion street → Shibuya Crossing → Roppongi Hills (sunset views)

Eat: Crepes in Harajuku, ramen at Shibuya, dinner in Roppongi

Cost: ¥1,500-2,500

Days 4-5: Mt. Fuji & Hakone

Day 4: Day trip to Mt. Fuji/Hakone

Transport: Tokyo → Hakone via Shinjuku (2 hours, ¥2,500-4,000 for mountain ropeway). Or climb Mt. Fuji (5-7 hours from Tokyo).

Do: Hakone Open-Air Museum, Mount Hakone views, hot spring resort (onsen) experience

Cost: ¥2,000-3,500 (transportation + activities) + ¥6,000-15,000 (resort overnight with meals)

Day 5: Hakone to Kyoto

Transport: Hakone → Kyoto via bullet train (Shinkansen, 2.5 hours, ¥13,500). Buy JR Pass before this leg if doing multiple Shinkansen trips.

Do: Arrive in Kyoto, explore Higashiyama district (geisha district, temple-lined streets), dinner at ryokan

Cost: ¥500-1,500 (food in Kyoto)

Days 6-8: Kyoto

Day 6: Southern Kyoto

Route: Fushimi Inari Shrine (famous red gates, ¥0), Sake brewery tours (¥500-1,500), Sanjusangendo Temple

Eat: Kaiseki dinner (upscale, ¥10,000-20,000) or casual ramen (¥800)

Cost: ¥1,000-2,000

Day 7: Temple Day

Route: Arashiyama Bamboo Grove → Tenryu-ji Temple → Philosopher's Path → Silver Pavilion (Ginkaku-ji, ¥500)

Eat: Tofu kaiseki (traditional, ¥2,000-5,000), street dumplings

Cost: ¥1,500-2,500

Day 8: Central Kyoto & Gion

Route: Golden Pavilion (Kinkaku-ji, ¥400) → Ryoan-ji rock garden → Gion district walking tour → possible geisha spotting at dusk

Eat: Kaiseki dinner in Gion (¥5,000-15,000)

Cost: ¥1,500-2,500

Days 9-10: Osaka & Return

Day 9: Kyoto to Osaka

Transport: Shinkansen Kyoto → Osaka (15 min, ¥2,800)

Do: Osaka Castle (¥600), explore Dotonbori district (street food paradise), karaoke night

Eat: Okonomiyaki (savory pancake, ¥800-1,500), takoyaki (octopus balls, ¥500), ramen

Cost: ¥2,000-3,500

Day 10: Osaka & Departure

Do: Last-minute shopping at Umeda Sky Building, explore side streets

Transport: Osaka → Airport (varies, ¥2,500-5,000)

Cost: ¥1,500-2,000

Total Budget (10 Days)

  • Budget: ¥70,000-100,000 (₹35,000-50,000 + flights)
  • Mid-range: ¥120,000-180,000 (₹60,000-90,000 + flights)
  • Notes: Suica/Pasmo card saves money on transport. Buy at airport (¥2,000 with ¥1,500 credit). JR Pass (7-day) costs ¥29,650 - only worth it if doing multiple Shinkansen trips.
💡 Pro Tips: - Book accommodations in advance during peak seasons - Convenience stores (7-Eleven, Lawson) have incredible cheap food - Most places don't take credit cards - carry cash - Pocket WiFi rental at airport (¥1,000-2,000/day) or SIM card - English is limited outside Tokyo/Kyoto - learn basic phrases or use Google Translate
