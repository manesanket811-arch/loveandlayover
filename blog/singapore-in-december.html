Singapore in December

Christmas lights, New Year fireworks and monsoon showers: what December in Singapore is really like, what it costs from India and how to plan around the rain.

6 min readUpdated October 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Mustang Joe, CC0

December is one of the most popular months for Indians to visit Singapore: school holidays, Christmas leave and New Year all line up. The city is at its most festive, with Orchard Road lit up and fireworks over Marina Bay. It's also the heart of the rainy season and the most expensive time to fly. Here's how to enjoy it anyway.

Weather25–31°C, wetWarm and humid, with frequent afternoon and evening showers and some fully rainy days

What's the weather like in December?

December falls in Singapore's northeast monsoon, which runs roughly from December to early March. Temperatures barely change (about 25°C at night and 31°C in the day), but rain is more frequent than in most months. Typically you get heavy showers that last an hour or two, often in the afternoon or evening, and then it clears. A few days each month can be grey and drizzly from morning to night.

The good news: Singapore is built for rain. Covered walkways link most MRT stations to malls, and many of the best attractions are indoors and air-conditioned.

Rain rulePlan outdoor sights (Gardens by the Bay outdoor gardens, Sentosa beaches, walking tours) for the morning, and keep an indoor backup for every afternoon.

Christmas and New Year in Singapore

Christmas on Orchard Road Free · mid-November to early January

Orchard Road's Christmas light-up is the city's biggest festive tradition: the whole shopping street is decorated, malls compete with themed displays and there are markets and events on weekends. Go after 7 PM and walk from Orchard MRT towards Dhoby Ghaut.

FreeBest after dark

Festive Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay Free to S$$

Marina Bay and Gardens by the Bay usually run festive events and light shows in December, and the Flower Dome often has a Christmas-themed display. Exact events change every year, so check what's on before you go. The nightly Garden Rhapsody and Spectra light shows are free all year.

Check this year's eventsFree light shows

New Year's Eve countdown Free · very crowded

The midnight fireworks over Marina Bay are the big one. Viewing spots around the bay (the Merlion, the Esplanade waterfront, The Float area and the Helix Bridge) fill up hours before midnight, and nearby MRT stations get extremely busy or may be closed briefly for crowd control. Arrive by 8–9 PM with water and snacks, or book a restaurant or hotel room with a bay view well in advance.

Arrive earlyExpect MRT crowds

Year-end sales Shopping

Malls on Orchard Road, VivoCity, Bugis and Marina Bay Sands run Christmas and year-end promotions. For budget shopping, Bugis Street and Mustafa Centre in Little India are year-round favourites.

Crowds: who else is travelling?

Singapore's own school holidays run from late November to the end of December, and many Indian, Southeast Asian and Australian families visit at the same time. Expect queues at Universal Studios, Gardens by the Bay domes and the Night Safari, especially on weekends and between Christmas and New Year. Buy tickets online for a fixed date and arrive at opening time.

What to pack

  • A compact umbrella (more useful than a raincoat in the heat)
  • Quick-dry clothes and sandals or shoes that cope with puddles
  • A light layer for very cold malls, cinemas and the MRT
  • Sunscreen: the sun is strong between showers
  • A power bank and offline maps for New Year's Eve, when mobile networks get congested

Is December a good time to visit Singapore?

Yes, if you want the festive atmosphere and your dates are fixed by school or work holidays. You'll get some rain, but rarely enough to ruin a day. If you're flexible, February to April is drier and usually cheaper. Early December (before about the 15th) is the best compromise: the Christmas lights are already up, and flights and hotels haven't hit their peak.

FAQs

Is December a good time to visit Singapore?

Yes for festive events like the Orchard Road Christmas lights and New Year fireworks. It is the rainy season and peak travel time, so expect showers, crowds and higher prices. Early December is the best balance.

Does it rain all day in Singapore in December?

Usually not. Most rain comes as heavy showers lasting an hour or two, often in the afternoon or evening. A few days a month can be drizzly all day, so keep indoor backups.

How much does a Singapore trip cost in December from India?

Return flights are about ₹22,000–32,000 in early December and ₹30,000–45,000 or more around Christmas and New Year. A mid-range 5-day trip is roughly ₹1–1.3 lakh per person including flights.

Where can I watch the New Year fireworks in Singapore?

Around Marina Bay: the Merlion, the Esplanade waterfront, The Float area and the Helix Bridge. Arrive by 8–9 PM because spots fill up and MRT stations get very crowded.

What is the temperature in Singapore in December?

About 25°C at night and 31°C during the day, with high humidity, the same warm weather as the rest of the year.
