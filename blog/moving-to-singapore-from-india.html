Moving to Singapore from India: The Complete Guide

Everything in one place, step by step: job, work pass, salary, home, setup, money and family, from an Indian couple who made the move.

8 min readUpdated November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Mustang Joe, CC0

We moved from India to Singapore for Sanket's job, and since then the most common questions we get are about making the same move. This page puts everything in order: the job, the pass, the money, the flat and the first month. Each step links to a detailed guide.

Our move at a glance Sanket & Nikita

Step 1: Get a job offer

For most Indians, the move starts with a job offer, because Singapore has no job-seeker visa and your employer applies for your pass. Tech, finance, healthcare and engineering hire the most foreigners. Start with LinkedIn and MyCareersFuture, and make your CV Singapore-ready.

→ How to get a job in Singapore from India · Work culture and interview tips · Watch: How He Got a Job in Singapore from India and From Call Center to Singapore Tech Job

Step 2: Understand the work pass rules

Most professionals get an Employment Pass. The minimum salary is S$5,600 a month now and S$6,000 from 1 January 2027 (higher with age and in financial services), and applications must also pass the COMPASS points test.

→ EP salary rules for 2026 and 2027 · Later: Singapore PR for Indians · Watch: Singapore Work Visa Changes 2027

Step 3: Check the salary is enough

Judge any offer on what's left after rent. As a rough guide: S$5,000+ for a single person, S$8,000+ for a couple on one income, S$12,000+ for a family with school fees.

→ Salary & EP calculator · What's a good salary in Singapore? · Watch: Our real monthly expenses

Step 4: Plan your first month

Book a hotel or serviced apartment for the first few weeks, then sort your SIM card, medical check-up, pass card, Singpass, bank account and remittance in the right order.

→ First 30 days in Singapore checklist · Sending money to India · NRI tax and bank accounts

Step 5: Find a home

Choose between an HDB flat (more space, lower rent) and a condo (facilities and security). Budget for a deposit, the first month's rent and stamp duty upfront.

→ Renting a flat in Singapore: HDB vs condo · Watch: Our S$3,000 condo tour

Step 6: Bring your family

With a fixed salary of at least S$6,000 a month, your employer can apply for Dependant's Passes for your spouse and children. Spouses need their own work pass to work, which is hard to get because of sponsorship, so plan on one income at first.

→ Dependant's Pass guide · Schools for Indian kids · Bringing your parents

Step 7: Settle into life here

Singapore has one of the biggest Indian communities outside India, with temples, festivals, Indian groceries and food everywhere. It's expensive and the rules are strict, but it's safe, efficient and only a few hours from home.

→ Living in Singapore as an Indian · Best Indian restaurants · Indian groceries · Healthcare · Rules and fines · Driving · Watch our Life in Singapore series

Your moving checklist

  1. Job offer that meets the Employment Pass salary for your age
  2. In-Principle Approval (IPA) letter
  3. Documents: passport, degrees, marriage and birth certificates
  4. Flights and 2–4 weeks of temporary accommodation
  5. SG Arrival Card submitted before you fly
  6. SIM card, medical check-up and pass card registration
  7. Singpass, local bank account and PayNow
  8. Wise or another remittance app, and NRI updates to your Indian bank accounts
  9. Long-term flat, internet and utilities
  10. Dependant's Passes for your family

FAQs

How can I move to Singapore from India?

Most Indians move with a job offer. Your employer applies for an Employment Pass or S Pass, you receive an In-Principle Approval letter, then you fly in, complete your medical and pass card registration, and set up your bank, Singpass and home.

How much salary do I need to move to Singapore?

For an Employment Pass you need at least S$5,600 a month (S$6,000 from January 2027), higher with age. To live comfortably, plan on S$5,000+ for a single person, S$8,000+ for a couple and S$12,000+ for a family with school fees.

How long does it take to move to Singapore from India?

Finding a job can take a few months; it took Sanket about 3 to 4 months. After an offer, the Employment Pass is usually decided within a few weeks, and most people move within 1 to 3 months of the offer depending on their notice period.

Is moving to Singapore from India worth it?

For many Indians, yes: higher salaries, low taxes, safety, efficiency and a big Indian community, a short flight from home. The trade-offs are high rent, strict rules and a competitive job market.
