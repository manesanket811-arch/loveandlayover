Singapore Layover Guide (6h-48h)

Make the most of your stopover. Guides for 6-hour, 12-hour, 24-hour, and 48-hour layovers.

📖 5 min read✍️ Love and Layovers📅 May 2026

Why Singapore for a Layover?

Singapore is perfect for layovers! Changi Airport is world-class with excellent connections. The city is compact, safe, and has incredible food and attractions. Even a short layover gives you a taste of Singapore.

🕐 Pro Tip: Singapore is only 1-hour from the airport to city center. Even 6 hours gives you time for a quick adventure.

6-Hour Layover Itinerary

Timeline

  • Deplane & collect luggage: 30 min
  • Airport → City: 45 min (MRT $2.50)
  • Explore: 3-4 hours
  • City → Airport: 60 min
  • Buffer time: 30 min

The Route

  • Take MRT from Changi Airport to Chinatown station (30 min)
  • Quick walk through Chinatown streets (30 min)
  • Eat at Maxwell Food Centre (30 min)
  • Quick visit to nearby temple (15 min)
  • Return to airport

Realistic? Tight but doable if you move fast. Focus on food and streets, skip paid attractions.

12-Hour Layover Itinerary

Timeline

  • Morning: Airport to Little India
  • Breakfast: Roti prata at local shop ($3)
  • Explore: Little India markets (1-2 hours)
  • Lunch: Biryani at restaurant ($8)
  • Afternoon: Walk to Chinatown or visit nearby temple
  • Afternoon snack: Hawker center visit ($5)
  • Return to airport with time to spare

Budget: $20-30 SGD including transport and food

Alternative Routes

  • Beach option: East Coast Beach via MRT (20 min). Beach swim or walk (2 hours). Hawker food. Return.
  • Gardens option: Marina Bay → Gardens by the Bay (free outdoor exploration) → Hawker food → Airport

24-Hour Layover Itinerary

Best Use of Time

  • Day: Explore major attractions (Gardens by the Bay, Chinatown, Little India)
  • Evening: Dinner at hawker center, Supertree light show (free, 8:45 PM)
  • Night: Sleep in airport or budget hotel ($30-40) if connecting flight allows

Budget Option ($60-80)

  • Airport → City: MRT ($2.50)
  • Food (breakfast, lunch, dinner): $15
  • Attractions: $10 (optional, many free ones)
  • Budget hotel: $30-40
  • City → Airport: $2.50

No-Sleep Option (24h awake)

  • Morning: Gardens by the Bay, Botanic Gardens (free)
  • Lunch: Hawker food
  • Afternoon: Chinatown, Little India, shopping
  • Evening: Dinner, light show, airport
  • Total cost: $25-30

48-Hour Layover Itinerary

With 2 days, you can see the main attractions and relax!

Day 1

  • Morning: Arrive, check into budget hotel ($30-40)
  • Afternoon: Gardens by the Bay, Botanic Gardens (free)
  • Evening: Dinner at hawker, Supertree light show (free)

Day 2

  • Morning: Chinatown, temples, street food
  • Afternoon: Little India or Kampong Glam exploration
  • Evening: Beach (East Coast), dinner, airport

Budget

  • Hotel: $40-50 (2 nights)
  • Food: $30-40 (meals + snacks)
  • Transport: $10 (MRT)
  • Total: $80-100 for 2 days

Changi Airport Tips

✈️ Airport Layers: Changi is huge. T1, T2, T3 are main terminals. Free WiFi everywhere. Luggage storage: $7-12/day at luggage storage.
  • Free at Changi: Merlion sculpture, gardens, art installations, sleeping areas
  • Transit Hotel: Book rooms for a few hours ($25-35)
  • Eating at Airport: Overpriced. Hit the city instead.
  • Getting to City: MRT (cheapest), Grab taxi ($25-30), hotel shuttles
  • Returning to Airport: MRT from multiple stations, Grab, or taxi

What to Bring & Know

  • Luggage: Use airport luggage storage if exploring
  • Visa: For Indian nationals: e-visa required (SGD 30-50, 3-7 days processing). Visa-Free Transit (96 hours): Allowed with confirmed onward flight same day—great for layovers. Check Singapore ICA for your nationality.
  • Cash: Get SGD at airport ATM, or use cards (widely accepted)
  • SIM card: Optional for short layovers (phones work on roaming)
  • Weather: Hot and humid. Bring light clothes and umbrella
  • Language: English widely spoken, no language barrier

Pro Layover Moves

  • Pre-download maps: Google Maps offline in Changi
  • Plan transport: Know MRT stations near attractions
  • Book accommodation ahead: Hotels near MRT for quick check-in/out
  • Time buffer: Always aim to be back at airport 2 hours early
  • Accept limitations: 6-hour layover = quick food tour. 24-hour = real exploration
