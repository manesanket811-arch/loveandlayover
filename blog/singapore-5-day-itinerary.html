The Complete 5-Day Singapore Itinerary

📖 8 min read✍️ Love and Layovers📅 May 2026

Introduction

Singapore is a stunning blend of modern architecture, vibrant culture, and incredible food. In just 5 days, you can experience the best this island city-state has to offer. This itinerary covers Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, cultural neighborhoods, Sentosa Island, and local experiences that will give you authentic Singapore.

💡 Tip: Buy a Tourist Day Pass ($15 SGD) for unlimited MRT and bus travel. This will save you money and make navigation easy.

Day 1: Marina Bay & Gardens by the Bay

Morning: Check-in & Arrival

  • Arrive at Changi Airport early morning
  • Take MRT to your hotel (45 min, $2.50 SGD). Tourist Day Pass recommended!
  • Check into hotel and freshen up

Afternoon: Marina Bay Sands & Observation Deck

  • Visit Marina Bay Sands observation deck (57 floors, $29 SGD)
  • Amazing 360-degree city views
  • Best time: late afternoon for sunset views
  • Grab lunch at nearby hawker center

Evening: Gardens by the Bay

  • Explore Gardens by the Bay (free outdoor gardens)
  • Witness the Supertree Light Show (7:45 PM & 8:45 PM, free)
  • Dinner at Gardens by the Bay area or nearby restaurant

Day 1 Cost (approx): $60-80 SGD (lunch, dinner, entry fees)

Day 2: Gardens by the Bay & Singapore Flyer

Morning: Cloud Forest & Flower Dome

  • Visit Cloud Forest ($31 SGD) - a 40-meter indoor waterfall with exotic plants
  • Explore Flower Dome ($31 SGD) - Mediterranean and tropical plants
  • Both indoor attractions are great if it rains

Afternoon: Singapore Flyer & Dining

  • Take Singapore Flyer observation ride (30 min, $35 SGD)
  • Best at sunset for gorgeous city views
  • Lunch at nearby restaurants or hawker center

Evening: Chinatown Exploration

  • Walk through Chinatown streets
  • Visit Buddha Tooth Relic Temple (free)
  • Explore street food and traditional shops
  • Dinner at a restaurant or hawker center in Chinatown

Day 2 Cost (approx): $110-140 SGD

Day 3: Chinatown & Singapore Flyer Light Show

Morning: Deeper into Chinatown

  • Visit wet markets and local shops
  • Explore hidden temples and shrines
  • Breakfast or brunch at a local café

Afternoon: Little India or Arab Street

  • Choose one: Little India (spice markets, temples) or Kampong Glam (Arab Street, heritage)
  • Explore shops and street food
  • Lunch at traditional restaurant ($8-12 SGD)

Evening: Singapore Flyer Sunset Experience

  • Return to Singapore Flyer for sunset views
  • The nightly light show is magical during twilight
  • Dinner in downtown area

Day 3 Cost (approx): $40-60 SGD

Day 4: Sentosa Island Adventure

Morning: Getting to Sentosa

  • Take cable car from Faber Peak or monorail from Harbourfront
  • Arrive at Sentosa Island by 10 AM

Midday: Beach Time or Activities

  • Option 1: Relax at Siloso Beach or Palawan Beach
  • Option 2: Universal Studios Singapore ($75 SGD, full day)
  • Option 3: S.E.A Aquarium ($39 SGD)
  • Lunch at beach restaurant or food court

Afternoon: More Sentosa Exploration

  • Skyline Luge Sentosa - thrilling luge rides ($30-45 SGD) or Wings of Time show ($20-35 SGD, free with some packages)
  • Explore Sentosa beaches and nature trails
  • Sky Bridge adventure activity (optional, $45-65 SGD)

Evening: Sunset Dinner

  • Dinner at beachfront restaurant
  • Return to city by cable car or monorail

Day 4 Cost (approx): $120-200 SGD (depending on activities chosen)

Day 5: Shopping, Food & Last-Minute Exploration

Morning: Orchard Road Shopping

  • Explore Orchard Road - Singapore's main shopping belt
  • Visit malls like Ion Orchard, Orchard Central, Takashimaya
  • Breakfast at a café on Orchard Road

Afternoon: Hawker Center Food Tour

  • Visit a major hawker center like Maxwell Food Centre or Tiong Bahru
  • Try multiple dishes: laksa, chicken rice, char kway teow, desserts
  • Cost per dish: $3-5 SGD

Late Afternoon: Final Exploration

  • Visit any missed attractions or favorite spots
  • Browse local markets for souvenirs
  • Relax at a café

Evening: Departure

  • Head to Changi Airport (45 min by MRT, $2.50 SGD)
  • Check in for your flight

Day 5 Cost (approx): $40-80 SGD

Practical Tips

💡 Best Time to Visit: February-April or June-August. Avoid November-January (monsoon).
💡 Money Saving: Eat at hawker centers ($3-5), use Tourist Day Pass, skip expensive theme parks if budget is tight, visit free attractions like temples and beaches.
💡 Getting Around: The MRT is clean, efficient, and cheap. Get a Tourist Day Pass ($15) for unlimited travel. Download the MRT map app.
💡 Safety: Singapore is very safe. Tap water is drinkable. Locals are friendly. English is widely spoken.

Conclusion

This 5-day itinerary covers Singapore's iconic attractions, cultural neighborhoods, beaches, and incredible food scene. You'll experience modern Singapore, traditional heritage, and authentic local life. Perfect for first-time visitors!
