Flights to Singapore from India

How long the flight is from your city, what a return ticket typically costs, which airlines fly direct and how to book it cheaper.

5 min readUpdated October 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: kallerna, CC BY-SA 4.0

Singapore is one of the easiest international trips from India: direct flights from every major metro, flight times of about 4 to 5½ hours and a time difference of just 2½ hours. Here's what to expect from your city, and how to pay less.

Fares from Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru and Kochi are often at the lower end; Delhi and Mumbai sit higher in peak weeks. These are approximate ranges we see, not quotes.

Full-service or low-cost?

Low-cost fares look cheaper, but checked bags, seat selection and meals cost extra. A family with suitcases can end up paying close to a full-service fare. Always compare the final price with bags, not the headline fare.

  • Full-service (Singapore Airlines, Air India): baggage and meals included, more flexible tickets. Worth it for families and long overnight flights.
  • Low-cost (Scoot, IndiGo): great for couples and solo travellers with cabin bags only.

Overnight flights: how to plan day one

Many flights from Delhi and Mumbai leave around midnight and land in Singapore early in the morning, while hotel check-in is usually around 3 PM. Make the first day easy:

  • Freshen up and explore Jewel Changi (the Rain Vortex waterfall is free to see) before leaving the airport.
  • Leave your bags at the hotel and do something gentle: Gardens by the Bay's free outdoor gardens or a hawker breakfast.
  • Paying for early check-in is worth it with kids or elderly parents.
Before you flyIndian passport holders need a Singapore visa (see our visa guide), and every visitor must fill in the free SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before arrival.

How to get cheaper flights to Singapore

  1. Book 6–8 weeks ahead, earlier for Diwali, December and summer holidays.
  2. Fly mid-week. Tuesday and Wednesday departures are often cheaper than Friday and Sunday.
  3. Compare nearby airports. In the south, Chennai, Bengaluru and Kochi fares can differ a lot.
  4. Look at one-stop routes via Kuala Lumpur or Bangkok in peak season; they're sometimes much cheaper, but check visa and transit rules.
  5. Set fare alerts on a flight search site and book when you see a dip.
  6. Use your credit card or airline points; Singapore is a popular redemption route.

From Changi Airport to the city

  • MRT: about S$2.50 and roughly 45 minutes to the centre. Tap in with a contactless card.
  • Grab or taxi: about S$25–35 to most central hotels, 20–30 minutes.
  • Private transfer: handy for families with lots of luggage; book in advance.

FAQs

How long is the flight from India to Singapore?

About 4 hours from Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Hyderabad and Kochi, and about 5½ to 6 hours from Mumbai and Delhi for direct flights.

What is the cheapest month to fly to Singapore from India?

Outside Indian school holidays and festival weeks, typically February to April and mid-July to September, avoiding long weekends. Booking 6 to 8 weeks ahead and flying mid-week helps most.

How much is a return flight to Singapore from India?

Roughly ₹18,000–28,000 booked ahead off-season, and ₹30,000–45,000 or more in peak weeks like Diwali, Christmas and summer holidays.

Which Indian cities have direct flights to Singapore?

All major metros, including Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata and Kochi, plus several other cities. Routes change, so check a flight search site for your city.

What is the time difference between India and Singapore?

Singapore is 2 hours 30 minutes ahead of India.
