Singapore vs Thailand vs Dubai

The three most popular first international trips from India, compared honestly on visas, flights, daily costs, food, weather and who each one suits.

6 min readUpdated October 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Marcin Konsek, CC BY-SA 4.0

"Singapore, Thailand or Dubai?" is the question we get most from friends planning their first trip abroad. All three are short flights from India, easy for first-timers and great for vegetarians. But they feel very different, and the right pick depends on your budget, who you're travelling with and what kind of holiday you want.

Singapore: the easiest first trip

Singapore is the trip we recommend most to parents and first-time travellers. It's spotless, very safe at any hour, and the MRT goes everywhere, so you never feel lost. Big-ticket sights like Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay, Sentosa and Universal Studios are close together, and the free light shows are some of the best experiences in the city.

  • Pros: safety, cleanliness, superb transport, great vegetarian Indian food, perfect for kids and seniors
  • Cons: the priciest hotels and drinks of the three, no big beaches, a visa is needed

Plan it with our Singapore guide, first-timer tips and trip cost from India.

Thailand: the best value

Thailand gives you the most holiday for your money: Bangkok's temples and markets, plus islands like Phuket, Krabi and Phi Phi. Food and massages are cheap, nightlife is lively and hotels are excellent value. It does take a bit more planning (ferries, island hops, avoiding tourist scams), and it's less polished than Singapore.

  • Pros: visa-free entry for Indians, beautiful beaches, low daily costs, great for friends and couples
  • Cons: busier and less orderly, beach towns get rowdy, the monsoon (roughly June–October) affects some islands

Start with our Thailand guide and Thailand food guide.

Before you fly to ThailandThailand has introduced an online Digital Arrival Card that visitors fill in before arrival. Check the current rules on the official Thai government website when you book.

Dubai: big, glamorous and close

Dubai is about scale: the Burj Khalifa, giant malls, a desert safari with dinner under the stars, water parks and Abu Dhabi as a day trip. It's the shortest flight from most of India and feels familiar, with Indian food and Hindi spoken almost everywhere. It's also the most expensive day-to-day of the three, and it's best visited between November and March.

  • Pros: short flights, luxury and shopping, desert experiences, very Indian-friendly
  • Cons: highest daily costs, very hot from May to September, spread out so you'll rely on taxis

See our Dubai guide for the visa options, areas and a day-by-day plan.

Which one should you pick?

  • Travelling with parents or young kids? Singapore.
  • Tight budget or a friends' trip? Thailand.
  • Honeymoon? Thailand's islands for beaches, Singapore for city romance (see our Singapore honeymoon itinerary), Dubai for luxury.
  • Only 3–4 days and a winter break? Dubai.
  • Want to see two countries in one trip? Singapore plus Malaysia by bus or train, see our 7-day Singapore and Malaysia itinerary.

FAQs

Which is cheaper for Indians: Singapore, Thailand or Dubai?

Thailand is the cheapest day to day, roughly ₹3,800–6,300 per person per day. Singapore is about ₹6,200–9,800 and Dubai about ₹9,200–16,000, excluding flights.

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore, Thailand and Dubai?

Singapore needs a visa through an authorised agent. Thailand currently allows visa-free entry for Indians, but check the allowed length of stay. Dubai usually needs an e-visa. Rules change, so confirm on official websites.

Which is best for a first international trip from India?

Singapore is the easiest and safest, especially with family. Thailand is best value with great beaches. Dubai suits luxury, shopping and short winter breaks.

Which is best for vegetarians?

All three are good. Singapore and Dubai have lots of Indian vegetarian restaurants. In Thailand you will find vegetarian food easily in cities, with a little more planning on the islands.

Which is the shortest flight from India?

Dubai, about 3 to 3½ hours from Mumbai or Delhi. Bangkok is about 4 hours and Singapore about 4 to 5½ hours depending on your city.
