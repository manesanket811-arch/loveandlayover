Malaysia Visa for Indians from 1 January 2027: What You Need to Know

Visa-free entry for Indian passport holders ends on 31 December 2026. Here's who's affected, how to apply, and how to plan trips around the change.

News · Visas & passes8 min readUpdated 19 November 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: CEphoto, Uwe Aranas, CC BY-SA 3.0

Big news for Indian travellers: Malaysia is ending visa-free entry for Indian passport holders. From 1 January 2027, you'll need a visa approved before you arrive. If you're planning Kuala Lumpur, Langkawi, Penang, a Singapore–Malaysia combo or even a day trip to Johor Bahru, here's exactly what's changing and what to do.

Updated 19 November 2026Based on the Immigration Department of Malaysia's notice of 17 November 2026 and news reports. Malaysia says it can change entry conditions at any time, so always check the official MyVISA portal before you travel. We'll update this page as more details come out.

The short answer

  • Until 31 December 2026: Indians can still enter Malaysia visa-free for up to 30 days for tourism and social visits.
  • From 1 January 2027: you need a valid visa approved before arrival. There's no visa on arrival.
  • Apply online on the official MyVISA portal (malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my). Applications open on 1 December 2026.
  • Fees are non-refundable, even if your plans change.
  • You still need the MDAC (Malaysia Digital Arrival Card) within 3 days before arrival.

What exactly was announced

On 17 November 2026, the Immigration Department of Malaysia (JIM) issued a notice saying the visa exemption facility for Indian nationals will no longer be available from 1 January 2027, and that Indian nationals entering Malaysia from that date must obtain a valid visa before arrival. Applications can be made through the MyVISA portal from 1 December 2026.

Visa-free entry for Indians began in December 2023 and was extended in December 2024 until the end of 2026, which Malaysia linked to its ASEAN chairmanship and Visit Malaysia Year 2026. The new notice doesn't give a reason for ending it.

Does this affect my trip?

Trip entirely in November or December 2026 No visa needed

Enter and leave before 1 January 2027 and you can still travel visa-free for up to 30 days. Remember the MDAC.

Trip over New Year (e.g. 28 December to 4 January) Plan carefully

The notice covers entries from 1 January 2027. If you enter visa-free in December and stay in Malaysia without leaving, check with Malaysian immigration or your airline how your stay will be treated. If you'll leave and come back (for example to Singapore or Thailand), any re-entry on or after 1 January needs a visa. When in doubt, apply for the visa: it's cheaper than being refused boarding.

Trip in 2027 Visa needed

Apply on MyVISA after 1 December 2026, ideally at least 2–3 weeks before you fly. Don't book non-refundable extras until your visa is approved.

Living in Singapore and visiting Johor Bahru Visa needed from 2027

Many Indians in Singapore pop over to JB for shopping, food or Legoland. From 1 January 2027, each entry into Malaysia, even a day trip, needs a valid visa. No exemption for Singapore residents has been announced. If you visit often, check whether a multiple-entry visa is offered when applications open.

Singapore + Malaysia combo trip Two visas

Indians already need a visa for Singapore. From 2027, you'll need one for Malaysia too, and if you go Singapore → Malaysia → Singapore, check that your Singapore visa allows two entries. See our 7-day Singapore and Malaysia itinerary.

Just changing planes in Kuala Lumpur Check your ticket

If you stay airside and don't pass immigration on a single through-ticket, rules differ from entering the country. If you have separate tickets (common with budget airlines) and need to collect bags or re-check in, you'll have to enter Malaysia and will need a visa. Confirm with your airline before you book.

How to apply for a Malaysia visa (step by step)

  1. Go to the official MyVISA portal: malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my. Applications open from 1 December 2026.
  2. Create an account and choose the visa type for your purpose (tourism, social visit or business).
  3. Upload your documents (see the checklist below).
  4. Pay the fee online. It's non-refundable.
  5. Wait for approval by email, and download your visa.
  6. Before you fly: print the visa, and fill in the MDAC within 3 days before arrival.
Use only the official portalOnce a visa becomes mandatory, look-alike "Malaysia visa" websites charge extra for the same form. The official portal ends in .gov.my. If you use an agent, choose a trusted one and confirm they apply through MyVISA.

Document checklist

  • Passport valid for at least 6 months from your arrival date, with blank pages
  • A recent passport-size photo (digital, as per the portal's specifications)
  • Confirmed return or onward flight ticket
  • Hotel booking or address in Malaysia (or invitation letter if visiting family)
  • Your itinerary and, if asked, proof of funds such as recent bank statements

The exact requirements, fees, processing time and visa validity will be shown on MyVISA when applications open. We'll add them here as soon as they're confirmed.

Still required: the MDAC arrival card

Whether you're travelling visa-free in December or on a visa in 2027, all foreign visitors must fill in the free Malaysia Digital Arrival Card (MDAC) online within 3 days before arrival. It's separate from the visa. If you're going back and forth, for example from Singapore, you need a new MDAC for each entry.

Our tips

  • Travelling in December? Enjoy the last weeks of visa-free travel, but carry your return ticket and hotel booking, as immigration may ask.
  • Book flights you can change until your visa is approved.
  • Apply early for travel in January and the school holidays, when demand will be high.
  • Keep everything consistent: the dates and hotel on your visa application should match your bookings.
  • Combining countries? Thailand and several other Asian countries are still visa-free or easy for Indians; see our visa-free countries list and the visa checker.

Still worth visiting Malaysia?

Absolutely. Kuala Lumpur's food, Batu Caves, Genting, Penang's street art and Langkawi's beaches are as good as ever, and Malaysia remains one of the most affordable trips from India. The visa just adds a step to your planning. Our Malaysia travel guide has day-by-day plans and costs, and if you're starting from Singapore, see Singapore to Malaysia by bus or train.

FAQs

Do Indians need a visa for Malaysia in 2027?

Yes. From 1 January 2027, Indian passport holders need a valid visa approved before arrival. Visa-free entry for up to 30 days continues until 31 December 2026.

When can I apply for a Malaysia visa for 2027 travel?

Applications open on the official MyVISA portal (malaysiavisa.imi.gov.my) from 1 December 2026. Fees are non-refundable.

Is there visa on arrival in Malaysia for Indians?

No. Malaysia's notice says Indian nationals must obtain a valid visa before arrival from 1 January 2027.

Do Indians living in Singapore need a visa for a Johor Bahru day trip?

From 1 January 2027, yes: every entry into Malaysia needs a valid visa, and no exemption for Singapore residents has been announced. You also need a new MDAC for each entry.

Do I still need the MDAC if I have a Malaysia visa?

Yes. The free Malaysia Digital Arrival Card must be submitted online within 3 days before every arrival, in addition to your visa.

Can I travel to Malaysia visa-free in December 2026?

Yes. Indians can still enter visa-free for up to 30 days until 31 December 2026. Any entry on or after 1 January 2027 needs a visa.
