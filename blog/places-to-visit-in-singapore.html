Best Places to Visit in Singapore

20 sights worth your time, grouped by area so you waste less time travelling, with what's free, what's paid and a 3-day plan.

7 min readUpdated October 2026By Sanket & Nikita, living in Singapore
Photo: Mustang Joe, CC0

Singapore is small, spotless and incredibly easy to get around, which means you can see a lot in a few days if you plan by area. Here are the places we recommend to friends and family visiting from India.

How to use this listPlaces are grouped by neighbourhood with the nearest MRT station, so you can do one area at a time. "Free" means no ticket needed.

Marina Bay: the postcard skyline

1. Gardens by the Bay Bayfront MRT · free outdoors, domes paid

The giant Supertree Grove is free to walk through. Return after dark for Garden Rhapsody, the free light-and-music show (usually around 7:45 and 8:45 pm). The cooled Flower Dome and Cloud Forest, with its indoor waterfall, are worth the ticket on a hot afternoon.

2–4 hoursDomes ≈ S$45–55Best: late afternoon into evening

2. Marina Bay Sands and the SkyPark Bayfront MRT

The famous three-tower hotel. Non-guests can go up to the SkyPark Observation Deck for the skyline view (the infinity pool is for hotel guests only). The Shoppes mall below has a canal with sampan rides.

1–2 hoursDeck ≈ S$32–40

3. Merlion Park and the waterfront Raffles Place MRT · free

Singapore's half-lion, half-fish mascot, with Marina Bay Sands across the water. Walk the promenade over the Helix Bridge and catch the free Spectra light-and-water show at the Event Plaza in front of Marina Bay Sands in the evening.

1 hourFreeBest at sunset

4. ArtScience Museum Bayfront MRT · paid

The lotus-shaped museum next to Marina Bay Sands, best known for its immersive digital-art exhibition, which is great with kids.

1–2 hoursTickets vary by exhibition

5. Singapore Flyer Promenade MRT · paid

A giant observation wheel with a 30-minute rotation over the bay. Book a sunset slot.

1 hour≈ S$35–40

Heritage neighbourhoods

6. Little India Little India MRT · free

Garlands, spice shops, gold jewellers and the colourful Sri Veeramakaliamman Temple. Shop late at Mustafa Centre and eat a banana-leaf meal; see our Indian restaurants guide. It's especially beautiful during Deepavali, when the streets are lit up.

2–3 hoursFreeVegetarian food everywhere

7. Chinatown Chinatown MRT · free

Shophouse streets, the Buddha Tooth Relic Temple, the Sri Mariamman Temple (Singapore's oldest Hindu temple) and Chinatown Complex hawker centre. Great for souvenirs.

2–3 hoursFree

8. Kampong Glam and Haji Lane Bugis MRT · free

The golden-domed Sultan Mosque, Arab Street's textile shops and the murals and indie boutiques of Haji Lane, one of Singapore's most photographed streets.

1–2 hoursFreeGreat for photos

Sentosa and Universal Studios

9. Universal Studios Singapore HarbourFront MRT, then Sentosa Express

Southeast Asia's Universal theme park, with rides like Transformers, Revenge of the Mummy and the Battlestar Galactica coasters. It needs a full day.

Full day≈ S$85–90

10. Sentosa beaches and attractions Sentosa Express

Palawan, Siloso and Tanjong beaches, the Skyline Luge, the cable car, Fort Siloso and the evening Wings of Time show. Read our full Universal Studios and Sentosa guide.

Half to full dayBeaches free

Nature and wildlife

11. Singapore Botanic Gardens Botanic Gardens MRT · free

A UNESCO World Heritage Site. Walk the lawns and lakes for free; the National Orchid Garden inside has a small fee and is well worth it.

2 hoursFree (Orchid Garden paid)Best: early morning

12. Singapore Zoo, Night Safari and Bird Paradise Mandai · shuttle or Grab

The open-concept zoo, the world's first nocturnal zoo and a huge bird park are side by side at Mandai. The Night Safari is the most unusual of the three.

Half day each≈ S$45–60 each

13. Southern Ridges and Henderson Waves Harbourfront / Labrador Park MRT · free

A 10 km chain of parks and walkways through the treetops, including the wave-shaped Henderson Waves bridge. Do a section rather than all of it.

2–3 hoursFree

14. MacRitchie Reservoir and the TreeTop Walk Caldecott MRT area · free

Rainforest trails and a suspension bridge above the canopy. Watch out for the monkeys, and don't carry food in plastic bags.

3–4 hoursFreeModerate walk

15. Pulau Ubin Changi Point Ferry

A short bumboat ride to Singapore as it was decades ago: kampong houses, mangroves and cycling trails. Rent a bicycle on the island.

Half dayFerry + bike rental only

City life and shopping

16. Orchard Road Orchard MRT · free

Singapore's shopping street, lined with malls from budget to luxury. Especially beautiful when it's lit up for Christmas.

2–4 hoursFree to browse

17. Clarke Quay and the Singapore River Clarke Quay MRT · free

Riverside restaurants and nightlife. Take a river cruise past Boat Quay and the Merlion for an easy evening.

1–2 hoursRiver cruise paid

18. Fort Canning Park Fort Canning MRT · free

A green hill in the middle of the city, home to the famous tree tunnel staircase photo spot and colonial history.

1 hourFree

19. Jewel Changi Airport Changi Airport MRT · free

Don't leave this for the last hour. The Rain Vortex, the world's tallest indoor waterfall, sits inside a forest valley of shops and restaurants. It's free and lit up at night.

1–2 hoursFreeGo before your flight

20. A hawker centre Everywhere · S$5 a meal

Singapore's hawker culture is UNESCO-recognised. Try Maxwell, Lau Pa Sat, Tekka Centre or Newton for cheap, excellent food. See our Singapore food guide.

1 hourS$5–8 per dish

Best free things to do

  • Garden Rhapsody at Gardens by the Bay and Spectra at Marina Bay, both nightly
  • Supertree Grove, Merlion Park and the Marina Bay waterfront
  • Botanic Gardens, Southern Ridges and MacRitchie
  • Little India, Chinatown and Haji Lane
  • Jewel's Rain Vortex at Changi
  • Sentosa's beaches (if you walk the Sentosa Boardwalk, entry is free)

Tips for Indian travellers

  • Weather: it's hot and humid all year with sudden showers. Carry an umbrella and plan indoor sights for the afternoon.
  • Getting around: the MRT reaches almost everything. Tap in with a contactless card.
  • Vegetarian food: easy in Little India and at most hawker centres; harder on Sentosa, so plan meals.
  • Rules: no eating or drinking on the MRT, no littering and no chewing gum sales. Fines are real.

FAQs

What are the top places to visit in Singapore for first-timers?

Gardens by the Bay, Marina Bay Sands and the waterfront, Merlion Park, Sentosa with Universal Studios, Little India, Chinatown, Kampong Glam, the Botanic Gardens and Jewel Changi Airport.

How many days are enough for Singapore?

Four to five days covers the main sights comfortably, including a full day at Universal Studios. Three days is enough for the highlights if you plan by area.

What can I do for free in Singapore?

The Garden Rhapsody and Spectra light shows, Supertree Grove, Merlion Park, the Botanic Gardens, Little India, Chinatown, Haji Lane, Jewel’s Rain Vortex and Sentosa’s beaches are all free.

Is Singapore good for families with kids?

Very. Universal Studios, the Zoo, Night Safari, Bird Paradise, Sentosa’s beaches and the ArtScience Museum are all family favourites, and the city is safe and easy to get around.

What is the best time to visit Singapore?

Singapore is a year-round destination. February to April is usually a little drier, while the November to January monsoon brings more rain but also festive lights on Orchard Road.
